==================== TITLE ====================
Plasma Globe

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A full-screen canvas Tesla globe on a near-black purple-lit table. A glass sphere with rim light, crescent and softbox reflections sits in the collar of a black lathe-turned metal pedestal with a knurled intensity knob and engraved label. From a glowing electrode ball at the centre, 6 to 14 jagged filaments reach for the glass, each drawn as a wide violet halo, magenta body and thin white core with additive blending, with side branches and a glow where each meets the glass. Touching or pressing the glass pulls the nearest filaments into a braided cluster with a spray of arcs and a glow under the finger; a phantom finger occasionally does the same when the globe is left alone. Ambient light pulses on the table and base, static sparks jump around the rim, and the palette can be switched between Violet, Blue, Ember and Emerald.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Recursive midpoint displacement bolts (64 segments, 6 levels) with per-level temporal coherence so coarse shape persists and fine detail flickers
- Occasional side branches with their own midpoint displacement, extra and longer branches near the touch point
- Layered additive strokes (wide violet, medium magenta, white core) on an offscreen bolt canvas redrawn at 20 to 28 Hz and blitted every frame
- Filaments wander in 3D angles projected to 2D so some foreshorten; touch selects nearest filaments and blends their endpoints to the finger
- Offscreen static layers for background, brushed-metal base (elliptical lathe rings, specular streaks) and glass highlights
- Dynamic ambient halo, table glow pool, mirrored electrode column and light spilling on the collar, all additive
- Static sparks pooled and spawned around the rim, collar and touch point
- Eased colour-scheme transitions in RGB, exposed to CSS variables
- Draggable knob (pointer, wheel, keyboard) with lit tick scale
- Optional WebAudio: looped filtered noise with random crackle modulation, plus a soft hum, both reacting to intensity and touch
- devicePixelRatio-aware canvases (capped at 2) for crisp lines

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Hover the glass with a mouse to attract one or two filaments; press or touch and drag to make several converge under your fingertip, with extra branches, a glow and a ripple; release and they scatter. Drag the knob (or scroll on it, or use arrow keys when focused) to change intensity: filament count, brightness, thickness and flicker rate. The four pills, or keys 1 to 4, change the colour scheme. The speaker button turns the arc hum on (off by default, modest volume). Keyboard users can focus the globe, use arrows to move a virtual finger and Space to touch. The "Touch the glass" hint fades after the first interaction. With prefers-reduced-motion, flicker slows to 8 Hz, wandering is slower and phantom touches are disabled.
