A single 1600x1100 SVG drawing sheet from the fictional workshop Kittiwake & Drake: side elevation (fuselage truss, recumbent pilot with pedalling legs, chain drive, pylon-head gearbox, tail), plan view with tapered twin-spar wings, ribs and fabric membrane, and Section A-A showing the counter-rotating crank gears, connecting rods and rocker levers. ==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Staged stroke-dashoffset draw-on with pathLength normalisation and per-stage stagger; dash-dot centre-lines fade in so their pattern survives
- Real four-bar crank-rocker: circle-circle intersection solved with trigonometry each frame, tabulated over the crank angle; the wing flap angle is the rocker angle, giving a natural quick downstroke (166 degrees of crank) and slower upstroke (194 degrees)
- Five-segment flexible wing with phase lag, projected to plan and side views; tip trace polylines plotted every 2 degrees of crank angle
- Meshing gear pair generated as tooth paths, counter-rotating with correct phase
- Two-bone inverse kinematics for the pilot's legs, animated chain with dash offset
- JS-built dimension groups, balloons and a scalloped revision cloud; SVG patterns for grid, hatch and fabric
- Paper noise tile drawn on a tiny canvas; crease gradients, vignette and coffee-ring gradient
- Viewbox pan and zoom (wheel, drag, pinch, double-click) with fly-to view chips

==================== INTERACTION ====================
