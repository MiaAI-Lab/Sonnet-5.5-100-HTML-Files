==================== TITLE ====================
Daylight Around the World

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
An editorial data-art page on warm cream paper with a large Didone headline, "How much light do we get?", and a short two-paragraph intro with a vermilion drop cap. The main card plots daylight hours for seven latitudes, computed live in the browser from the sunrise equation. In Line form the year runs left to right with one 0-24 h axis, 2px lines in seven earthy colours, hairline grid, month ticks, equinox and solstice markers and direct end labels; a Radial button morphs the same 365 points smoothly into polar coordinates so the lines become petals, all crossing the 12 h ring at the equinoxes and Singapore staying almost a circle. A shaded band marks Longyearbyen's polar night (and a warm one its midnight sun). A draggable scrubber (rule or spoke) carries colour dots and a tooltip listing every place's daylight for the chosen date; the side card shows a big sun glyph whose rays, glow and opacity scale with the selected place's hours, the length as text, sunrise and sunset in solar time and a 24-hour strip. A legend highlights a series on hover or focus and selects it on click; a Table toggle swaps the chart for an accessible table.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Sunrise-equation computation with polar day/night clamping, precomputed for 7 latitudes by 365 days.
- Chart built as generated SVG, sized to its container, with a single eased mix parameter that interpolates every point between Cartesian and polar positions (lines, grid, month labels, bands, markers, scrubber and handle all morph together).
- Selective labelling: end-of-line names with simple collision repel, extremes (max and min) only for the selected series, equinox and solstice tags.
- Stroke-draw intro using normalised pathLength and dash offsets, then the scrubber glides to today's date.
- Pointer-driven scrubber for both geometries (x to day, atan2 to day), focusable slider handle with arrow, Page and Home/End keys, quick-jump chips for equinoxes and solstices.
- Data-driven SVG sun glyph (ray length, glow radius, drop-shadow and opacity from hours) and a 24 h sunrise/sunset strip.
- Legend as toggle buttons with hover and focus highlighting (others dimmed) and an HTML table alternative; responsive layout that moves the tooltip below the chart on phones.
- Paper texture from a small generated noise tile; reduced-motion mode skips the intro, glides and morph.

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Drag anywhere on the chart, or the round handle, to move the date; in Radial form the spoke follows the angle. Keyboard: focus the handle and use the arrow keys (Shift or Page keys for 30 days, Home/End for the ends). The Line / Radial toggle morphs the chart; the Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec chips and Today glide the scrubber; Chart / Table swaps to the table. Hover, focus or click a legend row to highlight or select a place (which also drives the sun glyph and the extremes labels). On touch, dragging works directly on the plot. With prefers-reduced-motion the chart appears complete at today's date and the morph is instant.
