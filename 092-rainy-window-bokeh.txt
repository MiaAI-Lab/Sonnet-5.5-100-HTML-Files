==================== TITLE ====================
Nocturne — Window Seat

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A single full-screen canvas seen from a window seat on a rainy night. Behind the glass a deep blue-black city dissolves into hundreds of amber, sodium, teal, magenta and white bokeh discs, some pulsing softly, with lit-window grids and slow car-light smears along the lower third. Up to 350 raindrops act as tiny refractive lenses showing the city upside down with a dark rim, a lower-edge glow and a specular glint; tiny drops merge into larger ones, heavy ones slide down with stick-slip motion leaving wet trails and satellite droplets. A blue-white fog with condensation beads coats the inside of the glass and can be wiped clear by dragging, slowly re-fogging. A warm sill holds a steaming mug, a dark plant leaf edges the corner with amber rim light, and a faint lamp ghost floats on the glass. A glowing italic "Nocturne" and the line "Rain until morning." sit bottom-left; an opt-in rain soundscape adds distant thunder and lightning.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Bokeh city pre-rendered once to an oversized offscreen canvas from soft-disc sprites with additive blending; a subset of discs is redrawn as a pulsing overlay with slow parallax drift
- Refractive raindrops: flipped, slightly magnified sample of the offscreen city clipped to each drop, dark radial rim, warm lower-edge caustic, specular highlights
- Resting drops cached on a separate layer and redrawn only when they change; only sliding drops are drawn per frame
- Drop physics: area-conserving merging, weight threshold for sliding, stick-slip velocity, lateral wobble, water loss along the path, satellite droplets, 350 drop cap
- Persistent trail layer with translucent wet lines that slowly evaporate
- Half-resolution fog layer with noise blotches and condensation beads, erased with soft destination-out brushes and slowly restored
- Foreground painted procedurally: window sill, ceramic mug with steam wisps, plant leaves with amber rim light, lamp reflection, vignette
- Additive passing car-light smears from generated streak sprites
- WebAudio rain: filtered white and brown noise beds, soft droplet blips and low-passed thunder, master gain capped, off by default
- Lightning wash synchronised with thunder; italic serif title with amber glow

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Drag a finger or mouse across the glass to wipe the fog; the sweep also clears resting drops, and the fog gradually returns. Pressing W wipes a horizontal band for keyboard users. The "Rain sound" button (default off) starts a filtered-noise rain bed with droplet ticks and occasional thunder, timed with a brief lightning flash; toggling it again fades the sound out. The city pulses and drifts on its own, drops grow, merge and run down the pane without input. With prefers-reduced-motion the parallax, flashes and pulsing are removed or heavily softened and fewer drops fall. Animation pauses when the tab is hidden.
