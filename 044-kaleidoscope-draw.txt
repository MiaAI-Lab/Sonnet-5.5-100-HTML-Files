==================== TITLE ====================
Kaleido: Symmetry Instrument

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A dark, full-screen light-painting instrument. Whatever the visitor draws is replicated in real time across 6, 8, 12, 16 or 24 rotational sectors, optionally with mirror reflection, using soft additive glow dabs whose colour drifts along the stroke through one of four palettes (Aubergine Neon, Ink and Gold, Sea Glass, Sunrise). Trails fade slowly so the mandala breathes. Before the first touch an auto-painter traces rose curves and Lissajous figures so the page is already a glowing mandala; it stops the moment a pointer arrives and resumes after a few quiet seconds. A huge tracked-caps KALEIDO title with a neon halo dissolves at the first stroke. A floating frosted-glass dock holds a sector stepper, brush and glow sliders, palette chips, Mirror, Undo, Clear and Save PNG; dashed guide lines mark the sector boundaries while dragging, and a radial vignette frames the piece.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Persistent paint canvas: only the newest segment of each stroke is stamped every frame; a periodic destination-out fill fades old light.
- Symmetry by transforms: for each sector (and its mirror image) one setTransform with rotation, then all dabs of the segment are drawn under it.
- Additive glow brush from pre-rendered radial-gradient sprites (72-step colour ring interpolated along the palette) drawn with globalCompositeOperation "lighter"; glow slider controls dab spread and alpha.
- Distance-spaced stamping and coalesced pointer events for smooth, even strokes; hue advances with path length.
- Idle auto-painter: rose curves (odd k) and Lissajous figures with slow radius modulation, pre-painted at load so the first frame is already beautiful.
- Undo by storing strokes and re-stamping the remaining ones; Clear; Save PNG through canvas.toBlob and a temporary download link.
- Glassmorphism dock (backdrop-filter), custom range styling, conic-gradient palette chips, tiny mono tracked-caps labels, radial vignette and neon text-shadow title.
- DPR capped at 2, resize handling, pause when the tab is hidden.

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Press and drag with mouse, pen or finger to paint; guide lines appear while dragging. The dock's minus and plus buttons step through 6, 8, 12, 16 and 24 sectors, sliders set brush size and glow, the chips switch palette (background and colours change together), Mirror toggles reflection, Undo removes the last stroke, Clear wipes the canvas, Save PNG downloads the image. Keyboard: Ctrl or Cmd+Z undo, C clear, M mirror, [ and ] change sector count. The idle painter yields to the visitor and returns after about five seconds of inactivity. Reduced motion: the idle painter draws one still mandala and stops, and trails fade far more slowly.
