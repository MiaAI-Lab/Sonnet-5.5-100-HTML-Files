==================== TITLE ====================
The Reading Room

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A walnut library wall on a dark forest-green wallpaper: a bookcase of three shelves (six on phones) holding twenty-four invented books, each dressed in one of ten binding styles (gold-foil cloth, Swiss white with a red dot, art-deco fans and chevrons, pulp paperbacks with tiny illustrations, oxblood leather with raised bands, striped cookbooks, poetry pamphlets, travel cloth with compass emblems, graph-paper science slabs, modern paperbacks). A brass rail spans the room with a rolling ladder, a swing-arm reading lamp throws a warm cone of light in which dust motes drift, and a monstera, a stack of horizontal books with an hourglass, an ivy pot, a candle, a steaming teacup, a bookend, a framed map and a borrowers' card fill the scene. Hovering draws a spine out in 3D while neighbours lean away; clicking flies the book to the centre as a turnable 3D volume with a typographic cover that opens into a two-page spread with a blurb and a first paragraph.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Procedural walnut grain and paper grain generated on canvas at load (periodic value noise with warped rings), applied as CSS background images through custom properties
- CSS 3D books: every spine is a real box (spine face, side boards, page-edge top) inside a perspective row, so tall books reveal their sides and tipped-out books show their top page edges
- Hover pose driven by CSS custom properties (--pull, --lean) with :has() sibling selectors so neighbours and second neighbours lean apart with spring easing
- Ten spine treatments built from gradients only: foil text via background-clip, cloth weave, pebbled leather with raised bands, deco conic-gradient fans, graph paper, stripes, plus tiny inline SVG illustrations
- Vertical titles with writing-mode: vertical-rl and a measure-and-shrink fitting pass so long titles wrap into columns that always fit the spine
- Centred 3D book card with hinged cover (cover front and inner blurb page, page block, spine, back), turntable sway, pointer-drag spin with inertia, FLIP fly-out from the exact spine position and animated return with a settle bounce
- Warm lamp cone drawn on canvas with blurred edges and screen blending, a dust-mote canvas whose motes brighten only inside the cone, and a lamp switch that darkens the room
- Fluid unit system: everything sized in a --u variable derived from viewport width and height, with a separate six-shelf layout on phones
- Brass plaque caption, brass rail and rolling ladder (SVG with wheels), CSS steam, flickering candle, swaying plant leaves

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Hover (or keyboard focus) pulls a spine toward you with a tilt while neighbours lean apart; a brass plaque names the book. Click, tap or press Enter to take it down: the book flies from the shelf to the centre, turns to show its cover and gently sways; drag it to spin it in 3D (it keeps inertia and returns to its sway), left and right arrow keys nudge the spin. Click the cover or the "Open the book" button to open the spread (blurb on the left, chapter one on the right); "Back to shelf", the backdrop or Esc returns it with animation and a small settle. Arrow keys, Home and End walk the shelves with a roving tabindex. Genre chips (All, Fiction, Poetry, Science, Cook, Travel) dim non-matching spines; "Surprise me" pulls a random visible book and then opens it. Extras: click the lamp to switch it off and on, click the ladder to roll it along the rail, click the plant to make it sway, click the teacup for a puff of steam. With prefers-reduced-motion the intro drop, sway, steam, flicker and dust drift are stilled and transitions are shortened.
