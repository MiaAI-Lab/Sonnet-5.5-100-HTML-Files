Marginal note

Iron is not so much attracted by a magnet as turned by it. Each filing becomes, for the moment, a small magnet itself, and swings until it lies along the direction of force. What we see is therefore not the field, but the field’s direction — written in six thousand short strokes.

Where the filings crowd and darken, the force is strong: at the poles. Where they thin and fade, it is weak.
