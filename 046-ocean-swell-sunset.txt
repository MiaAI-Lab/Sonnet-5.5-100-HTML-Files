==================== TITLE ====================
Golden Swell

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A full-screen real-time ocean at sunset. One fragment shader draws an infinite sea in perspective looking toward a low sun: eight octaves of sharpened sine waves (exp(sin-1) crests) with analytic gradients give the normals, band-limited by pixel footprint so the distance stays calm while the near water keeps sharp crests. A Fresnel reflection of a procedural sky (apricot horizon, rose mid-sky, violet zenith, fbm cloud bands, soft sun with glow and disc) fills the surface, a long golden glitter pillar runs down from the sun, crests carry faint foam and haze melts the horizon. A "Sun" slider scrubs from golden light through sunset and blue hour to a moonlit night with stars, a Milky-Way haze, a shaded moon and a silver glitter path. Over the scene sits a giant light-serif quote with word-by-word blur-in, a proverb credit, tiny mono readouts and a glass control dial. Without WebGL a layered CSS sea (gradient sky, drifting wave tiles, glitter pillars, moon, stars) takes over and reacts to the same slider.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Single full-screen-triangle WebGL fragment shader (GLSL ES 1.0), highp with mediump fallback, all shader errors caught with console.warn and CSS fallback
- Height field of 8 sharpened sine octaves with analytic gradient, per-octave phase noise, and pixel-footprint band-limiting; lost slope variance widens the specular lobe (Toksvig-style)
- Regula-falsi ray/height-field intersection with a footprint-faded low-octave field for a clean horizon
- Fresnel (Schlick) mix of dark teal body colour and a procedural sky; backlit crest translucency; foam from crest height, slope and noise
- Sky: elevation gradient from JS-interpolated palette keyframes, fbm cloud bands and cirrus, sun glow and disc, twilight horizon band, hash-grid twinkling stars, Milky-Way haze, moon disc with fbm maria and phase shading
- Click swells: travelling Ricker wavelets with analytic gradients (three concurrent), dynamic intersection bracket, crest foam, DOM ripple ring
- Palette keyframes by sun altitude (+24 to -26 degrees) in JS drive both the shader uniforms and the CSS fallback variables
- Adaptive render scale (0.75 when DPR > 1, lower for software rasterisers, auto-adjusts to frame time), ResizeObserver, context-loss handling
- Camera bob, roll and pointer yaw/pitch with smoothing; dither and vignette in the shader
- Light-serif quote with staggered blur reveal, text shadow, pointer parallax, top-left scrim; glass slider dial with palette-tinted thumb
- Opt-in WebAudio sea (looped brown noise, swash LFOs, capped gain)

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Move the pointer (or drag on touch) to look around by a few degrees; click or tap the water, press S, or use "Send a swell" to launch a big swell that rolls to the horizon. The Sun slider scrubs the whole day; while "Drift" is on the sun slowly wanders between -2 and 12 degrees and the slider follows it, and moving the slider switches drift off until re-enabled. The Sound chip starts a quiet sea ambience (off by default, swells briefly surge it). With prefers-reduced-motion the waves slow, camera bob and parallax stop and the text appears immediately; the page still renders fully.
