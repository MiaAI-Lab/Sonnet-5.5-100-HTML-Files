==================== TITLE ====================
Bell Curve Machine

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
An editorial plate on warm paper: a brass funnel pours glossy coral, teal, mustard and sage balls onto a triangular pegboard of brass pegs; each ball bounces from peg to peg and drops into one of 13 bins (rows plus one), where it stacks in a hex-packed pile so the pile itself becomes the histogram. A thin ink line draws the theoretical normal curve for Binomial(rows, p), with a dashed coral mean marker, a shaded and dimensioned plus/minus one sigma band, and live statistics in mono type: ball count, observed mean and standard deviation next to theory, and a fit score (one minus the total-variation distance) with a chi-square readout. The Bias slider tilts the whole board and skews the odds; Rows, Speed, Pour 100, Reset and a trickle toggle complete the controls. The Distribution toggle morphs every ball in the pile into a slice of a clean teal bar chart (rounded 4px tops, 2px gaps, gridlines, y-axis) under the same curve. When a bin fills, the pile halves its resolution and each resting ball stands for more balls, so the histogram never overflows.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Canvas 2D with pre-rendered glossy ball and brass peg sprites (radial gradients, soft shadow) drawn via drawImage at devicePixelRatio (capped at 2)
- Fixed-step (1/240 s) physics with real circle-circle ball-peg collision and elastic response; each contact is steered toward the next peg by solving the projectile time, so the outcome follows a Bernoulli(p) choice per row and the counts form Binomial(rows, p)
- Bins hold balls in a hex-packed layered pile with randomised slot order, landing bounce and settle; when a bin is full the pile halves and each ball represents 2, 4, 8... balls (older balls merge into the histogram)
- Statistics: observed mean and sigma, log-space binomial pmf, total-variation fit score and chi-square with degrees of freedom
- Pile-to-bar morph: each resting ball interpolates position, size and colour into a slice of a bar whose height uses the true counts; pegs fade; the normal curve rescales with the view
- Bias tilts the board with a CSS rotation transition and shifts the theoretical curve; ResizeObserver rebuilds geometry
- Editorial layout: serif display title, mono readouts, hairline plate frame, paper grain from repeating gradients

==================== INTERACTION ====================
The funnel trickles a ball every fraction of a second by default (toggle "Trickle"). Pour 100 queues a hundred balls; Space pours ten; clicking or tapping the funnel drops one. Bias (0.10 to 0.90) tilts the board and changes the odds without clearing the pile, so the fit score visibly lags until new balls catch up; Rows (6 to 16) rebuilds the board and resets; Speed multiplies simulation speed from 1x to 8x; Reset clears everything. Pile / Distribution (or the D key) morphs the balls into bars with axes and the normal curve. R resets. Reduced motion: the view morph and tilt are instant; ball physics remain (they are the content) and all states are complete.
