==================== TITLE ====================
The Meridian Ball 1929

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A one-page Art Deco invitation in midnight emerald and burnished gold. The hero is a poster: a huge sunburst of conic-gradient rays radiating from the bottom centre behind a stepped, lit-window skyline whose gold outline draws itself, concentric SVG fan arcs and dotted rings whose gold sheen sweeps across, a double-line gilded frame drawn clockwise with stepped-fan corner ornaments, a slowly rotating starburst, and the title THE MERIDIAN / BALL in thin geometric caps with a gold gradient and shimmer, over a deco rule and the date. Below, ziggurat, chevron and scallop-fan bands divide the sections: The Evening (italic poem, table CTA and three chamfered fact cartouches), a live countdown to the next 31 December midnight with digits in chamfered cartouches, a seven-stop programme timeline on a gold centre line with deco diamond bullets that light as you scroll, a gilded dress-code card with line icons, and an RSVP form (name, guest stepper, champagne-tower switch). Submitting bursts gold confetti and sparkles on canvas and swaps in a thank-you card with a reference number and, if chosen, an SVG champagne tower. Faint champagne bubbles rise behind everything.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Repeating conic-gradients (thin rays plus soft wedges) in a huge masked, slowly rotating disc; seamless because it turns exactly one ray period
- Hand-drawn SVG: fan arcs with pathLength dash-offset drawing, animated gradient stroke (SMIL gradientTransform) for a travelling gold sheen, mirrored stepped skyline via a single <use>, patterned lit windows that blink
- CSS hairline frame: eight 1px lines scaling in around the hero in sequence with a moving highlight gradient, plus SVG corner ornament symbols
- Card frames drawn by a conic-gradient mask driven by an @property angle (double gilded line)
- Reusable SVG patterns and symbols: ziggurat, chevron and seigaiha-fan bands, starburst, corner, bullet, flourish, champagne coupe
- Gold gradient text with a background-clip sheen sweep; chamfered cartouches and buttons via clip-path
- Live countdown with Date maths, per-digit tick animation and a midnight-wrap celebration
- Timeline with scroll-driven progress line (CSS variable) and lit bullets
- Canvas fx layer: pooled 4-point sparkle stars emitted while hovering .orn ornaments, and a confetti burst (rectangles, diamonds, dots, stars with flip)
- Canvas background of rising champagne bubbles with sway; IntersectionObserver reveals; accessible form with validation, stepper and role=switch toggle

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Hover (mouse) any gold ornament (starburst, corner ornaments, cartouches, timeline bullets, flourishes, fact cards) to make it throw sparkles; on touch, tapping one releases a small burst. The countdown ticks live each second. Scrolling reveals sections, draws card frames and advances the gold timeline line, lighting each bullet as it passes. In the RSVP form enter a name (required, with gentle shake and message if missing), step guests from 1 to 8 with the buttons or typing, and flip the champagne-tower switch; Accept the invitation bursts confetti from the button and replaces the form with a thank-you card summarising the reply (nothing is sent anywhere); Send another reply restores the form. The header turns solid after scrolling. With prefers-reduced-motion, hairlines and reveals appear instantly, rotation and bubbles stop, and the confetti is skipped while the thank-you card still appears.
