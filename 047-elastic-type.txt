==================== TITLE ====================
Elastic Words

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A bone-paper poster in which a twelve-line original poem ("Still water is only a held note ... Go far. Come back ringing.") is set in huge letters, alternating heavy grotesque capitals and Didone italics line by line. Every letter is its own spring-mass object drawn on canvas: it drops in on load, rests on its home position, and reacts to the pointer field, shockwaves, a swallowing black hole and falling glyphs. Letters lean with their velocity, stretch and squash along the direction of travel, leave a faint red motion trail and shift from ink to signal red as they move. The layout is fitted to the viewport (two columns on wide screens, one on portrait, rows sized to fill their width and re-wrapped to find the largest type) and recomputes on resize. The poem also exists as visually hidden real text.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Canvas per-letter physics: semi-implicit spring/damper integration in fixed 1/120 s sub-steps with clamped dt, per-letter stiffness jitter, rotation spring driven by velocity
- Fit-to-viewport typesetting: greedy word-wrap search over max row width, rows scaled to fill width, uniform height fit, size cap for short rows, two-column split on landscape, letter homes from measureText prefixes
- Pointer field (radius 160 px) with Repel / Attract / Swirl modes and a dashed / dotted / solid ring cursor
- Click shockwave: expanding Gaussian force band plus red ring; hold black hole: weakened home springs, inverse-distance pull with tangential swirl, letters shrink and spin, blast on release scaled by charge
- Squash and stretch along the velocity vector via rotate-scale-unrotate transforms; faint destination-out fading trail layer on an offscreen canvas
- Velocity and displacement mapped to an ink-to-red colour lookup table (no per-frame string allocation)
- Typed letters spawn falling circle-collider glyph bodies with gravity, floor and wall bounces, and impulse collisions against the poem letters
- Stiffness slider retunes spring constant with constant damping ratio; sliding-pill segmented control
- SVG feTurbulence paper grain, vignette and crop marks; visually hidden poem text; DPR capped at 2

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Move the mouse to push, pull or swirl letters within about 160 px (choose the mode in the bottom bar; on touch the field follows a finger while it is down). Click for a shockwave ring; press and hold for a black hole that grows for about two seconds and blasts everything outward when released. Type any letter key to drop that glyph onto the poem; Space or "Breathe" sends a gentle ring from the centre. The Stiffness slider changes how quickly and bouncily letters return. With prefers-reduced-motion the drop-in and idle breathing are skipped and field forces are softened, but all interactions still work.
