==================== TITLE ====================
Hue Atelier

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
An airy, off-white (#faf8f5) colour-harmony studio with 20px-radius cards, soft shadows and hairline gradient borders that animate on hover and take their colours from the palette. The left card holds a large OKLCH wheel painted per pixel with ImageData (hue around, chroma outward, at a lightness set by a slider; out-of-gamut colours are shown veiled toward grey) with draggable handles for the base colour and each harmony point, joined by a thin polygon. Six harmony chips (complementary, split, triadic, tetradic, analogous, monochrome) glide the handles to their new positions. The right card shows five tall swatches with hex, rgb and oklch text, a lock toggle each, and click-to-copy with a toast; Shuffle (or the space bar) composes fresh, gamut-mapped palettes while respecting locks. Below sit a live poster preview (headline, button, card, bar chart) recoloured from the palette, a WCAG 2.x contrast panel with AA, AAA and AA-large badges from computed ratios, a nine-step OKLCH shades strip for the selected swatch, three export blocks (CSS variables, JSON, SVG strip) with copy buttons, a colour-blindness simulator (protanopia, deuteranopia, tritanopia) and saved palettes kept in localStorage as small chips. The page accent (title, buttons, borders) follows the selected swatch, adjusted for legibility.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Full OKLab/OKLCH to linear sRGB conversion, chroma-reduction gamut mapping by binary search, WCAG relative luminance and contrast ratios.
- Colour wheel painted per pixel into ImageData at 300x300 and scaled up smoothly; repainted only when the lightness slider changes.
- SVG overlay for handles and the harmony polygon, with pointer-event dragging, roving focus, ARIA slider semantics and springy eased handle motion when the harmony changes (idle loop skips work when settled).
- Palette generator: per-harmony hue offsets, lightness ramps and chroma multipliers; locked swatches keep their exact colours through shuffles and harmony changes.
- Colour-blindness previews using Machado matrices applied in linear RGB to swatches, poster, shades and contrast samples (values shown stay true).
- Correct contrast panel: six real text/background pairs, ratio to two decimals, AA (4.5), AAA (7) and AA-large (3) badges.
- Export generation for CSS custom properties, JSON with rgb and oklch, and a standalone SVG strip; clipboard API with execCommand fallback and a toast.
- localStorage persistence wrapped in try/catch, saved palettes as chips with load and delete, loading locks swatches.
- Gradient borders drawn with layered padding-box/border-box backgrounds (cheap, no masks) and animated on hover or focus.
- Accent adaptation: selected swatch becomes the page accent, darkened when too pale to read on paper.

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Drag the base handle (the first one) to change hue and chroma, or drag any harmony handle to rotate the whole shape and adjust that colour's chroma; the polygon and swatches update live. Click a harmony chip to animate the handles into a new shape. Move the lightness slider to repaint the wheel. Click a swatch to select it (the shades strip and page accent follow) and copy its hex with a toast; click the padlock to lock or unlock it (L key also toggles the selected one). Space or the Shuffle button generates a new palette without touching locked swatches. Arrow Left/Right nudge the hue (Shift for larger steps) and Up/Down nudge chroma. Click a shade to copy it. Use the colour-vision chips to preview protanopia, deuteranopia or tritanopia. Copy buttons under the export blocks copy CSS, JSON or the SVG. Save palette stores the current five colours; click a chip to load it, use its x to delete it. Works with mouse and touch; prefers-reduced-motion removes handle easing and transitions.
