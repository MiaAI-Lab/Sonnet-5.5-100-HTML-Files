==================== TITLE ====================
Paper Hours Diorama

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A calm, tactile paper-cut landscape sitting in a cream card shadow box (bevelled frame, hairline inset rule, deep inner shadow) on a dusky wall that changes with the hour. Ten stacked SVG sheets cut from card build the scene from far to near: a gradient sky with paper stars, a sun-and-moon layer of concentric paper discs, faceted far mountains, drifting back clouds, mid mountains with folded shading, front clouds and flapping paper birds, rolling hills, a far bank of pine cut-outs with a small log cabin (warm flickering window, curling paper smoke), a pale river with a folded paper boat that sails across, and dark foreground pines, grass and pebbles with fireflies at night. Every sheet has a soft feDropShadow, a lit top edge and a shared paper-grain tile, so the layers read as separate card stock. A segmented control (Dawn, Noon, Dusk, Night) with a sliding paper pill crossfades the whole palette and moves the sun and moon along their arcs; a "Let the hours pass" button cycles them automatically. A taped paper label reads "PAPER HOURS — No. 11".

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Ten inline SVG layers with per-depth feDropShadow filters (shadow cast upward so each sheet lifts off the one behind), a lit-edge stroke and faceted fold shapes.
- Paper grain: a noise-and-fibre tile drawn once on a small canvas and used as an SVG pattern overlay on every sheet, plus on the label.
- Palette system of 20 CSS custom properties per hour, interpolated in JS with an ease-in-out curve and throttled repaints, driving fills, sky gradient stops, wall colour, cabin light and smoke.
- Sun and moon positions tweened along per-hour anchor points; stars and fireflies fade in with the night factor.
- Depth parallax: each layer translates by pointer position (or deviceorientation tilt) scaled by its depth, smoothed with easing, with a slow layered sine idle sway.
- CSS-only details: flapping wing pairs, puff-by-puff smoke, cabin window flicker, bobbing boat on a long sail loop, seamless sliding clouds, twinkling stars, wandering fireflies.
- Shadow-box frame built from gradients, inset shadows and a vignette overlay; taped paper label; soft rounded UI with a sliding pill radio group.
- Reduced-motion support and a compact 4:5 portrait crop for phones.

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Move the pointer over the box (or tilt a phone) and the sheets slide by depth so you peer around them; when idle they sway slowly. Choose Dawn, Noon, Dusk or Night with the segmented control (click, tap, or arrow keys) and the entire palette, sun and moon crossfade over about a second and a half; "Let the hours pass" cycles the hours every few seconds and any manual choice stops it. Birds flap, clouds slide, the boat drifts and smoke curls on their own. With prefers-reduced-motion the loops stop, the sway is removed and the hour changes instantly, while the scene still renders complete.
