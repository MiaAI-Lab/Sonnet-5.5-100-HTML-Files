==================== TITLE ====================
Four Seasons Tree

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A single stochastic-branching tree drawn on a full-screen Canvas 2D paper-cut landscape (layered hills with paper-thickness shadows, cut-paper clouds, concentric-ring sun, grass fringe, paper grain and vignette). A continuous season value from 0 to 4 (spring, summer, autumn, winter, wrapping back to spring) blends the sky, ground, bark, leaf colours, blossoms, falling leaves, snow caps, creatures and daylight all at once. A large light italic serif season name, a ghosted month numeral, a month strip under the slider and a tiny sun/moon arc showing hours of daylight complete the almanac feel. Tapered curved limbs sway with correlated low-frequency noise that grows toward the tips; up to about 3800 pointed leaves cluster on the outer twigs.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Stochastic branching generator (seeded RNG) with leader plus laterals, phototropism, random curvature, area-preserving width taper, about 1000 branches and 3800 leaves
- Branch geometry recomputed each frame parent-first in typed arrays; sway angles from summed sines with phase inherited from the parent so neighbouring twigs move together, amplitude growing with depth
- Tapered quadratic-Bézier bark polygons batched per depth level, with light and dark edge strokes and an offset paper shadow on the main limbs
- Deterministic season timeline: per-leaf bud time and fall time, so scrubbing forward or back is exact; leaves flutter down along a sinusoidal path and settle into ground litter that snow then covers
- Leaves batched by six colour bins (depth-varied amber, rust, crimson) to keep 4000 leaves at a few fills per frame
- Cyclic keyframe blending with hold plateaus at each season for sky, hills, ground, bark, sun and clouds
- Blossoms, petal particles, dappled-light sprites, birds, butterflies, snowfall and a perched robin, each faded by its own season window
- Branch-by-branch growth animation over about 4 seconds, driven by wall-clock time
- DOM HUD: crossfading season name, blend percentages, month strip, SVG sun/moon day-length arc computed from a daylight curve
- Paper grain tile generated on a small canvas; frosted-glass control panel

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Drag the season slider (0 to 4) to blend seasons continuously; "Auto" (on by default, key A) advances a full year in about 40 seconds and pauses while the slider is held. Click or tap the tree (or press Regrow, key G) to regrow it branch by branch; "New tree" (key N) generates another seed. The Wind slider changes the sway amplitude and drift of clouds, petals and snow. In winter, click the robin and it flies away, returning later. Under prefers-reduced-motion the year does not auto-advance, wind starts gentle, flutter and snow are reduced. Animation pauses when the tab is hidden.
