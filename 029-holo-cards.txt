==================== TITLE ====================
Cosmic Fauna Collector Cards

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A dark-velvet card table under a soft spotlight beam, with dust motes drifting in the light. A giant foil-gradient "COSMIC FAUNA" headline sits above a hand of six 5:7 collector cards fanned in an arc: Nebula Fox, Comet Whale, Prism Moth, Lantern Jelly, Void Owl and Aurora Stag. Each card has an original geometric SVG illustration, a condensed-bold name bar, HP and type badge, mono species strip, two attacks with energy pips, italic flavour text and rarity stars. Rarities use four foil styles: regular rainbow, cosmos texture, gold etched and noisy rainbow-rare. Clicking a card lifts it to the centre with a full 3D spin, flanked by field-notes and specimen-data panels; clicking it again flips it to a holo star-map back with that creature's constellation. A "Open a pack" button gathers the cards face down, riffle-shuffles, draws three at random and flips them in a cascade with particle bursts.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Six original inline-SVG illustrations built from mirrored polygon facets, gradients, glow filters and seeded starfields
- Foil rig made of static blend containers (mix-blend-mode color-dodge / overlay / soft-light) whose oversized inner layers only move by transform, so nothing repaints per frame
- Rainbow 115deg gradient layer, conic sparkle layer masked by a runtime-generated tiny-star tile, and a pointer-following radial glare layer
- Canvas-generated textures (star mask, cosmos nebula tile, colour noise) exposed as CSS custom properties
- Four rarity foils: rainbow with diffraction lines, cosmos texture, gold etched hatching, noisy rainbow
- 3D tilt (max 15 degrees) via perspective transforms with preserve-3d flip and backface-visibility, plus idle foil drift so cards shimmer untouched
- Fan layout computed in JS with hover spread, neighbour push and CSS-transition motion; focus dock built from the same live card elements
- Star-map card back: SVG chart rings, tick marks via stroke-dasharray, textPath ring text, per-creature constellation
- Velvet canvas (folds and pile strokes), spotlight beam, light bloom tinted per creature, dust motes and spark bursts on a half-resolution canvas
- Pack opening as an async sequence: gather face-down, riffle, draw three, flip cascade with bursts

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Hover the fan to spread it; the hovered card straightens, lifts and tilts toward the pointer while the foil and glare follow. Click or tap a card to hold it up: it spins once and centres, pointer movement tilts it up to 15 degrees (device orientation is used on phones), click again, press Space/F or use the button to flip to the star map. Arrow keys (or the on-screen arrows, swipe on touch) cycle cards; Esc or "Back to the hand" returns; the dock thumbnails switch cards. "Open a pack" (or P) runs the shuffle and three-card reveal; clicking a pulled card inspects it. Clicking the empty table throws gold sparks and S sends a shooting star. Idle cards drift their foil on their own. With prefers-reduced-motion, idle drift, bobbing and long transitions are removed or shortened while everything remains functional.
