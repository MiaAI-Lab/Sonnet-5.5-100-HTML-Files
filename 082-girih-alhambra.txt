==================== TITLE ====================
Girih, the Geometry of Order

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A full-screen Islamic star-pattern tiling that is generated, not drawn: JavaScript builds a de Bruijn multigrid rhombus tiling (a periodic 6-fold and 8-fold star-and-cross lattice, a Penrose-based 10-fold and a 12-fold dodecagonal patch), decorates every tile with polygons-in-contact rays, and renders the result as interlaced ivory bands with gold double-line edges, woven over and under, above glazed tiles in turquoise, lapis, ivory, terracotta and gold. On load the piece is built as if by compass and straightedge on a deep indigo zellige-textured ground (a ring of 8 points, then 12, then 10, with construction circles, chords and star polygons drawn in pale gold hairlines) before the bands and the colour wash spread outward from the centre. Small ornamental rosettes at the high-valence vertices turn slowly and breathe; a scalloped gold-and-indigo border frames the screen, a serif caption sits in a dark capsule at the top and a floating control bar switches family, replays the construction and cycles the Alhambra, Marrakech and Nocturne palettes.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- de Bruijn multigrid generation of rhombus tilings with exact integer vertex keys (shared vertices, valence and bipartite parity), giving periodic 6- and 8-fold lattices and quasi-periodic 10- and 12-fold patches
- Hankin polygons-in-contact: rays from edge midpoints at one contact angle meet at star points, which yields continuous strapwork lines through every tile edge
- Over-under weaving decided by vertex parity: bands are drawn as a wide gold stroke plus a narrower ivory stroke (double lines), then short over-strand pieces are redrawn on top at each crossing so the under band appears to pass beneath
- Cells and star interiors merged into a handful of colour-class paths (turquoise, lapis, ivory, terracotta, gold) with translucent inner gem stars and a single soft glaze and vignette overlay
- Compass-and-straightedge intro drawn on a canvas: partial circle arcs, chords, radial lines and star polygons that grow with easing, ring sequence 8, 12, 10, then two feathered radial mask wipes reveal the woven bands and then the glazed fills
- Rosette ornaments (unit star tables, one sin/cos per rosette per frame) spin and breathe on a viewport canvas, with a radial glow and a spin-up on the hovered rosette
- Click reveals the construction geometry (circle, ring points, n-gon, star polygon and compass circles) around the chosen rosette and fades it away
- Layers split for performance: static SVG for fills and bands moved by compositor transforms, animated content only on a small canvas
- Scalloped ornamental border generated from arc segments to fit any viewport, faint zellige star-and-diamond pattern in the background, CSS-variable palettes with cross-fading colours

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Drag anywhere to pan the lattice; release for inertia, with an elastic pull-back at the edge of the generated patch (arrow keys nudge it too). Hover a rosette (a small star ornament at a high-valence vertex) and it spins up and glows; click or tap the lattice to unveil the compass-and-straightedge construction behind the nearest rosette or vertex, drawn in pale gold and fading after a few seconds. The bottom bar offers 6-, 8-, 10- and 12-fold families (keys 1 to 4), a Construct button that replays the full ring-of-8, 12 and 10 animation (key C) and a palette button cycling Alhambra, Marrakech and Nocturne (key P). With prefers-reduced-motion the intro is skipped, ornaments hold still and inertia is removed, but all interactions remain.
