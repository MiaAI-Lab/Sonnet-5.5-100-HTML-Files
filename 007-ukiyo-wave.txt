==================== TITLE ====================
Great Swell, Small Boats

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A flat, carved-outline woodblock seascape on cream washi. A huge vermilion sun sits low behind two ranges of misty indigo mountains, printed slightly off its sumi outline; stepped pale-blue washes and long tapering kasumi mist bands fill the sky. Below, some 25 rows of seigaiha scales grade from pale blue through teal to indigo, growing with depth. On the left a great wave (its back slope shaded with stepped bokashi and fine contour lines, its lip a teal shell edged with cream foam and hooked claw-like fingers over a dark hollow) rises, curls, crashes and falls on a 12-second loop. Three small fishing boats with straw-hatted fishermen bob on the swell. A cream cartouche at the right carries the bold vertical title, a small italic subtitle column and a vermilion hanko seal. Paper fibre, mottling, speckle, a soft edge vignette and a double neatline finish the print.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Three hand-keyed wave shapes (swell, crest, crash), each a fixed set of outline, lip-underside, face and toe points, morphed through one cycle and turned into smooth Catmull-Rom to Bezier paths every frame.
- The wave is built from stacked flat layers: body, stepped bokashi bands, contour lines, hollow in three tones, teal lip shell with pale band and hatch, a foam band with scalloped blobs, and clusters of hooked claw paths whose length and angle follow the lip.
- Each seigaiha row is its own small SVG layer (four concentric ring paths); the sea heaves purely with per-row CSS transforms (staggered sine offsets in x, y and scale), so the compositor does the work.
- Far layers (sky, sun, mountains) sit on separate layers that slide with pointer x at different rates for parallax; the wave phase is nudged by pointer x.
- Block-print misregistration: the vermilion sun colour is offset from its sumi outline; a stamped hanko uses feTurbulence + feDisplacementMap for an irregular edge.
- Washi paper from stacked feTurbulence filters (mottling, two crossing fibre directions, speckle) on a static overlay, plus a radial edge vignette and double neatline.
- Click crash: a 110-particle SVG spray pool with gravity, splash at the pointer, and a time-lapse that fast-forwards the wave loop to its crest and fall while the sea shudders.
- Responsive scene: separate landscape and portrait layouts, with the viewBox recomputed from the window aspect.
- Boats ride inside the layer of the row nearest their waterline so later rows overlap the keels; invisible button hit-targets follow them for hover, focus and touch.

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Move the pointer left and right: the sun, mountains, wave and every row of scales slide at their own rates and the wave's phase drifts. Hover a boat (or focus it with Tab, or tap it) and a paper slip unfolds beside it with an original three-line haiku. Click or tap anywhere on the print, or press Space/Enter: foam splashes at the pointer, the sea shudders, the wave loop hurries to its crest and the wave crashes in a burst of spray before reforming. A hint label fades after the first interaction. With prefers-reduced-motion the sea heaves gently, the loop runs at half speed, parallax is off and the sky does not drift.
