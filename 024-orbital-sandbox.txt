==================== TITLE ====================
Orbits: Gravity Sandbox

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
An editorial n-body sandbox on charcoal. Real units: distances in AU, time in years, G = 4 pi^2, so a planet at 1 AU with speed 2 pi AU/yr circles a one-solar-mass star in exactly a year. A warm-white star sits at the centre with a soft glow, slowly turning corona rays and a pulsing halo; faint polar rings and mono AU labels measure range from the camera focus. A huge, light-weight year counter, mono micro-labels and a slim left rail listing every body (colour dot, mass, distance, speed, focus and delete) frame the canvas; readouts show body count, energy drift and time scale; a slim bottom toolbar holds presets, planet size (S coral, M cyan, L sand, Gas giant lilac), a time slider, pause, trails, add and clear. Dragging launches a planet with a live dashed trajectory that integrates all bodies 300 steps ahead, shows speed, eccentricity and semi-major axis, and snaps to a perfect circular orbit with a cyan reference tick. Planets leave fading trails, merge on contact conserving momentum with a light flash, and are removed when they escape. Presets: Solar-ish, Binary star with circumbinary and circumstellar planets, the exact Chenciner-Montgomery figure-8 (three coloured stars) scaled to 2 AU, and a Slingshot in which five probes fly at a moving gas giant and are flung out.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Velocity-Verlet (kick-drift-kick) integrator with Plummer softening, adaptive sub-step from the closest pair's dynamical time and a clamped frame dt
- Inelastic merging with momentum conservation, mass-weighted colour, flash sprites; escape detection by distance and unbound energy
- Live prediction: copies all bodies to typed arrays and integrates 300 steps with a step tied to the local orbital period; dashed fading path, orbital elements about the dominant attractor
- Circular-orbit snap and speed tick, click-to-orbit auto placement around the strongest attractor
- Energy drift readout re-baselined after every non-conservative event
- Canvas 2D: pre-rendered glow and planet sprites, chunked fading trails from ring buffers, additive star corona, edge chevrons for off-screen bodies, camera that follows barycentre or a focused body
- Exact figure-8 initial conditions scaled to AU and years
- DOM rail rebuilt on change and refreshed at 5 Hz; keyboard shortcuts (space, T, 1-4, +/-, Esc, Delete)
- Pointer events with capture, two-finger pinch and wheel zoom; slow-motion aiming

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Press on empty space and drag: the planet appears at the press point, a dashed path shows where it will go, and the simulation slows while you aim; releasing fires it. A short click drops a planet onto a circular orbit. Wheel or pinch zooms; the size buttons (or keys 1-4) choose mass. Presets reset the system. The time slider spans 0.05 to 4 years per second; Pause (or Space), Trails (T), + Planet (random circular orbit) and Clear (a lone star) sit beside it. Click a body in the rail to focus the camera on it (again to release) or press its x to delete; on phones the rail opens from the Bodies button. With prefers-reduced-motion the simulation runs a little slower and the toast fade is instant.
