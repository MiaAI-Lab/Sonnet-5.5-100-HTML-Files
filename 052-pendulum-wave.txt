==================== TITLE ====================
Pendulum Wave

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A dark stage with warm spotlight cones, a glossy floor with faint lane lines and a brass rail hung from the ceiling. Twenty pendulums (10-30 with the slider) hang from the rail; string lengths follow the physics of their tuned frequencies, and glowing spherical bobs are coloured along a coral-amber-lime-teal-sky-violet gradient. Motion is purely analytic: the angle of pendulum i is A·cos(2π(N+i)u) with u = t/T the cycle progress, so every cycle ends in an exact re-alignment. A small 3D camera renders the row from a slight three-quarter front angle and smoothly pitches up to a top-down view where bobs become dots on a line whose vertical displacement shows the phase pattern, joined by a faint wave curve. Bobs have fading analytic trails and reflections in the floor. A timeline strip with ticks marks time within the cycle, labels the phases (travelling wave, standing wave, apparent chaos, re-alignment) and can be scrubbed; mono readouts show osc / cycle for the first and last pendulum, or full details for a hovered bob. "Drop all" holds every bob at full extension and releases them left to right with a stagger quantised to each pendulum's own period so alignment stays exact. Sound is opt-in: every centre crossing plays a soft marimba-like pentatonic note mapped to the pendulum's index.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Analytic harmonic motion with cycle progress u = t/T, so changing the cycle time never makes the pattern jump and re-alignment is exact
- Lightweight 3D projection (yaw, pitch, perspective) animated between Front and Top-down views, with a depth-scaled vertical exaggeration in the top view
- Pre-rendered radial-gradient sprites per bob for glow and glossy sphere, drawn additively
- Trails computed by sampling the analytic function at earlier times (no history buffers), tapering alpha and width
- Mirror-plane reflections whose brightness falls with height above the floor, glossy floor plane, lane and depth grid lines, additive spotlight cones and light pools
- Brass rail built from stacked strokes with highlights and ceiling rods
- Release-time quantisation to each pendulum's period for a theatrical stagger that keeps exact alignment
- Scrubbable SVG timeline with phase bands, ticks and playhead; keyboard-operable slider semantics
- WebAudio marimba voice: sine fundamental plus a 4x partial, fast exponential decay, convolver reverb from generated noise, compressor and polyphony cap; zero-crossing detection by integer phase counting
- Minimal monospace readouts, hover and tap inspection of bobs, and reduced-motion handling (slow cycle, instant view change)

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Switch between Front and Top-down with the segmented control (or V); the camera glides between them. Drag or tap the timeline to scrub through the cycle, or use its arrow keys. The Cycle time slider (30-120 s) changes speed without breaking the pattern; the Pendulums slider (10-30) adds or removes pendulums. Drop all (D) resets to the held row and releases the bobs in a staggered sweep. Pause/Play (Space) and Step buttons move a quarter second at a time (also the arrow keys when paused). Hover or tap a bob to see its index, oscillations per cycle, period and frequency and to lengthen its trail; tapping also plays its note when sound is on. The speaker button (S) turns the pentatonic marimba on after a user gesture, at a modest volume. With prefers-reduced-motion the cycle defaults to 120 s, the opening drop is skipped and view changes are instantaneous.
