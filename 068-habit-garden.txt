==================== TITLE ====================
Habit Garden

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A cream-paper mini-app in which a checklist card grows a garden. On the left, a wooden-framed window looks out on a rolling green landscape (meadow flowers, two hill layers, drifting clouds, a sun and moon on arcs, stars, a string of fairy lights) whose sky is computed from the visitor's real local hour: pink dawn, blue day, golden sunset, violet dusk and a deep night full of twinkling stars, glowing fairy lights and blinking fireflies. Two hanging rope shelves (one long shelf on desktop, two stacked shelves on phones) hold up to six terracotta pots, each with a cream name label. Five habits start in the garden (Drink water as a fern, Move as a sunflower, Read as a tulip, Meditate as lavender, Sleep by 11 as a flowering cactus) at different growth stages, and a sixth empty pot with a plus sign invites a new habit (a daisy bouquet is the sixth plant type). Every plant is drawn procedurally in SVG with gradient shading and grows continuously through five stages, seed, sprout, leaf, bud and bloom, then sways in the wind; a fern unfurls fiddleheads, a tulip bud opens into a cup, a sunflower turns into a seed-disc head, lavender builds floret spikes, the cactus grows arms and a pink crown. On the right, a paper card lists today's habits with big tick boxes, a leaf-shaped streak chip, a seven-dot week strip, rename and compost buttons, a progress ring, an add-habit form with six plant-type radio tiles, a Fast-forward a day button and a Start over button. Below the shelf sit summary tiles (in bloom, best streak, watered this week, thirsty) and a friendly garden log. Missed days wilt a plant by desaturating it toward straw and drooping its head, leaves and stems; ticking revives it in a shower of sparkles. A butterfly flits between blooming plants by day, and hovering a tick swaps the cursor for a watering can that drips.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Fully procedural SVG plants built from JavaScript-generated path strings: fern fronds integrated from a curling rachis with unfurl-controlled curvature and paired lens-shaped pinnae, cubic-stem sunflower with Vogel-spiral seed disc and layered petals, tulip cup from four gradient petals, lavender spikes of curve-following ellipses, ribbed cactus with generated spines and arms, three-stem daisy bouquet
- One continuous growth parameter per plant (0 to 4) drives every part through smoothstep windows, animated with an ease-out-back tween so growth overshoots and settles
- Wilting computed from stored dates (days missed since the last tick, capped at three): a per-plant feColorMatrix filter desaturates and warms colours, and every plant type droops heads, leaves and stems by its own rules; streak and stage changes are undoable on the same day
- Sky engine: hour-keyed palette table interpolated in RGB (top, middle, bottom, three hill layers, ambient overlay, stars, fairy lights, fireflies, cloud colour), sun and moon on arcs with horizon fade, smooth shortest-path transitions when previewing Dawn, Day, Dusk or Night, live clock label
- Ambient life driven by one rAF loop with delta time: wind sway per plant (JS-set transforms), drifting clouds, twinkling stars and bulbs, 16 firefly sprites with glow blinking, and a butterfly state machine (fly along a cubic Bezier to a bloom, perch with slow wings, choose another)
- Effects via Web Animations API on SVG groups: staggered star sparkles around the flower head, a rain shower of droplets onto the pot, pot pop-in for new plants
- Persistence with localStorage wrapped in try/catch and an in-memory fallback; habits store name, plant type, stage, streak, last date, recent days and an undo snapshot; a virtual day offset powers time travel; everything visual is derived from dates
- Responsive scene: the layout is rebuilt when the container crosses 620px (single shelf to two shelves), CSS grid reordering with display contents on small screens, ui-rounded font stack and a disciplined seven-colour palette
- Accessibility: real checkboxes with labels, radio group for plant types, rename and compost buttons with inline confirmation, keyboard-operable empty-pot button, aria-live announcements, aria-labelled plants, visible focus rings, prefers-reduced-motion (no sway, no flight, static fireflies, instant growth)

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Tick a habit (mouse, touch or Space on the focused checkbox): a rain shower falls on its pot, the plant grows one stage with a springy tween and a sparkle burst, its streak chip and week dots update, the progress ring fills and a line is added to the garden log; ticking again the same day undoes it. While the mouse hovers a tick box the cursor becomes a tilted watering can that drips droplets, and pressing pours a burst. Hover or focus a list row and its plant glows with a tooltip (name, type, stage, streak or thirst); hover, click or tap a plant to make it wiggle and show the same tooltip. Rename with the pencil (Enter saves, Escape cancels) and compost a habit with the bin (press twice to confirm). Add a habit by typing a name, choosing one of six plant types (Fern, Sunflower, Tulip, Lavender, Cactus, Daisy) and pressing Plant it, or by activating the empty pot on the shelf; the shelf holds at most six and the form disables itself when full. Fast-forward a day advances a virtual date so unticked habits reset, streaks break and neglected plants wilt one to three days' worth; Start over (with a second click to confirm) restores the starter garden. The Live, Dawn, Day, Dusk and Night chips preview the sky; Live follows the real clock and the label shows the local time. A butterfly visits blooming plants during the day. Progress persists across reloads through localStorage (or in memory if storage is blocked). With prefers-reduced-motion, sway, cloud drift, butterfly flight, firefly movement and long tweens are switched off while all states remain visible.
