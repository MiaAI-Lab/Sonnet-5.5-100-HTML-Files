==================== TITLE ====================
Make a Wish: Dandelion

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A full-screen canvas portrait of a dandelion clock at golden hour. Two hundred and twenty seeds sit on a slowly rotating, gently swaying sphere; each has a filament, an achene and a starburst pappus of fine bristles, lit with a warm apricot rim and a soft halo, in front of a blurred sage-and-apricot meadow with drifting bokeh discs, slow light rays and blurred foreground grass blades. Press and hold (or sweep the pointer quickly across the head) and a gust builds: seeds on the downwind side let go first, become buoyant parachutes that tilt, spin and tumble on turbulent wind, and drift across the screen, many dissolving into out-of-focus glows. A counter of wishes released climbs, "make a wish" fades in as delicate italic serif, tiny sparkles trail each seed, and seeds that settle in the grass sometimes release a gentle phrase such as "may you find the quiet hour". When the last seed has gone the bare receptacle sways; after about six seconds (or on "Grow again") a green bud appears, opens into a golden bloom and swells into a new puffball. Palette: apricot, sage, sky, cream and seed white; type: light italic serif with tiny tracked sans.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Pseudo-3D seed sphere: Fibonacci-distributed unit vectors rotated each frame by a hand-built 3x3 matrix (yaw, tilt, sway roll) with weak perspective; depth and rim factors pick one of eight stroke styles, and all segments of a style are stroked in a single batched path.
- Each seed is a filament from the receptacle plus a fan of up to 16 bristle segments whose directions live in a per-seed tangent frame; warm rim colour on silhouette seeds, faint cool underlay so white seeds keep contrast.
- Stalk drawn as a tapered bezier ribbon that bends with an inverted-pendulum sway spring driven by the wind.
- Flight physics: drag toward a wind field made of gust, breeze and sine turbulence, per-seed buoyancy and thermals, pendulum tilt, spin-squash for 3D tumbling, and depth-of-field (scale, fade and crossfade from crisp lines to soft glow sprites).
- Detach hazard depends on gust strength and the seed's alignment with the wind direction; hover wind is derived from pointer speed.
- Backdrop composed on a small canvas every other frame (gradient, animated light rays, sprite bokeh with screen blending) and scaled up, plus a pre-blurred foreground grass layer; CSS vignette and SVG grain finish.
- Sparkle pool with additive four-point stars; DOM phrases that rise and fade; regrow sequence bud, golden bloom, staggered seed emergence.
- Optional WebAudio wind (filtered brown noise whose gain and frequency follow the gust) and soft pentatonic chimes for phrases, created only after the sound button is pressed, master level under 0.2.
- Adaptive quality (fewer bristles when frames run long), devicePixelRatio cap, pause when the tab is hidden.

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Press and hold anywhere (mouse or touch), or hold the space bar, to blow; the wind direction runs from the pointer through the seed head, and the gust builds over about half a second and fades over a second after release. Sweeping the pointer quickly over the head without pressing makes a lighter hover wind. Seeds detach probabilistically, the wishes-released counter and progress line advance, and "make a wish" appears while blowing. When all seeds are gone a "Grow again" button appears (also automatic after roughly six seconds). The speaker button toggles soft wind ambience (off by default). Reduced motion: gentler sway, slower drift, fewer sparkles, no idle release, shorter regrowth. Screen readers get a live region announcing milestones and landed wishes.
