==================== TITLE ====================
Molecule Cabinet

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A cream-and-walnut curio cabinet with brass corner fittings and a glass front holds a large display case in which one molecule at a time turns on a brass turntable: water, methane, ethanol, benzene, caffeine and buckminsterfullerene, drawn on a canvas as softly shaded ball-and-stick models (graphite carbon, near-white hydrogen, red oxygen, blue nitrogen) with cylinder bonds, parallel double bonds and depth fog. Below the case a shelf of six glass specimen jars with cork lids and brass labels shows mini spinning previews and selects the specimen; beside it a specimen card gives the name, a subscripted formula, molar mass, atom and bond counts, a fun-fact paragraph with a drop cap and a Measure panel that reports the element, bond lengths and bond angles of any clicked atom. All coordinates are schematic, authored or computed in angstroms.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Painter's algorithm: atoms and half-bonds are sorted together by depth every frame, so each bond half (coloured by its own atom) interleaves correctly with the spheres in front and behind it; half-bonds that face the viewer start at the sphere surface
- Pre-rendered 256 px sphere sprites per element (radial gradient, bounce light, specular highlight) scaled by atomic radius, plus a cream depth-fog wash on far atoms
- Bonds as three-stroke shaded cylinders (dark base, body offset toward the light, soft highlight) with double bonds as two parallel cylinders offset in the plane of the neighbouring atoms
- Coordinates built by hand and by rule: water from 0.957 angstrom and 104.5 degrees, methane tetrahedron, ethanol, benzene hexagon with Kekule bonds, caffeine as a purine skeleton with slight z jitter and computed methyl hydrogens, and C60 computed from the cyclic permutations of the truncated icosahedron with nearest-neighbour bonds and double bonds on hexagon-hexagon edges found by a five-cycle test
- Trackball rotation as incremental screen-space matrices with inertia, idle auto-rotate, wheel and two-finger pinch zoom, and smooth animated blending between Ball and stick, Space-filling and Wireframe radii and bond widths
- Hover picking (nearest front-most atom) with a brass tooltip, bond highlight and selection ring with animated dashes; measurements drawn on the glass (bond lengths in pills, bond angles in blue)
- CSS cabinet: walnut grain from repeating gradients, brass gradient plates and corner fittings, lacquer grid interior with glass reflections, jar glass, cork lids and shelf edge, all in the specified cream, walnut, brass and glass palette
- Mini previews in each jar rendered with the same engine at 30 fps; DPR-capped, ResizeObserver-driven canvases

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Drag the display case to turn the molecule (it keeps inertia and, after a couple of seconds of stillness, resumes a gentle auto-rotate); scroll, pinch or use plus and minus to zoom, double-click to reset the view. Hover an atom to see its name and atomic number in a brass tooltip while its bonds light up; click an atom to select it (dashed brass ring) and read its bond lengths and angles on the glass and in the Measure panel, click empty space or press Escape to clear. The Style toggle (or keys 1, 2, 3) switches between Ball and stick, Space-filling and Wireframe with a smooth morph. Click a specimen jar (or press [ and ]) to bring that molecule into the case with a bouncy pop-in; hovering a jar lifts its cork lid. Arrow keys rotate the focused canvas. With prefers-reduced-motion the auto-rotate is nearly stopped and jar previews stop spinning.
