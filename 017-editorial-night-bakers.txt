Before the City Wakes

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A printed-magazine feature that breathes on screen: issue 07 of the fictional "Alba Quarterly", carrying a 759-word, second-by-second account of two night bakers, Marta and Tomás, working the hearth oven of Horno Ferrer on Calle Alba from 21:00 to 05:30. The page opens on a full-width Didone wordmark with a rotated ember "Issue 07" stamp, a double rule and a spaced-grotesque section strip, then a monumental headline, "Before the City wakes", set in roman and italic with the last word in ember and a large hand-drawn boule at its right that draws itself line by line, steam curling off it. Seven timed chapters follow (21:00 the key, 22:15 flour water salt, 23:30 the fire, 01:00 shaping, 02:30 the hearth, 04:00 the singing, 05:30 the city wakes), each opened by a giant italic ember clock time over a hairline, set in justified two-column text with column rules, a drop cap with a small-caps lead-in, soft hyphenation and hanging punctuation. Four engraved line illustrations (a bearded wheat stalk, a whisk and enamel bowl, a domed brick oven with logs, flames and peel, and an annotated top view of a loaf being scored) draw themselves as they scroll into view. Three giant pull-quotes, an "At a glance" strip, a wide "A loaf by numbers" data box with tiny animated bars, and a closing recipe card that folds open in three hinged panels complete the spread. A sticky "Timeline of a night" sidebar runs a live night clock that advances from 21:00 towards 06:00 as you read, a thin ember reading-progress line and a slim sticky bar give reading time left, faint flour dust drifts across the whole page, and a "Lamplight" switch turns the paper page into a warm ink-and-cream night edition.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Palette held to four tokens (paper #f5f0e6, ink #1a1714, ember #c8402a, flour #e9dfcb) plus tinted neutrals; a "lamplight" theme swaps the semantic variables (bg / fg / accent / soft) for a dark ink edition with a 0.6 s colour transition and localStorage persistence.
- Type system from system stacks only: Didone stack (Bodoni 72 / Bodoni MT / Didot / Playfair / Times) for the wordmark, headline, numerals and pull-quotes; old-style stack (Iowan / Palatino / Book Antiqua / P052) for text; Helvetica-style spaced capitals for labels; a chancery script stack for the pencilled recipe note. The masthead wordmark is JS-fitted to the column width on load and resize.
- Hero headline built from masked, staggered "rise" words (overflow-hidden lines, translateY reveal) with mixed roman/italic and an ember final word; the hero boule is an inline SVG with gradient crust, ember scoring crescents, "ear" lips, engraved hatching, flour specks and looping steam.
- Self-drawing illustrations: every stroke is a path with pathLength="1"; one CSS custom property (--d) written from scroll position (smoothed with exponential easing) drives stroke-dashoffset through calc()/clamp(), with per-path start offsets (--s) so strokes draw in sequence and fills fade in after; a feTurbulence + feDisplacementMap filter adds a subtle hand-inked wobble.
- Editorial typesetting: CSS multi-column with column rules that collapse by available width, justified text, ::first-letter drop cap plus small-caps lead-in, hand-placed soft hyphens with hyphens:auto, negative text-indent hanging punctuation (with a hanging-punctuation @supports override), text-wrap balance/pretty.
- Scroll-spy timeline: a sticky sidebar whose current item is highlighted, with an ember dot that glides between entries and a big serif "night clock" that interpolates real clock time (21:00 to about 06:00) from reading position; on narrow screens it becomes a sticky pill strip that auto-centres the active pill.
- Reading progress: a 3 px ember scaleX line, a sticky bar that slides in after the masthead, and a "N min left" counter computed from the actual word count of the prose.
- Flour dust: a fixed full-viewport canvas of ~50-120 pre-rendered soft sprites with depth-scaled drift, scroll-parallax, pointer repulsion and click "flour puffs"; frozen in reduced-motion mode.
- Paper grain: a 180 px speckle tile generated on a canvas at runtime and used as a fixed background overlay.
- "A loaf by numbers": semantic list rows with hairline-track bars (aria-labelled) that fill with staggered easing when the box enters view.
- Recipe card: three stacked panels; the lower two sit in height-animated wrappers with perspective and rotateX hinges plus a fold-shadow gradient, measured with JS so the card genuinely unfolds; auto-opens once when scrolled into view and can be folded away again.

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Scroll drives everything: the ember progress line grows across the top; a sticky bar appears after the masthead showing "N min left"; the sidebar timeline highlights the chapter under a reading line at 42% of the viewport, its clock ticks forward and its dot slides; each illustration draws itself progressively as its figure passes through the lower two thirds of the screen (and un-draws when scrolled back). Timeline entries are anchor links with smooth scrolling. Clicking or tapping empty page area releases a small puff of flour; moving the pointer gently pushes nearby dust motes aside. The "Lamplight" buttons (masthead and sticky bar, aria-pressed) toggle the dark night edition. The recipe card unfolds automatically once when it first scrolls into view and the "Fold open / Fold it away" button toggles it with mouse, touch, Enter or Space. Keyboard users get a skip link, visible ember focus outlines, and fully tabbable timeline and buttons. On phones the text goes to one column, the timeline becomes a sticky horizontally scrolling pill strip and the hero loaf drops below the headline. With prefers-reduced-motion the intro reveals, steam, dust drift, fold animation, smooth scrolling and bar transitions are removed and all illustrations appear fully drawn, so the page is complete without motion.
