==================== TITLE ====================
Glass Harp

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
Fourteen to seventeen translucent glass rods stand in a gentle arc on a teal-black seabed, taller rods for lower notes, each with a pearl on top whose colour drifts from aqua through pearl white and rose to gold across the row. Rods are drawn as bending tubes with a gradient body, an inner glow that rises toward the pearl, refraction streaks, a soft reflection in the floor and a key hint underneath. Behind them, slow caustic networks (computed per pixel at low resolution and upscaled) glide like aurora ribbons, light bands sweep the water and plankton drifts. Plucking makes a rod sway with a decaying oscillation, blaze, throw elliptical ripple rings and release rising bubbles, while a WebAudio glass tone (four inharmonic sine partials, detuned chorus on the fundamental, long exponential decay) sounds through a generated-impulse reverb and a feedback delay. A generative player, on by default, wanders a Markov walk over the scale with rests and occasional chord tones, so the page plays itself; a scale selector retunes the rods and their heights, and delay and reverb knobs shape the space.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Canvas 2D scene with gradient glass tubes drawn as segmented polygons displaced by a cantilever-shaped sinusoid, additive inner glow, refraction streak lines, radial-gradient pearls and mirrored low-alpha reflections
- Low-resolution per-pixel caustics (sums of warped sines sharpened by a power curve) drawn every few frames and smoothly upscaled with additive blending, plus drifting light bands and twinkling plankton in three tints
- Segment-crossing detection for pointer plucks (fast sweeps strum in order with tiny time offsets), pointer speed mapped to velocity, Pointer Events with per-pointer state for multi-touch chords
- WebAudio synth: partial ratios 1, 2.76, 5.4, 8.93 with individual decays, detuned pair for chorus, stereo pan by pitch, master gain 0.22 into a compressor, voice cap
- Generated stereo impulse response (filtered decaying noise) into a ConvolverNode, low-passed feedback delay with a knob-controlled amount
- Four scales (pentatonic, Dorian, whole-tone, Hirajoshi) mapping rod index to semitones; rod heights ease to new pitch-dependent targets when retuned
- Markov-walk generative player with rests and chord tones that yields for a few seconds after manual play
- Custom accessible rotary knobs (role=slider, drag and arrow keys), segmented radio scale selector, keyboard mapping A S D F G H J K L ; ' then Q W E R T Y U with visible hints
- Audio created only on the first pointer down or key press; canvas resizes with devicePixelRatio capped at 2

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Move a mouse across the rods to pluck them; drag with a finger or mouse for the same, use several fingers for chords. Faster movement plays louder. Keys A S D F G H J K L ; ' play the first eleven rods, Q W E R T Y U the rest, and each rod shows its key. The first pointer down or key press wakes the audio (a gold note at top right says so); before that the rods still ripple and the generative player animates silently. The "Play for me" button toggles the generative melody, which politely pauses for a few seconds whenever you play. The scale selector retunes the harp, and the Delay and Reverb knobs respond to dragging up or down or the arrow keys. With prefers-reduced-motion the caustics, plankton, rod sway and generative tempo slow down.
