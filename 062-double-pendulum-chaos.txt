==================== TITLE ====================
Sensitive Dependence

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A dark, warm observatory for the butterfly effect. Two hundred and forty exact double pendulums are released a hair's breadth apart (a spread of 1e-4 rad by default) and their outer bobs draw glowing additive threads in a plum, magenta, orange and gold gradient by index: first one narrow bright thread, then a fan, then a bloom that fills the whole reach circle. A small brass pivot with a hanger, three faint arms and countdown numerals in giant italic Didone frame the stage, and a Didone "Sensitive Dependence" title, mono readouts and italic captions sit around it. Along the bottom a log-scale chart plots the mean gap between every outer bob and pendulum 0 in real time; a least-squares fit of the exponential stretch is annotated with a brass e-fold triangle and a "Lyapunov time ≈ x s" label. Sliders, a countdown Restart, Trails, Slow motion, skip-ahead and Save controls sit beside it. The page opens by showing the finished bloom, rewinds, counts down and runs the experiment for real.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Exact double-pendulum equations (unit masses and lengths) integrated with RK4 at a fixed dt = 0.004 s in Float64Array state, with an accumulator and a clamped frame dt so tab switches never explode the simulation
- Persistent trail canvas: additive ('lighter') thin strokes drawn per frame from recorded sub-step positions, faded with a source-over black fill plus a colour-burn pass that clears dark residue; screen-blended over the warm backdrop
- Draw-call batching: 240 pendulums drawn as ~30 colour-bucket paths per pass (glow pass and core pass), heads batched the same way; canvases sized to the 2L reach square and CPU-backed (willReadFrequently) for speed
- Curated four-stop colour ramp by pendulum index, with dark plum strands lifted so they stay visible on additive black
- Live divergence chart on canvas: log10 axis over 8 decades, hairline grid, gradient curve with soft area, hover read-out, and a least-squares Lyapunov fit annotated as a brass e-fold triangle (one Lyapunov time wide, one e-fold tall)
- Two-link inverse kinematics turns the pointer position into (θ1, θ2); a faint ghost pose with an angle read-out follows the pointer and fades when it rests
- Captions driven by the measured gap rather than the clock (waiting, one thread, still one thread, opening, fully unfolded)
- Adaptive time-lapse while the gap is still invisible (same physics, eased back to real time), a skip-ahead button that repaints the last stretch of trails, and Save-as-PNG through a blob download
- Countdown numerals, arming ring, release flash, pivot glow, film grain and vignette layers; fully keyboard operable with shortcuts and aria-pressed toggles

==================== INTERACTION ====================
On load the finished bloom is shown for a few seconds, then it rewinds and a 3-2-1 countdown arms the fan; at zero the pendulums drop. Click or tap anywhere on the stage to place the outer bob there (the two arms are solved by inverse kinematics) and relaunch with a one-second countdown; on desktop a ghost pose and its angles follow the pointer. Drag the "Initial angle difference" slider (1e-6 to 1e-2 rad, logarithmic) or the "Pendulums" slider (24 to 360); either restarts the run. Restart, Trails (persistent long exposure or short comets), Slow motion (quarter speed), +5 s (skip ahead) and Save (PNG) are buttons; keyboard shortcuts are R, T, S, F and Space (pause). Hover the chart for exact gap values. With prefers-reduced-motion the intro rewind flash and countdown animation are dropped, the simulation starts in slow motion and the time-lapse is disabled.
