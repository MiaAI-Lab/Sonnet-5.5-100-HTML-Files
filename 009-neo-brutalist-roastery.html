      04 — House rules

      The rules. They’re laws.

      1. No decaf.

        We looked into it. It’s not a personality, it’s a rumour.

      2. Milk is a condiment.

        Not a food group. Not a hug. A condiment. Pour like you mean it.

      3. Grind it fresh.

        Pre-ground coffee is a cry for help. Buy the grinder. Buy the good one.

      4. Stop at the crack.

        First crack is a pop. Second crack is a warning. Third crack is your smoke alarm.

      5. It’s not bitter. It’s you.

        Or your water. Or your grind. Mostly your grind. Try again, gently.
