==================== TITLE ====================
Circles that Draw

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A slate-green chalkboard in a grained wooden frame with a wooden chalk tray below. Visitors draw any closed or open shape in chalk; on release the path is resampled to 256 complex points, transformed with a discrete Fourier transform, and the coefficients are sorted by amplitude to drive a chain of thin chalk-white rotating circles with radius lines. The chain's tip redraws the shape as a glowing, slowly fading chalk trail in the selected chalk colour, shedding falling dust. A circles slider (with a live "k = 41 circles" annotation and a "percent of the shape captured" figure) shows the approximation improving, and a new shape auto-ramps from one circle upward. Six authored presets (heart, treble-clef curve, spiral, π outline, star, Signature) are provided. Handwritten italic cursive annotations, a hand-lettered title and a ghost of old chalk writing complete the classroom feel.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Discrete Fourier transform of the resampled path computed once per shape; terms sorted by amplitude, chain of at most 120 circles (hard cap 200) drawn per frame
- Board texture pre-rendered to an offscreen canvas: radial slate gradient, wide faint eraser sweeps, chalk smudge blobs, tiled noise grain, ghosted old equations
- Chalk stroke rendering with a jittered second pass for a grainy edge; glowing trail drawn in ten age buckets with widening, brightening strokes plus a soft halo
- Catmull-Rom interpolation of hand-authored control points for the clef and Signature presets; parametric heart, star polygon, double spiral and π outline
- Wooden frame and tray from layered CSS gradients with grain lines; chalk sticks as CSS gradients with pressed/selected states
- Falling chalk dust particles from both the drawing chalk and the tracing tip
- Opt-in WebAudio chalk tick (filtered noise burst plus a tiny sine) at every completed loop, quiet gain
- Pointer Events with pointer capture, touch-action none, ResizeObserver, DPR capped at 2

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Drag on the board with mouse, pen or finger to draw; release to start the epicycles (a plain click keeps the current shape). Presets in the top-right reload the built-in shapes. The Circles slider (or Arrow Up/Down, +/-) changes how many circles are used; Speed changes the rotation rate; Circles and Fade trail toggles show or hide the wheels and choose whether the trail fades or persists; the four chalk sticks choose the trail colour; Sound enables the loop tick; Erase clears the board. With prefers-reduced-motion the animation runs slower, dust is removed and the circle count starts at a detailed value instead of ramping.
