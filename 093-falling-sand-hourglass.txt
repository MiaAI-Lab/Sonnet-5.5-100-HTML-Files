==================== TITLE ====================
Sandglass · 3 min

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A dusk-grey stage lit by a warm glow holds a tall hourglass built from a 160x280 cellular sand simulation. Six strata of terracotta, ochre, sand, sage, indigo and rose sit in the upper bulb and drip through a throttled neck (calibrated to roughly three minutes) into a stratified conical pile below, avalanching as it grows. The glass itself is drawn in the canvas pixels (bright rims, tinted interior, point-symmetric glints) and framed in DOM by walnut caps with brass studs and two turned brass pillars. Engraved small-caps title, a big elapsed clock, a grain gauge, poetic status lines, and a compact control panel with tools, sand swatches, flip, refill, rustle and a tilt slider complete the piece.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Uint8Array cellular automaton (0 air, 1 wall, 2+ colour*4+tone), bottom-up scan with alternating left/right direction and a per-step moved buffer, fixed 120 Hz sub-steps with clamped dt
- Glass mask generated from an analytic profile (sine funnel plus elliptical domes), walls thickened by neighbouring-row min/max so no grain squeezes through diagonals
- Throttled neck row whose gate probability is auto-calibrated from the grain count so the top bulb empties in about three minutes
- ImageData written through a Uint32Array view, scaled crisply with image-rendering: pixelated; sand top edges get a lighter highlight variant
- Precomputed point-symmetric background, glass rim and interior shading so the 180-degree flip swaps invisibly
- Flip via the Web Animations API (rotate plus a mid-flight scale) with the cell arrays reversed in place at the end of the rotation, then the animation is cancelled in the same task
- Poke tool carves a tunnel through the sloped wall so sand leaks and piles up outside on the glass shoulder and floor
- Gravity tilt: sideways slide probability and diagonal bias follow a smoothed tilt angle, sign-flipped by orientation; the whole piece rotates visually
- CSS-only walnut caps (repeating grain lines), brass pillars with collars, engraved text-shadows, inline SVG grain
- Opt-in WebAudio rustle: looped filtered noise whose gain follows grain movement, plus tiny tick sounds

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Pour tool (default): press and drag inside the glass to pour a stream of the selected sand (six colours or the cycling strata swatch). Poke tool (button or P): click the bright glass wall to open a leak. Flip: click the wooden caps or pillars, press F, or use the Flip button; the piece rotates 180 degrees and the simulation orientation swaps at the end. Refill (R) restores fresh strata and mends the glass. Tilt: drag the dusk beside the glass horizontally (springs back on release), hold the left/right arrow keys, use the Tilt slider (double-click to reset), or tilt a phone (device orientation). Keys 1-7 choose sand. The Rustle button enables the filtered-noise sound (off by default, starts only after a gesture). Reduced motion: shorter flip, 60 Hz sim steps. The animation pauses while the tab is hidden.
