==================== TITLE ====================
The Descent

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A 640vh scrollytelling dive in which a sticky full-viewport canvas carries the visitor from a sunlit sea surface (sky strip, sun, glitter, god rays, a submersible just under the waves) down through five colour zones to a trench floor with glowing hydrothermal chimneys. Nine hand-drawn, procedurally animated creatures appear at their depths (green sea turtle, bluefin school, crown jellyfish with a rotating blue alarm ring, giant-squid silhouette, lanternfish with photophores, anglerfish with a face-lighting lure, gulper eel with a pink tail light, tripod fish on a rock ledge, hadal snailfish with amphipods), each with an annotated fact card carrying pressure, temperature and sunlight figures. A glass instrument panel shows depth, zone, pressure, light and a temperature profile with a moving dot; a ruler with a floating marker runs down the left edge. A light-sans title, mono numerals and a quiet closing line ("You are at the bottom.") with a "Scroll up to rise" button finish the piece.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Sticky 640vh track; scroll progress smoothed with frame-rate-independent lerp, mapped to depth by a monotone cubic (Fritsch-Carlson) curve so each creature gets screen time and the counter accelerates
- Single 2D canvas: depth-keyed water gradient (per-channel monotone spline through the brief's colours), sky and wavy surface, fanned god rays whose apex recedes with depth, drifting sun glints
- Lamp cone rendered from a per-pixel generated cone sprite (smooth angular falloff, hot core) that widens and lengthens as ambient light dies; creatures are lit by an analytic lamp-falloff function, ambient depth and sonar pings
- Procedural swimmer skeletons (travelling-wave spines, half-width profiles, tapered ribbons) for tuna, lanternfish, tripod fish, snailfish, eel; bezier-built turtle, jellyfish, squid and anglerfish
- Additive glow sprites for photophores, lures, bioluminescent plankton and vent embers
- Marine snow in two parallax layers that drifts and streaks proportionally to smoothed scroll velocity
- Zone threshold hairlines that travel with the world, fact cards positioned beside each creature (with side/below fallbacks and leader dots)
- Instruments: inline SVG ruler generated from the depth curve, temperature sparkline, DOM writes only when values change
- Resolution cap on the canvas backing store, vignette drawn in-canvas, no backdrop filters (cheap on software renderers)

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Scroll (wheel, touch, keyboard) is the main control: it drives depth, colour, creatures, cards and readouts, and scrolling back up rises again. Moving the pointer swings the submersible's lamp toward it; clicking, tapping or pressing P sends a sonar ping that lights nearby creatures. The "Scroll up to rise" button smooth-scrolls back to the surface. On phones the HUD becomes a compact top bar and fact cards dock at the bottom. With prefers-reduced-motion, ambient motion is slowed, bubbles and flicker are removed and the scroll smoothing is snappier, while the piece remains complete.
