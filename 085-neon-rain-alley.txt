==================== TITLE ====================
Ramen Alley 2049

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A rainy cyberpunk noodle alley seen from street level. Two real CSS-3D walls (perspective planes) converge on a glowing vanishing point; their facades are canvas-painted textures of lit windows, and they carry neon blade signs (RAMEN, NOODLE PARADISE, ZEN CYBER-CLINIC, PACHINKO, 24H, SAKE BAR, DUMPLING, KARAOKE, HOTEL ORCHID) built as multi-layer text-shadow tubes, one of them broken and buzzing. Cross-alley catenary cables carry swaying lanterns; behind it all stand a hazy two-layer skyline with blinking aircraft lights, animated gradient holo-ads, a drone and passing flying cars. Below the horizon the whole stage is cloned, flipped, blurred and displaced into a wet mirror street with puddle sheen, splash rings, umbrella-carrying silhouettes with glowing umbrellas and a warm noodle stall with steam. A tall condensed neon title with a tri-colour underline hangs over the sky. Palette: teal, orange, magenta, violet on blue-black.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- True CSS 3D: perspective container with two rotateY wall planes, moving perspective-origin for real parallax
- Facade textures pre-rendered on canvas (windows, pipes, AC units) and applied as data-URL backgrounds; depth fog gradient along each wall plane
- Blade neon signs rotated perpendicular to the walls; multi-layer text-shadow and box-shadow tubes, flicker/ignite keyframes, broken sign with per-letter dropouts
- Cross-alley 3D planes carrying SVG catenary cables and swaying lanterns
- Skyline layers pre-rendered from a seeded layout to canvas, with DOM aircraft blinkers and animated gradient holo-ads with scanlines
- Wet street: the entire stage DOM cloned, flipped with scaleY(-1), masked, blurred and displaced by an SVG feTurbulence/feDisplacementMap ripple filter whose frequency drifts slowly
- Single full-screen canvas for depth-bucketed rain streaks, splash rings (ellipses) and sprite-based steam particles from wall vents and the stall
- SVG umbrella walkers with leg swing and reflections; hand-drawn noodle stall with noren curtains and bulbs
- Pointer-driven flashlight glow (radial gradient with screen blend), grain, vignette, occasional lightning flash
- Opt-in WebAudio: filtered noise rain, low hum, sign buzz, zaps and thunder

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Move the mouse or drag a finger to shift the camera (parallax through the 3D perspective origin) and sweep a warm flashlight glow; without input the camera drifts slowly by itself. Click or tap any neon sign (they are focusable buttons, Enter/Space works) to switch it off or relight it with a flicker. The Rain slider changes drop density, splash rings, the readout and the rain audio level. "Lights out" kills every sign, lantern, holo-ad and the title, then restores them one by one in sequence with ignition flicker. "Sound" starts rain noise, a distant hum, buzzing and thunder after a user gesture at a modest volume. Reduced-motion: idle camera drift, sign flicker and the ripple animation are off, rain is thinned and other loops slowed. Animation pauses when the tab is hidden.
