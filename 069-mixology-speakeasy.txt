==================== TITLE ====================
The Copper Door

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A moody 1926 speakeasy in a single page: a deep-teal damask wall, three copper pendant lamps throwing amber cones of light (with drifting dust motes), a dim back-bar of bottles standing on a brass-edged picket rail, and a polished wooden bar with a brass foot rail. On the bar stands a large SVG coupe glass that the visitor fills in four steps (Spirit, Modifier, Top, Garnish) from illustrated bottles on a little wooden shelf. Every choice runs a real pour: a bottle swings in from the upper right and tilts, a stream arcs into the glass, and the liquid climbs as separate density-sorted layers (honey sinks, spirits float, mixers sit between) with soft gradient boundaries, so the drink is a pousse-cafe until it is shaken. Choosing a fizzy top strains the drink into a highball, drops in four bobbing ice cubes with splashes and sends bubbles rising; a top of "none" gets a brass bar spoon stir instead. Garnishes (lemon twist, olives on a pick, cherries on a pick, rosemary, orange wheel) plop in with a splash and settle on the rim. A name generator titles the drink ("The Velvet Alibi", "Midnight Ledger", "Smoked Getaway & Tonic"), a parchment recipe card types itself out with proportions, method, a witty line, an ABV estimate and animated sweet / sour / bitter / strong bars, and a hand-lettered chalkboard lists tonight's specials that build themselves on click. "Shake it" jitters the glass, blends the layers into one colour and frosts the glass with sliding condensation droplets. The house pours the first drink on arrival.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Inline SVG scene with a hand-authored coupe and highball glass; interior volume tables computed from the glass curve (Bezier sampling and pi*r^2 integration) so liquid heights are physically proportional
- One dynamic linearGradient built per frame from density-sorted liquid layers, with a "mix" parameter that softens layer boundaries into a blend (pousse-cafe to shaken)
- Parabolic pour streams (gravity, launch angle, wobble for viscous honey, drip mode for citrus) with splash droplets, surface ripples and a vessel that swings and tilts about its mouth
- Reusable bottle, jar, garnish and lamp art defined once in <defs> and instanced with <use>; CSS custom properties (--cap-o, --extra-o) hide caps and props inside the shadow tree while pouring
- Queue-based action engine (drain, swap glass, ice drop, pour, stir, garnish, shake, serve) with cancel and rebuild-from-step, so changing an earlier choice re-pours cleanly
- Ice cubes with float/rest physics, rising bubbles, frost via feTurbulence + colour matrix, and sliding condensation droplets with trails
- Name generator from spirit x modifier x top x garnish tables; recipe card typing engine; ABV and flavour-vector maths
- Damask wallpaper from an SVG <pattern>; grain overlay from feTurbulence; conic-gradient light cones; canvas dust motes lit by the lamp cones
- Chalkboard with displacement-filtered chalk lettering and a hand-drawn underline (stroke-dashoffset)
- Radio-group fieldsets with real inputs, aria-live announcements, aria-current steps, visible focus rings; responsive desktop grid and a sticky-stage mobile layout

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Pick a spirit, modifier, top and garnish from the shelf (mouse, touch, or arrow keys inside each radio group); the shelf restocks for each step, pointer picks auto-advance, and the step chips on top let you jump back at any time. Each pick pours immediately; changing an earlier step drains and re-pours the later steps quickly. Hover or focus a bottle to read its tasting note. Buttons: Shake it (jitters and frosts the glass, blends the layers, rewrites the recipe method), Surprise me (the barman builds a random drink), Clear glass, Back / Next / Serve it (a flourish of sparks and a glowing name plate). Tonight's Specials on the chalkboard build a whole drink with one click. Click the little copper door in the header to knock (the peephole slides open); typing the password "swordfish" anywhere unlocks the secret house special. With no interaction, the house pours the first drink after a moment. Reduced motion: pours run faster, the shake jitter, lamp flicker, dust motes, chalk-draw and the typing animation are removed or made instant, while every state still renders fully.
