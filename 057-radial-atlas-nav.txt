==================== TITLE ====================
Hotel Meridiana: Radial Atlas

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A single-screen boutique-hotel site whose only navigation is a brass compass at the bottom centre of a warm espresso lobby. The lobby is an engraved sundial: hairline rings, tick marks, Roman hour numerals and a soft sun beam that follows the pointer (with a live "sundial time" readout) sit behind a gold-foil "Meridiana" wordmark. Touch the compass and five large coloured discs (Rooms in terracotta, Rooftop in deep blue and gold, Spa in sage and white, Table in saffron and plum, Journal in paper and ink) spring out into a semicircular fan, each with an inline-SVG icon and a hanging tag that swings like a pendulum as the wheel turns. The fan can be dragged around its pivot like a wheel with inertia and detent snapping; a big serif name and a line of copy appear inside the arc for the focused item. Choosing a disc expands a full-screen circular clip-path from the compass, chased by a thin brass rim, into that room's own colour world: an arched-window carousel of three illustrated rooms on terracotta; a starlit rooftop with a shooting star, crescent moon, dome-and-campanile skyline, lit windows and a shimmering string of bar lights; a sage spa with a slowly breathing white orb and concentric ripples; a saffron table with a plum disc, a steaming plate of tagliolini and a four-line menu; and a paper-and-ink journal with a newspaper masthead and three unfolding article cards. In a room the compass turns into a back arrow, its ring text reads "YOU ARE HERE" with the room name, and a bezel arc rotates to that room's slot. On phones the fan becomes a tall elliptical arch of vertical arcs with larger discs.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Spring physics written by hand: per-item damped springs (staggered) unfold the fan with overshoot; labels start rotated at about -110 degrees and swing into place, then hang like pendulums driven by wheel velocity.
- Wheel model on a wrapped ring of five slots: angular drag measured in the ellipse's parametric angle (so it works on the circular desktop fan and the elliptical mobile arch), velocity tracking, projected inertia landing on the nearest detent with a critically-tuned spring, wrap-around with edge fade, mouse-wheel and arrow-key stepping.
- Full-screen circular reveal with a pure CSS transition on clip-path: circle(r at button) using CSS variables for the pivot, plus a synchronised rim ring animated by width/height with the same easing; content rises in after the circle passes; reduced-motion swaps it for a crossfade.
- Engraved sundial drawn in one inline SVG: concentric hairline circles, degree ticks made with stroke-dasharray, 13 hour rays, Roman numerals; a compositor-only rotating sun beam (masked, clipped wedge) that follows the pointer and drifts on its own when idle, with a HH:MM readout.
- Compass instrument: SVG rose, spring-driven needle that swings toward hovered items and lags the wheel, a rotating text-on-path ring (textPath with textLength) that changes to "YOU ARE HERE / room name", a bezel arc that rotates to the room's slot, and a needle-to-back-arrow morph; colours adapt per room through CSS variables.
- Five distinct colour worlds built from CSS gradients and hand-drawn inline SVG: arched windows with three scenes, canvas starfield with twinkle and shooting star, a runtime-built skyline (buildings, deterministic lit windows, bar-light bulbs on Bezier strings), breathing orb with CSS-timed inhale/exhale text, ripple rings and click ripples, a plate illustration with blurred flowing steam paths, and a paper masthead with self-drawing ink spot illustrations (pathLength dash animation).
- Deep links and history: pushState per room with popstate handling so the browser back button closes a room; number keys 1-5 jump between rooms.
- Ambient details: film grain painted once to a canvas and reused as a data URL, glow layers that tint the lobby to the focused item, gentle orbit drift on every disc, double-click the compass for an easter egg.
- Accessibility and robustness: real buttons in a nav list, roving focus that follows the wheel, visible focus rings, aria live announcements, skip link, prefers-reduced-motion path, per-frame work kept to a handful of transforms, delta-time clamped, loops paused when the tab is hidden.

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Desktop: the fan introduces itself about a second after load. Click the compass (or press Enter, or any arrow key from the empty lobby) to open or close it. Drag anywhere on the fan to spin the wheel: it coasts and settles on a detent, and the focused item is named inside the arc. Click or tap any disc, or press Enter on the focused one, to open that room; hovering a disc points the compass needle at it. Mouse wheel, Left/Right/Up/Down and Tab step through items; the digit keys 1-5 jump straight to a room, even from inside another room. Esc closes the fan or leaves the current room. Moving the pointer across the lobby drags the sun beam and changes the sundial time. Inside a room, the compass is the back button (its ring reads "YOU ARE HERE"); Esc and the browser back button do the same, and the fan reopens on the room you left. Rooms: click or swipe the arched windows, use the arrow buttons or Left/Right keys, and press "Hold this room" for a toast; Rooftop has a shooting star every few seconds; Spa reacts to pointer parallax and every click drops a ripple; Table steam surges when you click or press Enter on the plate; Journal cards unfold with "Read the note". Touch: the same gestures work with pointer events; on phones the fan is a full-height elliptical arch and the hero title stays visible above it. Deep links such as #rooftop open a room instantly. With prefers-reduced-motion the orbit, label swing, springs' overshoot, sun drift, steam, ripples, breathing and the circular reveal are removed or replaced by short fades while every layout stays complete.
