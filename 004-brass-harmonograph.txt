==================== TITLE ====================
Patent Harmonic Recorder No. 7

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A Victorian brass harmonograph on a dark walnut board, lit as if by a gas lamp. At the centre a cream cotton-paper disc, mottled and fibrous, with a printed watermark round its rim and a centring cross, sits inside a knurled brass bezel engraved with degrees every five, numerals every thirty and four slotted screws. A telescoping brass boom hinged on a post beside the bezel carries a small pen: it extends onto the paper, draws a curve built from four damped pendulums (x = A1 sin(f1 t + p1) e^(−d1 t) + A2 sin(f2 t + p2) e^(−d2 t), y likewise) over 32 seconds with pressure that thickens the ink where the pen slows, a hairline of hand tremor and faint bleed, in sepia, oxblood or Prussian blue, then withdraws to the bezel to reveal the finished figure. Left: "The Governor", four knurled brass knobs (Ratio, Damping, Phase, Turntable) with engraved scales, a brass rocker switch for the turntable, and a glass-fronted gallery of four brass pendulums that swing exactly as the four components do and slowly come to rest. Right: a riveted brass plaque, "Patent Harmonic Recorder No. 7", with a live small-caps ledger of ratio, detune, damping, phase, turntable and vertices; three presets with tiny drawn thumbnails (Rose, Lissajous Knot, Spider); and three glass ink bottles with paper labels. Below the disc, a slotted brass meter counts the amplitude spent ("Fig. II · complete") and a brass "Set another" button pulses when the sheet is done. Type is old-style serif with small caps and old-style numerals, with engraved highlights on brass and gilt lettering on walnut.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Harmonograph maths in JavaScript: four damped sinusoids with musical ratios 2:3, 3:4 and 4:5 (each knob zone locks to the exact ratio, with a fine detune of up to plus or minus one percent between and within pendulum pairs), a smooth start ramp for turntable drawings, and normalisation so every figure fills 86 percent of the paper
- 24 000-vertex paths precomputed into typed arrays in both the pen's frame and the paper's frame; the turntable is physically part of the model, the pen is fixed in the screen frame and the ink is laid in the paper frame, which yields rosettes, roses and spider bursts
- Layered canvases inside a rotating paper disc: a generated cotton texture (mottling, fibres, rim shading, watermark), a pencil preview of the whole curve, the progressive ink layer (multiply blend) and a speckle grain overlay (multiply)
- Ink rendering with speed-driven pressure: line width follows the pen's slowness, faint wider bleed strokes, occasional pooled blots at turning points, a tiny high-frequency hand tremor baked into the path
- Brass built from CSS: conic-gradient metal whose start angle follows the pointer, masked ring bezel, knurled knobs from repeating-conic-gradient masks, inset and outer shadows, riveted plaque, engraved text via text-shadow highlights
- Pen mechanism as HTML parts moved only with transforms (boom scaled by distance, sleeve, post, nib with barrel, tip and ink dot, shadows that slide away as the pen lifts) so no SVG or blur is repainted per frame; the boom extends, draws, then parks on the bezel
- Draggable rotary knobs with pointer capture and relative angle tracking, mouse wheel, keyboard (arrows, Page keys, Home, End), full slider ARIA with descriptive value text; knobs also turn themselves with eased tweens when a preset or Set another is chosen
- Live parameter changes re-pencil the new figure at once as a faint sketch, then ink it after a short idle
- A pendulum gallery in SVG driven by the same equations and decay as the drawing; procedural walnut grain with two SVG feTurbulence layers; three hand-drawn glass ink bottles with labels and a raised, glowing selected state
- Random-but-beautiful generator for Set another (zone, detune, damping, phase, harmonic character, turntable), with parameters validated offline against hundreds of samples; reduced-motion mode completes the sheet instantly with a static turntable angle

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Mouse or touch: drag round any brass knob like a real dial (or use the wheel over it); the settings change live on the plaque ledger and a faint pencil sketch of the new figure appears on the paper, and about half a second after you let go the boom extends and inks it from scratch. Keyboard: Tab to a knob, then arrow keys, Page Up and Page Down, Home and End adjust it; the ink bottles form a radio group with arrow-key navigation; every control has a visible focus ring. The Turntable switch engages or stills the paper (the paper then spins under the fixed pen, or not, and once the sheet is finished the paper keeps turning slowly like a display stand). Click a preset (Rose, Lissajous Knot, Spider) and the knobs whirr to their positions by themselves and the figure starts. Click an ink bottle to re-ink the current drawing instantly and all later ones. "Set another" chooses a fresh musical ratio, detune, damping, phase, turntable and ink at random and starts a new figure; the button glows softly once the pendulums have decayed and the sheet is complete, and the meter and screen-reader status announce the figure number. Moving the pointer changes where the light catches the brass. With prefers-reduced-motion the sheet is drawn instantly and shown at its final turntable angle, the paper stays still, the pen does not tremble and the button does not pulse; all controls still work.
