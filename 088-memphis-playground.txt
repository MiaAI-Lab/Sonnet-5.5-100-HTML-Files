==================== TITLE ====================
Memphis Playground

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A teal, dotted desk with a slightly tilted 4:5 poster (an inline SVG) beside a chunky white tool panel with hard black offset shadows. The poster holds a huge white headline (default HELLO WORLD, auto-split over two lines) with a nine-layer stacked extrusion in pink, yellow, teal and blue, black outlines and a wavy pink underline, surrounded by ten kinds of Memphis shapes: squiggles, zigzags, triangles, half circles, dot grids, checker tiles, bold arcs, lightning bolts, plus signs and terrazzo confetti. The starting composition is art-directed rather than random: four large anchor shapes sit in the corners, the rest are placed by rejection sampling that keeps clear of the headline and of each other, with some shapes tucked behind the headline and others in front. Everything drops in on springs. The panel offers a headline field, add-shape buttons with live icons, background pattern chips, background colour swatches, Shuffle, Save PNG, Delete and Clear.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Inline SVG poster where every shape is generated by hand-written markup functions using presentation attributes only, so a serialised copy renders faithfully to PNG
- Layered SVG text extrusion (nine offset copies with cycling colours, deepest layer outlined) plus a generated wavy underline path
- Per-item spring physics (position, rotation, scale and a squash "pop" spring) integrated in sub-steps with underdamped constants for overshoot
- Pointer Events drag with pointer capture, z-raise on pick-up, squash and stretch, custom double-tap detection for touch
- Rotation handle on a selection overlay (dashed box, stick and round handle, angle snapping to 15 degrees), wheel rotation, keyboard nudge, spin, resize, recolour and delete
- Composition engine: anchor corners, rejection sampling around the headline zone, palette rules, random z-interleaving, staggered spring shuffle
- SVG pattern fills for dots, stripes, checker and a hand-authored terrazzo tile, plus five background colours
- Save PNG: clone the SVG, serialise, draw to a 1600 by 2000 canvas via a blob image and download
- Hard black offset shadows, bouncy cubic-bezier button transitions and a star-shaped data-URI cursor

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Drag any shape or the headline; the item raises, squashes, and settles with a bounce. Drag the round yellow handle above a selected item, or use the mouse wheel, to rotate it (Shift or Ctrl with the wheel resizes). Double-click (or double-tap) an item to cycle its colour. Delete or Backspace removes the selected shape; arrow keys nudge (Shift for larger steps), [ and ] rotate, plus and minus resize, C recolours; Tab moves between items. Type in the Headline box to change the word live. Add-shape buttons drop a new shape onto the poster; Shuffle animates everything into a fresh composition (topping up to at least fifteen shapes); pattern chips and colour swatches restyle the background; Save PNG downloads the poster; Clear the board removes all shapes. Reduced motion: the first composition appears in place and hover wiggles are disabled (springs are still used for direct manipulation).
