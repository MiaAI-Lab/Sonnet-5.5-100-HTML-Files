==================== TITLE ====================
MONOLITH: Concrete & Light

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A stark, monumental single-page site for a fictional brutalist architecture atelier. Five board-formed concrete slabs, each with its own tone, tie-hole grid and caption, first form a closed wall behind a "curing" counter, then lift like shutters (staggered, on a heavy ease) to reveal a giant condensed MONOLITH wordmark that stays partly masked by the hanging slabs and keeps sliding with scroll. Below it: an oversized index of eight built works whose rows expand into self-drawing, seeded generative elevation drawings (dimension strings, ghosted neighbours, a scale figure); a dark sun-angle study where a round concrete plinth carries a rotated podium, tower, cantilever and blade wall under a real solar-geometry sun (equinox, 47° N) that travels an orange sun-path arc, throwing convex-hull shadow polygons that lengthen and swing like a sundial hand while sky, walls and lit slots shift from dawn to night; a philosophy statement revealed word by word from behind slabs; a concrete numbers strip (41 built, 9 countries, 0 apologies); a thin-line architectural section that draws itself, then is "cut" by a sweeping orange plane that reveals concrete hatch, earth hatch and finally a blade of light through the roof slot; and a dark contact block with a "N°42" slab. An Exposure toggle inverts the entire page into a blue-and-white blueprint, including the sun study. Palette: concrete greys, signal orange, warm white. Type: heavy condensed grotesque with mono labels.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Board-formed concrete built from stacked repeating-linear-gradient board seams of co-prime periods plus an SVG feTurbulence tile (mottling and pores) rendered once to a small data-URI and multiplied over each slab; tie holes are a radial-gradient dot grid.
- Giant wordmark drawn as SVG text with textLength/lengthAdjust so the condensed letters always fill the width whatever font is installed; separate two-line MONO / LITH version on phones.
- Slabs slide with the individual CSS translate property (opening, staggered, cubic-bezier(.76,0,.18,1)) while JS drives a separate transform for scroll-linked parallax; a curing counter precedes the opening.
- Crosshair cursor made of two hairlines, a ring and a coordinate readout that also names the section under the pointer (fine pointers only).
- Exposure mode: CSS custom properties re-map the whole palette to blueprint blue with a drafting grid, a photographic flash overlay plays on toggle, and the JS-drawn sun study re-maps its colours through a luminance-to-blue ramp.
- Index rows expand with grid-template-rows 0fr to 1fr; each elevation is generated on first open from a mulberry32 seeded PRNG in eight architectural styles (slot, bridge, stepped, grid, vault, cantilever, sawtooth, cluster) and draws itself via pathLength=1 stroke-dashoffset animation.
- Sun study: orthographic axonometric projection, equinox sun position at latitude 47° N, Lambert shading with sky-ambient and sun-colour terms, convex-hull (monotone chain) shadow polygons of every prism onto the disc and onto the podium roof (SVG clipPaths), shadow-tip sundial curve with hour dots, sun-path arc with hour ticks, stars, moon, glowing lit slots at night.
- Section drawing: one evenodd poché path with voids and windows, concrete and earth hatch patterns revealed through a growing clipPath rect that follows a sweeping orange cut line; light rays and labels fade in last.
- Word-by-word sliding reveals for the statement (each word masked by its own overflow box), IntersectionObserver-triggered counters (the "0 apologies" counts down from 99).
- Fully responsive down to 360px with reflowed grids, a cropped mobile viewBox for the sun study and a scrollable section drawing; reduced-motion shows every drawing in its finished state and skips parallax.

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Load: a "curing" counter runs while the slab wall stays closed, then the slabs lift. Scroll: the slabs drift downward at different rates over the wordmark; sections reveal with rises and slab wipes; numbers count up; the section drawing plays once when it enters the view. Pointer (desktop): a crosshair with live X/Y and section readout follows the mouse; moving over an index row opens its elevation drawing (real mouse movement is required, so a row that slides under a still pointer does not cascade); clicking or tapping a row toggles it, and the first row opens by itself when the index scrolls into view. Sun study: drag the Time of day slider (arrow keys work), drag the scene itself left and right, or press "Play the day" (it also autoplays gently when first seen); readouts show altitude, azimuth, shadow-to-height ratio and sky phase. Section drawing: "Replay the cut" button. Exposure button or the E key toggles blueprint mode; double-clicking the hero or pressing P closes the slabs and pours them again. Anchor links scroll smoothly. Touch: everything works by tap and drag; the crosshair is hidden. Keyboard: all controls are buttons or a range input with visible focus outlines. Reduced motion: slabs open immediately, parallax and autoplay are off, the section drawing and elevations appear complete.
