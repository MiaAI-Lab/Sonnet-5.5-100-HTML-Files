==================== TITLE ====================
Academy Leader 8→2

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A single 4:3 letterboxed canvas frame in a near-black room plays a vintage film countdown, then a title card. The leader counts 8, 7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2 in a high-contrast Didone numeral (one per second) framed by a double ring, a 60-tick dial, ruler-marked crosshairs, corner registration marks and printed legends (ACADEMY LEADER, 35MM 1.37:1 24 FPS, PICTURE START, SONNET SONATA). A bright arm sweeps 360 degrees every second, trailing a translucent radar wedge and lighting the dial ticks as it passes, while a clock wipe reveals the alternate tone of the field; numbers alternate between warm sepia (#f1e4c3 ink over dark brown) and a cooler silvery green, so each cut has a subtle duotone shift. Sprocket holes and edge-print scroll up both sides, and the whole picture suffers gate weave, projector flicker, grain, dust, hairs, flickering vertical scratches and a heavy vignette, all stepping at 24 frames per second. After the 2 the screen goes black for a beat, a soft white pop blip flashes, and the film cuts to the title card: a staff-in-a-circle emblem, A SONNET SONATA PRESENTS, a diamond rule and THE LAST REEL revealing letter by letter while its tracking tightens, under a slow zoom, an orange film-burn flash and drifting light-leak bands, with changeover cue dots at the end. HUD elements in projector typography sit in the corners: a typewriter REEL 1 OF 1 label with a blinking block cursor, an opt-in Sound toggle, Replay and Skip controls that arrive with the title, and a running 24 fps timecode.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- One 2D canvas designed in a virtual 1280 x 960 space, scaled to the 4:3 frame (device-pixel-ratio capped, internal width max 1280), redrawn every animation frame with a delta-time clock and a film-state layer that only changes at 24 fps
- Clock wipe built from a clipped wedge path whose swept region is filled with the alternate duotone, plus a 26-slice fading radar wedge, a glowing arm, dial ticks brightened by angular distance behind the arm, and a per-second numeral punch (scale and flash)
- Alternating warm and cool sepia duotones where the wipe colour of one number becomes the base of the next, keeping the cut nearly continuous
- Deterministic hash-based film randomness (seeded by frame index) for gate weave (smooth value noise plus jitter, translate and rotate on the whole picture and strips), flicker, dust specks and fibres, persistent flickering scratches and bezier hairs
- Six pre-generated 256 px gaussian-ish noise tiles as canvas patterns blended with overlay and screen at random offsets for grain; pre-rendered vignette bitmap
- Scrolling sprocket strips with rounded perforations and rotated edge-print text that move with the weave
- Title card: staff-in-circle emblem drawn with clip paths, per-letter reveal with animated tracking and shadow glow, auto-fitted title size, slow zoom, radial-gradient film burn in lighter blending, and two screen-blended linear-gradient light-leak sweeps
- HUD in DOM over the canvas: typewriter label with CSS block cursor, condensed-grotesque capital buttons, tabular timecode; layout moves the HUD into the black bars for portrait screens
- WebAudio (opt-in, master gain 0.24): sine tick beeps with a noise click each second, a 1 kHz sine pop, a burn whoosh (swept band-passed noise) and a projector whirr made of brown-noise rumble, a 24 Hz shutter flutter LFO and a 24-per-second sprocket rattle buffer, with fade-out at the end
- Accessibility and control: aria-live status messages, real buttons with visible focus, keyboard shortcuts (S skip, R replay, M sound), an optional #t=seconds deep link, reduced-motion mode that damps weave, flicker, flashes, zoom and scroll speed

==================== INTERACTION ====================
The piece starts playing on load with no input needed: the countdown runs for seven seconds, then a black beat with a pop, then the title card, then a held final frame that keeps flickering, weaving and scratching. The Sound button (top right, off by default) switches on tick beeps, the 1 kHz pop, the burn whoosh and the projector whirr; it also works as the M key. Moving the mouse, touching the screen or pressing a key reveals the Replay and Skip buttons early (they also arrive automatically with the title card). Skip jumps from the countdown to the title card, and from the title card to its final held frame (then it disables itself); Replay restarts the whole sequence, re-typing the REEL 1 OF 1 label; keys S and R do the same. A live timecode (hours:minutes:seconds:frames at 24 fps) runs bottom right and freezes at the end. On portrait phones the frame stays 4:3 and the HUD moves above and below it. With prefers-reduced-motion the gate weave, flicker, flashes, film burn, light leaks, zoom and sprocket scroll speed are strongly reduced and the label appears instantly, while the countdown, wipe and title still play.
