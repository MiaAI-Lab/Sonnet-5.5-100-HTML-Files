==================== TITLE ====================
Contour: Rising Tides

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A survey sheet of the fictional Skerry Isles on aged paper with mottling, fibres and foxing. Nine seeded blobs and layered value-noise become a height field in metres; it is shaded from the north-west, tinted from lowland green to pale tan, and traced with 20 m contours, heavier 100 m index lines carrying rotated labels, and dashed 5 m and 10 m supplementary lines. Summits are marked with triangles and named, five villages sit on coasts at deliberately different heights, and a neatline with tick marks frames the sheet. On the right a tall brass-and-ink gauge sets the sea from 0 to +12 m: shoreline, translucent depth-shaded water, hatched shallows and the village markers recompute, while readouts show land remaining and villages at risk. A storm-surge button adds a pulsing +2 m. A humanist small-caps title cartouche, an SVG legend, compass rose and scale bar complete the sheet.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Seeded value-noise fbm plus radial island blobs make the archipelago; heights use a power curve so coasts have broad low plains.
- Marching squares (with linear interpolation) draws every contour level, the supplementary lines and, per update, the shoreline; index-contour segments are chained into polylines so labels can be placed along them, rotated to the tangent, with a paper-coloured halo.
- Hillshade computed per grid cell from NW light and drawn from a grid-resolution canvas scaled up with smoothing; all static layers (paper, land, contours, labels, neatline) are pre-rendered once to an offscreen-quality base canvas.
- Flooding uses a flood fill from the sea, so low inland hollows stay dry until the water can actually reach them; the water raster is coloured by depth (shallow #a6cfd9 to deep #4b8fa8), translucent over drowned land, with a hatch pattern masked to the shallow band.
- Only the water layer is rebuilt when the level changes; each frame composes the cached water, a moving streak pattern clipped to the water's own alpha for shimmer, a gently wobbling shoreline and pulsing red village rings.
- Vertical slider built as an ARIA slider with pointer capture and keyboard support; storm surge is an enveloped +2 m pulse with a warning banner.
- Hover/focus crosshair with elevation, fictional latitude/longitude in typewriter type and nearest place name; arrow keys move the crosshair.
- A tiny sailing ship follows a superellipse route around the archipelago with a wake.

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Drag the gauge (mouse or touch), use the arrow keys, Page Up/Down, Home and End, or scroll over the map to change the sea level from 0 to +12 m; the shoreline retreats, lowlands drown into shallows, the land-remaining percentage falls and villages turn red and pulse as they flood. Press Storm surge for a 2 m wave that swells, holds and recedes over about eight seconds with a warning banner. Move the pointer over the map for a survey crosshair and a tooltip with elevation (or sea depth), coordinates and the nearest place; focus the map and use the arrow keys to move it. A ship sails around the isles and the water shimmers. With prefers-reduced-motion the shimmer, shoreline wobble and pulsing rings are switched off and the ship crawls.
