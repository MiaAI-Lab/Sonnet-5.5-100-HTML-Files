==================== TITLE ====================
Structure / Rhythm Swiss Poster

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A single-screen, flat, razor-sharp Swiss poster for the fictional "Structure / Rhythm — Festival of Sound Design, Basel 12–15 November 2026", built on paper white (#f4f2ee), ink black (#0b0b0b), Swiss red (#e4002b) and one mid grey. Twelve visible hairline columns, numbered 01–12 like a ruler, run the full height of the sheet and draw themselves in at load. A giant heavy-grotesque "26" is cropped by the right and bottom page edges; over its upper half sits a circle system of thirteen concentric rings built from hundreds of hairline ticks (dashed SVG circles) turning at different speeds and in alternating directions, with a 60-tick seconds dial and a red disc that orbits one ring at exactly 6 degrees per real second, trailed by a thin red elapsed-seconds arc, a red "hot" tick on the dial and a live second counter at the centre. The ring block is blended with mix-blend-mode: difference, so its strokes are black on the paper and flip to paper white wherever they cross the black numerals (an x-ray overlap that also works in the inverted theme). Text is flush-left / ragged-right in exactly three sizes: a heavy "Structure/Rhythm" lockup with a red slash, a lede paragraph, and small tabular text in the meta strip, table and footnote; every line sits on a shared baseline grid (press B to see it). A rotated line of tracked capitals runs up the left edge. A ledger-style programme table (four days, seven sessions each, tabular numerals, hairline rules) has a red bar that slides to the hovered or focused row. Four presets rearrange the same blocks: 1 Grid (type left, rings and numerals right), 2 Stack (one-line title, numerals and rings in a band, lede and wide table below, a taller sheet), 3 Diagonal (title top-left, rings centre, numerals bottom-right joined by a red diagonal rule) and 4 Poster (numerals stacked "2" over "6" bleeding off the left edge, huge rings, tiny text). Clicking the red disc inverts the whole poster to black with paper-white type through a circular reveal that opens from the disc. No gradients, no shadows, no rounded corners; the disc is the only round thing.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- 12-column CSS grid whose rows are an exact baseline unit (viewport height divided into an integer number of rows, about 20px at 1440x900); 13 hairline elements are grid children that draw in with a staggered scaleY animation, and ruler numerals 01–12 sit on the first row.
- Columns lean toward the pointer: JS writes a 12-track grid-template-columns of minmax(0, Nfr) with gaussian weights, quantised to half-column steps, and a CSS transition on grid-template-columns animates it; the column under the pointer turns red in the ruler. Off for touch and reduced motion.
- Ring system: thirteen rings, the tick bands being a single dashed circle each (stroke-dasharray gives 36–240 hairline ticks per band) on its own SVG layer rotated by a CSS animation with a negative animation-delay computed from Date.now(), so every band is phase-locked to the wall clock (periods 10, 12, 15, 20, 30, 60 and 120 seconds, alternating direction, all realigning on the minute); static hairline groove circles and a 60-tick dial complete the system.
- mix-blend-mode: difference on the ring block and the vertical text, using a "paper minus ink" stroke colour, so the same strokes are black on paper and white over black numerals in both the light and inverted themes.
- The red overlay (elapsed arc, hot tick, the disc button) lives outside the blend group and is re-synchronised every frame to the ring block's box and transform; disc angle = (Date.now() mod 60000) / 60000 x 360 degrees, hot tick and counter update once per second, Basel time via Intl.DateTimeFormat (Europe/Zurich) with a local-time fallback.
- Rigorous baselines: text-box: trim-start / trim-both cap alphabetic plus cap-height padding maths put the first baseline of every block on a grid line (line-heights are whole multiples of the unit, rules sit 0.3 unit under baselines, the title's cap height equals four units); an SVG pattern overlay (key B) shows the grid. Progressive enhancement: without text-box support the type still sits on the grid rows, only a few pixels off.
- Numerals are a clipped "window" (overflow hidden, flex-end) with negative margins to crop them at the page edge, thickened with -webkit-text-stroke so the heavy grotesque look survives any system font (Helvetica Neue, Helvetica, Nimbus Sans, Arial, Liberation Sans stack).
- Layout presets are sets of CSS custom properties per data-layout (grid areas, sizes, crops); switching uses a FLIP technique driven by a JS tween loop with an easeInOutQuint "mechanical" curve, staggered per block; the ring block also scales, the numerals re-rise inside their window, and lede and table fade out in Poster.
- Invert uses the View Transitions API with an animated circular clip-path expanding from the disc (instant swap fallback), theme tokens flip on :root.inv.
- Programme table: absolutely positioned red bar moved with transform and height transitions to the hovered or focused row, rows wipe in per day, per-session mark toggles with a live "n marked" counter, day tabs for Thu 12 to Sun 15.
- Opt-in WebAudio metronome (triangle-wave ticks, quiet, accent every fifth second = one dial group) with a pulse on the disc; created only after a click or key press.
- Responsive: 12 columns down to about 760px with table and meta adjustments, then a single-column flow with the ring block overlapping the numerals and 40px touch targets; prefers-reduced-motion stops draws, spins and transitions and steps the disc once per second.

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Mouse: moving the pointer makes the twelve columns compress and expand toward it (blocks, ring system and numerals shift with the grid); hovering a table row slides the red bar to it and turns its text paper white; clicking a row (or its numbered square button) marks the session and updates the counter; day buttons swap the seven-row timetable. Click the red disc (a hint tag "Click · invert" appears a few seconds after load and on hover) to invert to black with white type through a circular reveal; click it again to restore. Keyboard: 1, 2, 3, 4 switch Grid, Stack, Diagonal and Poster (the four small boxed numbers in the meta strip do the same, and are the touch controls); I inverts; B shows or hides the red baseline-grid overlay; M turns the metronome tick on or off (also the Tick button). Every control is a real button with a visible red focus outline; table rows light up when their button receives keyboard focus. Touch: tap the layout buttons, day tabs, table rows and the disc; the column-lean effect is disabled and the poster becomes a single scrolling column. The rings and the disc run by themselves, phase-locked to the real clock, with the seconds counter in the centre. Reduced motion: no draw-in, slide or spin; layout changes are instant and the disc advances in whole-second steps.
