==================== TITLE ====================
Field Notes: Magnetism

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A vintage scientific plate: warm paper with a runtime-drawn fibre texture, a double-ruled sheet, an engraved "Plate III" masthead and a framed plate area with platemark, edge rulers and a caption line. On the plate lie up to about six thousand graphite filings on a jittered grid; each is a short round-capped stroke (5-9px) whose direction eases, with a slightly under-damped spring, toward the direction of the summed field of point poles belonging to draggable bar magnets (red north end, prussian-blue south end, letters in serif) and a horseshoe. Field strength sets both opacity and thickness so the filings darken and thicken at the poles. Five arrangements (Bar magnet, Two attract, Two repel, Horseshoe, Quadrupole) glide the magnets into place while the filings swirl after them; captions change to matching figure numbers. Instruments include RK4 streamlines with arrowheads that draw themselves in, a compass needle that follows the pointer with an inertial swing and a small readout, "Tap the card gently" which shakes the filings loose so they re-settle, plus a marginal note in italic with a drop cap. On first load the filings start scattered and align in a ripple outward from the magnet.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Point-pole field model: each magnet contributes a +/- pole pair with softened inverse-square terms, summed at every filing
- Jittered-grid filing layout with per-filing length, spring stiffness and damping; wrap-aware angle easing in the mod-pi sense
- Counting-sort binning by field strength so thousands of strokes are drawn as a dozen batched canvas paths (alpha and width scale with log field magnitude)
- Settling detection: the filings canvas stops redrawing when everything is at rest; two stacked canvases separate filings from magnets, streamlines and compass
- RK4 streamline integration from every north pole with termination at south poles, arrow marks at arc-length intervals, a baked cache and an animated reveal
- Canvas-drawn magnets with shading, upright pole letters, dashed selection frame and a rotation handle; horseshoe drawn as a stroked arc with painted tips
- Pointer Events for drag, handle rotation (Shift snaps 15 degrees), wheel rotation, double-click / double-tap flip and two-finger rotation
- Runtime-generated paper tile (fibres, specks, foxing) used as a CSS background, engraved rulers via repeating gradients
- System serif stack with italic captions, small caps and a drop cap; lite mode with fewer filings on software renderers
- Keyboard controls: R, F, +, Delete, arrows, T, S, C, digits to select magnets

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Drag a magnet by its body to move it; the filings re-orient continuously. Grab the round handle at the north end and drag to turn the magnet (hold Shift to snap), or press R (Shift+R the other way), use the mouse wheel over a magnet, or turn it with two fingers on a touch screen. Double-click or double-tap a magnet, or press F, to flip its polarity. "Add magnet" (or +) drops in a new random magnet (up to six), "Remove" (or Delete) removes the selected one. Pick a preset to slide the magnets into a classic arrangement. Toggle Streamlines (S) and the Compass needle (C); the compass follows the pointer and shows a bearing and strength readout. "Tap the card gently" (T) jolts the filings so they resettle. Arrow keys nudge the selected magnet. With prefers-reduced-motion the intro ripple, magnet glides and compass swing are replaced by instant, critically damped settling.
