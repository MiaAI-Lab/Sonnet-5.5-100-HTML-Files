01 — About

Colour never really sits still. It pools, thins and slips past itself — the way light behaves in a glass of water held up to a window.

7fields of colour, each on its own path
4moods, 1.2 s crossfade
0images used, ever

Lucid is a small study in slowness. Seven soft fields of pigment drift along Lissajous paths that almost, but never quite, repeat. A turbulence map bends their edges the way heat bends air, and a breath of film grain keeps every gradient honest.

Nothing on this page is an image. Every colour is computed, every refraction is drawn live, and each mood is only four colours and a temperament. Move your pointer — the nearest fields lean toward it, gently, like plants toward a window.

02 — Effects

Three small tricks, done slowly.

Everything in the hero is made from three ingredients. Here they are on their own, looping in place, wearing whichever mood you last chose.

Effect 01Lissajous 3 : 2

Drift

Each field of colour follows its own curve — two frequencies that almost, but never quite, agree. The dotted path closes only after nearly a minute.

Effect 02Displacement map

Refract

A turbulence map bends whatever sits behind the glass, and splits it a little by channel. The word in the hero is a window, not a sticker.

Effect 03Film grain

Grain

A breath of fine grain dithers the banding out of every gradient and gives the light a little tooth. Left of the line: without. Right: with.
