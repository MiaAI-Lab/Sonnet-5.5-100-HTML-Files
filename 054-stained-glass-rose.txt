==================== TITLE ====================
Rosace: Generative Rose Window

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A dark stone transept wall holds a generative gothic rose window, ringed by a voussoir moulding with a band of small ball-flowers, above a flagstone nave floor. Each window is baked per pixel from implicit geometry: a hub, a ring of lens-shaped petals, N pointed lancet arches springing from the ribs, N roundels with segmented borders, spandrels and a rim of cut stones, all divided by 8, 10, 12 or 16 radial ribs. Inside every cell jittered Voronoi seeds cut irregular panes, coloured from one of four jewel schemes (each window is named, e.g. "Rose of the Sapphire Vigil"). Lead came has three widths, rounded profiles and a bevelled highlight; the glass carries streaks, bubbles, edge darkening and a pot-metal glow. A slow sun sweeps an arc of light across the panes with bloom, tinting from rose at Lauds to white at Sext to deep amber at Compline, spilling the window's colours onto the wall, and throwing a rippling, sheared, blurred projection onto the floor, with translucent shafts and drifting dust motes. Clicking a pane breaks it into flying shards and glass dust, leaves a hole of white light, then the pieces fly back and re-glaze with a flash.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Raster tracery: per-pixel classification into cells using signed-distance style tests for circles, ribs, a two-centred pointed arch, a vesica lens and roundels, folded into one wedge by angle
- Voronoi panes per cell from template seeds (Poisson rejection, mirrored left/right, jittered per wedge), exact bisector distances for thin lead, and angular segmentation for the border strips
- Three lead widths with anti-aliased coverage, a rounded height profile and a second pass bevel (gradient lighting plus specular ridge)
- Glass shading: rotated value-noise streaks, large swirl, edge darkening, per-pane centre glow, bubbles with bright rims and a filmic shoulder to keep pale glass from clipping
- Window baked by a generator function (synchronous at load, time-sliced for "New window") with per-pane statistics, bounding boxes and an anti-aliased low-resolution light map
- Additive lighting: per-pane luminosity from a moving sun hotspot, hover, pulses, bell wave and reveal front, written to a 192px light map, upscaled for bloom and sun glare
- Floor projection: the same light map sheared and squashed by the sun's height, drawn as a soft pool plus a sliced sharp copy that ripples, coloured shafts sampled from the panes and dust motes lit by proximity to shafts
- Shatter system: pane pixels split into shards by nearest random seeds, burst with gravity and spin, return along eased paths to the rotating window, with glass dust and glints
- Static stone drawn once: ashlar courses, speckle, buttress niches, moulded surround with voussoirs, perspective flagstones, vignette
- System serif small-caps typography, canonical-hour clock names, dark control pill with a custom range slider

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Move the pointer over the window to brighten a pane (its glass colour and cut number appear top right). Drag anywhere to turn the window; releasing lets it coast with inertia back to a slow resting drift. Click or tap a pane to shatter and re-glaze it; click the hub to ring a shimmering bell wave. The Time slider scrubs the sun from dawn (Lauds) to dusk (Compline) and pauses the slow automatic sun for a few seconds. The rib buttons 8, 10, 12 and 16 and the New window button fade the window out, glaze a new seeded design in slices and reveal it with a ring of light. Keyboard: focus the canvas, then arrows turn it, Enter or Space breaks a pane, N makes a new window, B rings the bell. Touch works through pointer events. With prefers-reduced-motion the resting rotation stops, the sun crawls, shards travel less and ripples calm, while the window still looks complete.
