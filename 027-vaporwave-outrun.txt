==================== TITLE ====================
OUTRUN 1986 Night Drive

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A full-screen canvas synthwave drive. The sky runs violet #1a0b3d to magenta #a4126e to hot orange #ff7b3a at the horizon, with twinkling stars, a huge yellow-to-magenta sun whose horizontal cut-out stripes grow toward the bottom and drift continuously, and two ranges of wireframe mountains (magenta far, cyan near) built from a triangle mesh, contour lines and triple-stroke glowing ridges that pan as the road bends. Below the horizon a scanline-projected highway rushes at the viewer: alternating asphalt bands, magenta/violet rumble strips with cyan edge lines, yellow dashes, plus a neon grid on both sides (magenta perspective rails, cyan cross lines). Palm silhouettes with magenta rim light and neon lamp posts scale with perspective, oncoming headlights streak past on the left lane and tail-lights recede ahead. A hand-drawn SVG sports car seen from behind (wing, louvres, slatted glowing tail-light bars, plate, exhausts with boost flames, wheels with moving tread) steers and rolls with the input. The chrome-gradient italic OUTRUN title has a pink neon glow with a cyan 1986; the HUD has a seven-segment speedometer with LED tach bars, score, distance and top speed, and a fake "Radio: Night Drive FM" cassette deck with spinning reels, a marquee and VU meters driven by a real analyser. CRT touches: scanlines with flicker, a rolling refresh bar, vignette, RGB-split chromatic aberration.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Pseudo-3D road: per-scanline projection from a camera 3.4 m up over an 8 m road (z = focal x camH / dy), parabolic curvature offset per row, stripe parity from scroll distance for asphalt, rumble strips, dashes and grid rows.
- Perspective vertical grid: 48 polylines with geometrically growing spacing, drawn twice (glow and core) with additive compositing.
- Sun: offscreen canvas with gradient disc and animated destination-out stripes of growing thickness, halo sprite; horizon haze overlay; sun and stars parallax with heading.
- Mountains: procedural periodic ridges rendered once to wide textures (mass, triangular wire mesh, contours, three-pass glow) and panned with wrap-around.
- Sprites: palm trees drawn procedurally to offscreen canvases (tapered trunk, fronds with leaflets, rim light), glow sprites for lamps and lights; sorted far-to-near.
- SVG car with per-frame transform (lateral offset, roll, skew from curvature, bob), animated tread, tail-light glow reacting to braking, exhaust flames while boosting.
- Seven-segment speedometer built from SVG lines, LED tach bars, score/distance counters.
- WebAudio (opt-in): scheduler with lookahead playing a minor-key arpeggio, sawtooth bassline, soft kick and hats, feedback delay and compressor; filters open with speed; three tracks; master gain 0.2.
- CRT: repeating-gradient scanlines with flicker keyframes, rolling bar, vignette, SVG feColorMatrix/feOffset RGB-split filter on the canvas, text-shadow fringes on the HUD.
- Adaptive quality: render scale and the aberration filter drop automatically on slow frames; loop pauses when the tab is hidden; reduced motion lowers top speed, stops shake, flicker and twinkle.

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Move the mouse (or drag a finger) left and right to steer; the road bends with the wheel and the car rolls. Arrow keys or A/D steer, Up/W or Space accelerate, Down/S brake. Holding the mouse button, a touch, or Space raises speed toward 320 km/h with speed lines, camera shake and boost flames; release and it settles back to a cruise. On touch devices a Tilt steering button enables device orientation steering (with the iOS permission prompt). The radio deck's play button (or M) starts the synthwave arpeggio, Next changes track; audio is off by default and only starts after a click or key press. A hint line fades after the first input.
