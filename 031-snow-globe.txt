==================== TITLE ====================
Winter, 1924 Snow Globe

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A warm-lit parlour scene: a brass-shaded lamp glows at the left, bokeh garland lights twinkle on a papered wall, and a burgundy cloth drapes the table edge with a gold braid. On it stands a snow globe on a carved mahogany base with a brass plaque engraved "Winter · 1924 · Val d'Argent". Inside the glass, an SVG alpine village at night: moon and stars, snowy peaks, snow-capped houses with glowing windows, a church with a swinging bell, pines, a picket fence, a lamppost with a pulsing halo, a frozen pond with a skater gliding in ovals and smoke curling from chimneys. About 400 flakes fall on two canvases, behind and in front of the village, and pile into a soft drift on the ground. A handwritten "Shake me" hint fades after the first interaction.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Layered globe: sky SVG, back snow canvas, village SVG, front snow canvas, glass overlay, all clipped to a circle for depth
- 400 flakes with per-flake depth, terminal velocity, drag toward a swirling flow field, glass-wall reflection and a vortex spin term
- Fictitious force: flakes accelerate opposite to the globe's spring acceleration, so shaking flings them and they lag behind the glass
- Settling flakes add gaussian mounds to a 100-column height field rendered as a soft drift; shaking re-suspends them
- Spring-driven 3D rig (translate, rotateX/Y/Z) with underdamped return, plus a tilt that also skews gravity
- Glass built from radial gradients, rim light, crescent highlight and warm bounce light; caustic glow on the table that shifts opposite to the tilt and flickers
- CSS-only carved wood base with moulding, brass plaque with small-caps engraving effect, drape folds from stacked repeating gradients
- SVG animation: skater on an ellipse computed in JS, chimney smoke puffs, halo pulse
- WebAudio inharmonic-partial church bell with a strike transient, and a convolver-reverbed music box playing Silent Night (opt-in)
- Film grain from a tiny canvas, vignette, devicemotion shake support

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Press and drag the globe: it follows the pointer with spring physics, tilts and wobbles, and the snow whirls up; release and it springs home while the flakes slowly settle into the drift. The Shake button or a device shake gives an impulse. Click or tap the church (also keyboard-focusable) to ring its bell with a swinging animation. The Lullaby toggle (off by default) plays a music-box tune at modest volume. "Shake me" fades away after the first shake. With prefers-reduced-motion the agitation decays faster and the twinkling is stilled.
