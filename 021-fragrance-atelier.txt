==================== TITLE ====================
Atelier Néroli: Blue Hour

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A weightless, dreamlike one-page launch for an imaginary perfume, Eau de Parfum N°5 "Blue Hour". A fixed canvas paints a soft-focus world of slowly morphing lavender, apricot, seafoam and milky-pink blobs, with drifting bokeh rings and a film-grain veil. In the hero a hand-drawn SVG flacon floats between the glass-outlined italic words "Blue" and "Hour": faceted lavender crystal cap, champagne collar, thick glass foot, a dusk-to-apricot liquid with a shimmering, counter-tilting meniscus, tiny label typography, and an inverted, magnified refraction of the live background inside the glass. SVG petals, citrus wheels, blossoms, peppercorns, wood shavings and pearls drift upward with parallax and slow rotation. Six glass note pills (Top: bergamot, pink pepper; Heart: neroli, jasmine sambac; Base: cedar, musk) re-tint the whole world and morph every floating particle into that note's motif while a sensory caption fades in. Scrolling reveals a light italic Didone story paragraph that surfaces word by word, a thin-line scent pyramid that draws itself with rising scent molecules and tier-aligned notes, three floating frosted-glass review cards with pointer-tracked sheen, and a "Discovery set" pill with a flowing pastel gradient and shimmer sweep above a row of five little vials. A faint perfume-mist trail follows the cursor. As you scroll the light deepens to dusk and closes in apricot dawn.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Low-resolution canvas 2D world: eight elliptical radial-gradient blobs on Lissajous paths plus bokeh rings, upscaled smoothly by CSS; palette is lerped every frame toward the active note's colours and biased toward dusk/dawn by scroll progress
- Procedural film grain: a 200px noise tile generated once on a canvas and applied as a tiled data-URL background (no filter cost)
- Hand-drawn SVG perfume bottle: clip-paths for body, cavity and live liquid surface, gradient glass, dispersion-tinted edges, faceted crystal cap with animated prism bands, metallic collar, label with tiny SVG text
- Physically inspired details in JS: liquid surface as a two-sine wave path recomputed at 30 Hz, counter-rotated against the bottle's tilt with slosh energy; refraction layers mapping the world's blob positions through an inverted, magnified lens (stronger inside the liquid)
- Reusable SVG symbols (petals, three citrus wheels built from dashed strokes, orange blossom, jasmine, peppercorns, wood shaving, pearl) with SVG blur filters for depth of field; 28 items with parallax, sway, flutter and staggered motif cross-fades
- Note-driven theming: hover/focus/tap sets palette + motif targets, updates caption and highlights the matching pyramid tier
- Perfume-mist trail: pooled canvas particles using pre-rendered radial sprites, spawned along the pointer path, plus bursts on bottle and CTA clicks
- Word-by-word scroll reveal of the Didone story paragraph; IntersectionObserver-driven line-drawing pyramid (pathLength dash animation), staggered tier text and CSS-animated scent molecules
- Glass UI: backdrop-filter pills and cards, inner highlights, layered translucent gradients, pointer-tracked radial sheen and subtle 3D tilt; animated 640px-tile gradient CTA with skewed shimmer sweep
- System font stacks only (Didone stack with Yrsa/Rasa fallbacks, geometric sans for tracked caps); glass-outlined display lettering via -webkit-text-stroke with paint-order
