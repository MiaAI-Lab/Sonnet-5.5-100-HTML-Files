==================== TITLE ====================
Little Delights

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A sunny pastel board of twelve handmade microinteractions, each living in its own rounded tile (peach, lilac, mint, butter and sky washes over a dotted cream backdrop) with a numbered plum chip, a bold label and a one-line description. A big heavy rounded "Little Delights" headline (the second word in hot pink with a plum sticker shadow, letters that hop on hover, and a squiggle that draws itself in) sits above a "Discovered n/12" meter whose dots light up as the visitor finds each interaction. Tiles are laid out as a masonry-like grid of varying heights (three balanced columns on desktop, two on tablets, one on phones) and lift with a springy tilt on hover. The twelve tiles are: a gooey toggle, a heart burst, a ripple button, a hamburger-to-cross menu that fans out satellites, a magnetic button, an elastic rubber-band slider, a self-drawing tick with confetti, a pull-to-refresh drop that stretches and snaps, a liquid tab bar, a watchful password eye, a rolling odometer like counter, and a day/night switch that turns the whole tile into a starry night. Everything is driven by real buttons and inputs, with spring easing and overshoot throughout, visible focus rings and aria states, and a cheerful copywriting voice.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- SVG goo filter (feGaussianBlur + alpha-threshold feColorMatrix) applied to three staggered knob blobs with direction-dependent transition delays, so the knob stretches into a peanut shape and snaps together; a duplicate dark copy gives it a soft shadow
- A tiny shared spring engine (semi-implicit Euler with sub-steps, one rAF loop that sleeps when idle, critical damping under reduced motion) driving the ripple button press, magnetic offsets, slider thumb/bend, pull-to-refresh and the liquid tab pill
- Web Animations API particle layer (fixed overlay) for heart bursts (hearts, dots and radially aligned dashes plus an expanding ring) and confetti (rects, dots, triangles with rise-and-fall gravity keyframes)
- Elastic slider built from a single SVG quadratic Bezier: the control point follows the thumb x and the pointer y, the thumb sits exactly on the curve (closed-form t from x(t) = thumb x), the filled part is the de Casteljau sub-curve, stroke width thins as the band is stretched, and an underdamped spring makes it wobble on release; a hidden native range input keeps it keyboard and screen-reader accessible
- Metaball pull-to-refresh: an anchor blob, a chain of shrinking neck circles and a drop under a second goo filter, so the neck thins and detaches, then a spring snaps the drop to a spinner, then a stroke-drawn check appears and a new feed row slides in
- Liquid tab pill: leading and trailing edges are separate springs with different stiffness (stretch while travelling), plus a velocity-based vertical squish; roving tabindex with arrow, Home and End keys
- Password eye: SVG almond eye with clip-path, gradient iris and pupil that tracks the pointer (rAF-throttled, with idle wandering and random blinks); hiding scales the lid shut with overshoot and fades in lashes, and swaps the input type
- Odometer: per-digit strips of three repeated 0-9 runs translated in em units with an overshooting cubic-bezier, carry-aware roll direction, right-to-left stagger, silent re-normalisation, and a masked fade at the top and bottom
- Day/night switch built from layered gradients, radial-gradient sun with halo rings and a cratered moon, twinkling four-point stars with staggered entry, drifting cloud SVGs, and a 360-degree knob roll; the whole tile flips to a night palette through CSS custom properties
- Stroke-dashoffset tick drawing, animated strike-through line, CSS masonry via grid-auto-rows with per-tile row spans and explicit columns, ui-rounded font stack with font-synthesis disabled for clean heavy weights
- Accessibility: real buttons, role=switch, aria-pressed, aria-expanded/controls, tablist semantics, aria-live regions, visible focus rings, reduced-motion overrides that shorten transitions, halve particles and remove idle loops

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Mouse, touch and keyboard all work on every tile. (1) Click, tap or press Space on the toggle: the knob stretches across and the label flips. (2) Tap the heart to like it: it pops, a ring expands and particles fly out; tap again to unlike; the replay icon re-fires it. (3) Press and hold the ripple button: it squishes on a spring and a ripple spreads from the exact touch point (or the centre for Space/Enter), then rebounds with overshoot on release; a boop counter increments. (4) Click the three-bar button: it morphs into a cross on a dark circle and three satellite buttons (Home, Play, Favourites) pop out in sequence; Escape or choosing one closes it. (5) Move the pointer near the pill button: it and its label lean toward you with a jelly spring, the dashed field ring lights up and a live "pull" readout updates; keyboard focus gives a small nudge; clicking says "Gotcha!". (6) Drag the slider thumb: the value bubble follows, and pulling the pointer up or down bends the rubber-band track; on release it wobbles back; arrow keys on the focused slider also move it with a little kick. (7) Tick the checkboxes: the box pops, the tick draws itself, the label is struck through and confetti bursts; the replay icon unticks all and re-ticks them in sequence. (8) Drag the mini phone feed downward: the drop stretches out of the top, the arrow flips at the threshold, releasing snaps it into a spinner, then a check, and a new post slides in; the replay icon (keyboard route) performs an automatic pull. (9) Click or use Left/Right/Home/End on the tab bar: the pink pill stretches toward the destination, squishes, and settles with overshoot while the caption changes. (10) Move the pointer anywhere: the eye follows it (and wanders and blinks when idle); click the eye to hide the password: the eyelid closes and the text turns to dots; click again to reveal. (11) Tap the heart-and-number pill to like or unlike: the digits roll like a mechanical counter; the replay icon jumps the count by a random amount so several digits and carries roll. (12) Toggle the day/night switch: the sun rolls over and becomes a cratered moon, stars twinkle in, clouds float away and the whole tile turns to a deep indigo night. A "Discovered n/12" meter in the header lights a dot for every tile the visitor has tried and celebrates with confetti and a wiggling title when all twelve are found. Tiles lift and tilt slightly on hover (hover-capable devices only). With prefers-reduced-motion the springs become critically damped (no overshoot), transitions are near-instant, particles are halved and idle animations (pulsing dot, floating stickers, drifting clouds, twinkling stars) are switched off, while the layout and all states remain fully usable.
