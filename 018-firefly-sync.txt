==================== TITLE ====================
Synchrony

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A blue-hour meadow in which 1,000 pulse-coupled fireflies (the 140 nearest drawn as large bokeh orbs, the rest as smaller and smaller glimmers towards a far tree line) slowly learn to flash together. Each firefly is an oscillator with its own natural frequency; it flashes when its phase wraps and, when it sees a neighbour flash, nudges its own phase forward, with absorption so that clusters snap into step and phase waves roll across the meadow. Glowing lime-to-amber orbs drift on curl-like flow fields over a dusk scene of gradient sky, stars, a thin crescent moon, layered conifer and crown tree lines, drifting mist and three layers of swaying grass and seed-head silhouettes, while the ground itself brightens whenever the swarm flashes in unison. A glass HUD shows the order parameter r as a thin arc gauge with live number, a unit-circle phase plot of every oscillator with the mean-field arrow, and a one-minute sparkline. A quiet serif caption reads "Synchrony — 1,000 tiny clocks learning to agree" beside a live poetic status line and tiny mono notes.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Pulse-coupled oscillator model: 1,000 phases with Gaussian-spread natural frequencies (f0 0.55 Hz, sigma 6%), refractory window, distance-weighted phase-response nudge, chain-reaction absorption; parameters tuned in an offline prototype so coupling 0 stays random, ~0.65 syncs in about 40 s with visible domains and waves, and 1 syncs within seconds.
- Neighbour lists from a 3D spatial hash (position, depth and height), rebuilt every 0.65 s as fireflies drift; fixed 1/60 s sub-steps with clamped dt.
- Order parameter r and mean phase computed every frame and shown as an SVG arc gauge (pathLength dash), a unit-circle canvas plot and a sparkline.
- Curl-like drift from sums of sinusoidal stream functions plus soft walls; pseudo-3D projection gives perspective size, height and depth ordering.
- Canvas 2D additive ("lighter") rendering with pre-rendered core and bokeh-ring sprites, batched specks for the far swarm, smooth size/halo scaling by depth, and adaptive thinning when frames run long.
- Scenery generated at runtime as SVG: stars with composited twinkle layers, two tree layers of crowns and pines, three grass layers cut into slices that each sway on the compositor, dandelion-like seed heads, gradient mist tiles drifting with CSS.
- Meadow "breathing": a ground light overlay driven by the mean flash intensity of the swarm.
- Click ripples as lightweight DOM elements; WebAudio crickets whose chirps are triggered by the flashes of 14 chosen fireflies, so the soundscape itself falls into step as coherence rises (soft compressor, stereo panning, opt-in).
- System-font typography only: old-style serif caption, monospace notes; deep-link #k=0.9 sets initial coupling.

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Click or tap the meadow (or press F) to flash: a ripple and glow appear and every firefly near the pointer, scaled by depth, is pushed forward in phase, flashing at once and triggering neighbours according to the coupling. The Coupling strength slider (0 to 1, keyboard arrows work) changes how strongly fireflies respond to what they see; the readout, gauge, phase circle and status line update live. Scatter (or S) re-randomises every phase and briefly stirs the swarm. Crickets off/on toggles the opt-in WebAudio ambience, which starts only after the click and stays soft. On phones the HUD reflows into a compact coherence card under the caption and a bottom control panel. With prefers-reduced-motion the grass, mist and stars stop, drift slows, flashes are softened and the ground glow is reduced.
