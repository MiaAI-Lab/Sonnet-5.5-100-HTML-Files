Beyond Neptune

Nothing you could see. The planets end here; the Sun’s reach does not.

Voyager 1 is over four times farther still — roughly 170 AU out, some 2.2 million px down this same line. The nearest star, Proxima Centauri, sits 3.46 billion px away: about forty days of nonstop scrolling.
