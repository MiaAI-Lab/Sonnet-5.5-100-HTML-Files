==================== TITLE ====================
Last Light at Camp

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A full-screen pixel-art night scene rendered in software onto a tiny canvas (about 240×135 logical pixels on a 1080p screen, 206×129 at 1440×900, 130×282 on a phone) that is scaled by a whole-number factor so every pixel stays perfectly crisp. A shaded full moon with craters hangs in a Bayer-dithered indigo sky (#0b1026 to #232a57) full of twinkling one-pixel, plus-shaped and cross-shaped stars; two hill ridges and three layers of tiered pine silhouettes sway on the horizon while dark near-pines frame the sides, one of them hiding a pair of blinking owl eyes. In the middle a campfire built from the classic Doom-fire algorithm burns inside a hand-drawn ring of stones, over crossed logs and an ash bed with glowing embers. To its left a teal tent glows from a lantern inside; to its right a camper in a bobble hat sits on a log toasting a marshmallow that slowly goes white, golden, brown and finally catches fire before being pulled back and replaced. Every ground, log, stone, tent and character pixel is coloured from a six-step night-to-firelit ramp chosen with ordered dithering, so the light of the flickering fire falls off in crisp dithered rings. The caption LAST LIGHT AT CAMP types itself out in a hand-authored 5×7 bitmap font, and a blinking PRESS TO FEED THE FIRE invites you to throw logs, because the fire slowly burns down toward embers if you leave it.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Software-rendered pixel canvas: one ImageData with a Uint32Array view is rebuilt every frame; the logical size is derived from the viewport so the device-pixel scale is always an integer (image-rendering: pixelated, centred crop), and the scene layout is computed from width and height so it also composes in portrait and ultra-wide windows.
- Doom-fire cellular automaton: a 46×48 heat buffer, each cell copying upward with random lateral drift and random decay, run at a fixed 30 Hz; fuel, surge and wind change the seed width, decay chance and lean; 16-step palette from black through #7a1f0b, #f26b1d and #ffd23f to #fff6d5; low heat values are dropped for a clean silhouette.
- Ordered dithering with a 4×4 Bayer matrix everywhere: the eight-step sky gradient and moon halo, ground depth bands, the trampled-dirt patch, hill crests, moon terminator and the light falloff itself.
- Light logic: a precomputed elliptical 1/(1+d²) falloff map from the fire and a second small one from the tent lantern, multiplied per frame by fuel, surge and noisy flicker, then mapped to a per-material six-step ramp (night blue to warm firelit) with Bayer thresholds, so ground, logs, stones, tent, camper and the near-pine rims all respond.
- Hand-authored ASCII sprite maps compiled at start-up: 35×25 tent with glowing interior, 18×19 outlined camper, crossed diagonal logs, a horizontal log, small logs, three stone shapes; a hand-authored 5×7 font (letters, digits and punctuation) draws the captions with a 1px shadow and typewriter reveal.
- Layered parallax pines: far, mid and near silhouettes drawn per row with integer sway that grows toward the tip and leans with the wind; a hash-based grass, pebble and ember texture; procedural pixel moon with hand-placed craters.
- Particles and set pieces: rising sparks that blink out, faint dithered smoke, gust marks while blowing, an occasional shooting star, a ballistic thrown log with tumbling frames, and a marshmallow that samples the heat buffer to toast at the flame edge.
- Opt-in WebAudio: looped noise through a low-pass for a fire roar, randomly filtered bandpass noise bursts for crackles that match the visual spark pops, a wind whoosh that follows the blow strength, and a low sine thud when a log lands; master gain kept modest and everything created only after the toggle is pressed.
- Robustness: requestAnimationFrame with clamped delta time, fixed-step fire simulation, no per-frame allocations in the hot loops, resize handling with rebuilt static layers, dithered fade-in from black, prefers-reduced-motion support (slower fire, calmer sway, no shooting stars, instant caption).

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Click or tap anywhere to throw a log: it arcs from the pointer into the hearth, the fire surges taller and brighter, sparks burst out and a thud plays if sound is on. Press and hold for about a quarter of a second (mouse or finger) to blow on the fire: the wind pushes the flames, sparks, smoke and pine tops away from the pointer while white gust marks stream toward the fire, at the cost of burning fuel faster. Keyboard: Space or Enter throws a log from a random side; hold Left or Right arrow to blow. Leave the fire alone and its fuel slowly drops so the flames shrink toward embers and the hint changes to FEED THE FIRE BEFORE IT DIES. The small pixel-styled Crackle button at the bottom right toggles the sound (default off; it needs a click before any audio starts). The camper's marshmallow cycle, owl eyes, twinkling stars, smoke and occasional shooting star run on their own. With prefers-reduced-motion the fire updates at 15 Hz, the trees sway less, twinkling is slowed, the shooting star is disabled and the caption appears at once, but the scene stays fully alive and interactive.
