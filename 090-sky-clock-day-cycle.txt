==================== TITLE ====================
Sky Clock — A Day Over the Lake

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A full-viewport canvas painting of a lake valley whose whole atmosphere is a function of the clock. Three procedurally generated mountain ridges, a pine ridge, a small lakeside village with a church spire, a wooden jetty, a rowing boat and swaying reeds sit beneath a sky gradient interpolated between hour-keyed palettes (pre-dawn indigo, coral sunrise, azure noon, golden hour, magenta dusk, navy night). The sun and moon follow arcs computed from a 45 degree north solar formula and today's real moon age, with a phase-correct moon disc. Stars, a Milky Way band, drifting under-lit clouds, sun rays, birds, bats, fireflies, dawn mist and a rippling mirrored lake reflection complete the scene. A large thin-serif time readout with an italic phrase, sunrise/sunset/daylight figures, and an arc-dial scrubber keyed to the sky's colours drive it.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Hour-keyed 3-stop sky gradients (top, mid, horizon, cloud tint) interpolated with smoothstep, with time remapped so real sunrise and sunset land on the 06:00 and 19:00 keys
- Standard solar declination / hour-angle formula at 45 N for sunrise, sunset, altitude and azimuth; moon position from synodic age
- Phase-correct moon disc with terminator ellipse, earthshine and maria
- Scene rendered to an offscreen canvas, then mirrored into the lake in 2px strips with sine ripple displacement for a shimmering reflection
- Fbm-noise mountain ridges with atmospheric haze tinted by the current horizon colour; instanced pine triangles
- Layered radial-gradient clouds with shade, body and highlight passes tinted by the time of day
- Additive rays, horizon glow, sun and moon glitter columns and ripple rings on the water
- Windows and lanterns light up by night factor; birds by day, bats at dusk, fireflies at night, dawn mist banks
- SVG arc-dial scrubber with colour-keyed gradient stroke, pointer capture and keyboard support
- Auto, manual and 20-second time-lapse modes; canvas scaled by devicePixelRatio (capped) with resize handling

==================== INTERACTION ====================
By default the scene follows the visitor's real local time. Drag or tap the arc dial (or focus it and use arrow keys, PageUp/PageDown, Home/End) to jump to any minute of the day; the sky, light and life update instantly. "Auto - now" returns to real time; "Time-lapse" runs a full day in about 20 seconds and loops until toggled off. Adding #t=17.7 to the URL opens a specific hour. Animation pauses when the tab is hidden; with prefers-reduced-motion clouds, mist, birds and ripples slow down and the colon stops blinking.
