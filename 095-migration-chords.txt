==================== TITLE ====================
Flyways Chord Atlas

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
An editorial data-art page on warm paper. A large chord diagram shows 20 directed shorebird flows between seven regions (invented, in thousands of birds): seven earthy arcs sized by total flow, Bezier ribbons through the centre with source-to-target gradients and 2px paper gaps, and an inner ring of tick marks with month labels carrying a moving month marker. Tiny cream triangle "birds" stream along the ribbons at speeds proportional to flow. The left column holds the hand-drawn sandpiper icon, the italic-accent serif title, a context line built from the data, Season toggle, a play/scrub month slider with a seasonal-activity sparkline, an "on the wing" live figure, a Diagram/Table switch and a clickable legend.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Chord layout computed by hand: arcs sized by in+out flow, sub-spans allocated clockwise so paired ribbons never cross, quadratic Beziers through the centre
- Per-ribbon userSpaceOnUse linear gradients from source to target hue; non-scaling 2px paper strokes as gaps
- Canvas particle layer aligned to the SVG viewBox: triangles oriented by the Bezier tangent, count and speed scaled by flow, alpha following each ribbon's current emphasis
- Seasonal model: Gaussian spring (peak early May) and autumn (peak September) activity curves drive ribbon opacity, particle speed and the live "on the wing" total
- Calendar ring: 48 ticks, 12 month labels, rotating triangle marker coloured by season; sparkline of both curves with a scrubbing cursor
- Isolation and locking: hovering or focusing arcs, ribbons or legend items dims unrelated chords; click locks, Esc releases
- Accessible alternatives: keyboard-focusable arcs with aria-labels, ribbon titles, a real th-scoped matrix table view
- Self-drawing SVG line icon (stroke-dashoffset), feTurbulence paper grain, refined-serif title with small-caps sans labels
- Sticky side column, responsive reflow (labels hidden on narrow screens, legend carries the names)

==================== INTERACTION ====================
The year plays automatically (pause button). Drag the month slider to scrub, or press Spring or Autumn to jump to each peak. Hover an arc for in/out totals and to isolate its chords; hover a ribbon for a tooltip such as "Arctic Tundra → South America: 1.2M birds"; click an arc, ribbon or legend chip to lock the selection, click empty space or press Esc to release. Arcs are focusable (Tab, Enter/Space to lock). The Table button swaps the diagram for an accessible matrix. Reduced motion: no autoplay, particles at one third speed, transitions removed, icon static.
