(01)StudioEst. 2014 — Oslo

We design brands, interfaces and spaces that behave — quiet on the surface, opinionated underneath.

Since 2014 we have worked with hotels that never close, seed merchants who never hurry, museums that hold the entire past and one very patient bike-share. Every project begins with a question and ends with a system — a small set of rules that lets a brand keep being itself when we are not in the room.

  • 01

    Identity

    Naming, logotypes, type systems, packaging and the guidelines people actually open.

  • 02

    Interface

    Products, prototypes and websites built to be used, not admired. Admired anyway.

  • 03

    Space

    Wayfinding, signage and exhibition graphics for buildings that hold a lot of people and very little patience.
