==================== TITLE ====================
How Big Is Empty?

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A single-canvas walk along the Solar System drawn at true scale: the Sun is 120 px wide, 1 AU is 12,890 px, and Neptune sits 387,600 px away, so the eight planets are 0.4 to 12 px glowing dots strung along a hairline ruler in near-total darkness. A traveller marker rests at 27% of the screen width while the world slides beneath it. As a world nears, a zoom-lens callout opens above its dot: a magnified, shaded, rotating sphere (procedurally textured Earth with drifting clouds, banded Jupiter with its Great Red Spot, ringed Saturn, tipped-over Uranus with upright rings, Neptune with its dark spot) joined to the true-size dot by two thin leader lines, with a typographic fact card and honest magnification label. Live readouts show light-time from the Sun, AU, km, px, screens crossed and speed as a multiple of the speed of light. A tiny light pulse drifts beside the traveller at exactly 25.8 px/s (real light speed at this scale) and is left behind when you move faster. Warm sunlight glows from the left edge and fades with distance, three depths of parallax stars and a faint Milky Way band sit behind, poetic whispers appear in the long voids, and past Neptune a quiet hint points toward Voyager 1 and Proxima Centauri. Type is a slender light sans with tabular mono numerals on a space-black field with warm-white text and sun-gold accents.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- One full-viewport 2D canvas driven by a virtual position p in world pixels; all bodies placed at true relative distance and diameter (1 px = 11,606 km).
- Adaptive input gearing (1:1 beside a world, up to about 120x in the middle of the dark) so a 400,000 px journey stays playable with wheel, drag, arrows or the slider.
- Critically damped target-follow motion model (velocity smoothing with a speed cap) for glide, fling inertia and the auto-braking Warp.
- Procedural planet surfaces: 3D value-noise fbm sampled on a sphere, sliced generation to keep the main thread free, craters stamped for Mercury and Mars, cloud layer for Earth, band and storm painters for Jupiter and Neptune.
- Per-pixel orthographic sphere rasteriser (lookup tables + ImageData) giving true spherical rotation, then canvas overlays for atmosphere rim glow and a terminator lit from the Sun side.
- Pre-rendered ring sprites (Cassini division, faint upright Uranus rings) drawn in back and front halves around the planet for correct occlusion.
- Zoom-lens callouts: tangent leader lines computed from the dot to the magnified circle, radius easing with proximity, magnification factor printed live.
- Light-pulse simulation in world coordinates at exactly c = 25.8 px/s, so it falls behind whenever you outrun light.
- Parallax star field in three depths with motion-blur streaks, warp speed lines, half-resolution Milky Way band, static film-grain tile applied through CSS.
- Schematic minimap (native range input mapped piecewise-linearly through the planet positions) with clickable stops, plus deep links via location hash (#earth, #saturn...).

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Mouse wheel or trackpad scrolls forward and back along the line; click-and-drag (or touch-drag) grabs the space and flings with inertia. Left and right arrow keys travel (Shift for faster); W or the Warp button auto-flies to the next world with a streak effect and brakes on arrival (Shift+W, PageUp go back, pressing again brakes); keys 0 to 8, Home and End jump to the Sun, a planet or the far dark; D or the Drift button toggles a slow autopilot that runs on load until the first input. The bottom schematic slider scrubs the whole route and its labelled stops warp to each world; planet names on the ruler are clickable too. Pressing Earth again while parked there sends a small easter-egg ripple. Approaching a world fades in its lens and fact card; whispers appear in the empty stretches; past Neptune a Voyager hint appears with a "Return to the light" button. With prefers-reduced-motion the streaks, grain shuffle and fast spin are removed and drift and rotation slow down, while the page stays complete. All motion pauses when the tab is hidden; the layout adapts from 360 px phones (cards move below the line) to wide desktops.
