==================== TITLE ====================
Weave: Over and Under

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A textile swatch lit on a dark linen table inside a hairline frame. The cream cloth is a canvas grid of Truchet tiles drawn as glossy, ribbed ribbons in indigo, madder, saffron and moss: quarter-circle arcs, diamond-zigzag diagonals, and a braid tile whose two ribbons cross with a raised over-ribbon and a shadowed under-ribbon. Every strand is traced across tile edges with union-find, so each continuous strand carries one dye, and the colour follows the strand as flips reconnect it. Tiles spring a quarter turn with overshoot; a slow travelling wave plus noise keeps the cloth quietly turning. The left and right edges shed loose fringe threads coloured by the strands that exit there, the top and bottom are hemmed with running stitches, a sheen drifts across the weave, and a sewn label hangs from the corner. Below sit the label "Weave - a study in over and under", live strand and loop counts, four pattern modes, a tile-size slider and Shuffle.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Canvas tile engine with offscreen sprite caches: ribbon sprites per kind and dye, blurred shadow silhouettes, whole-tile pair sprites, and a crossing-shadow sprite, all drawn with drawImage and rotation transforms
- Ribbon shading built from stacked strokes (dark edge to highlight core) plus dashed rib overlays for a grosgrain look
- Two-pass rendering (all ground shadows first, then ribbons) so shadows never darken neighbouring ribbons; light direction stays fixed while tiles rotate
- Braid tiles: z-order flips at the half turn, the over-ribbon widens slightly, crossing shadow fades with the flip
- Union-find over tile-edge midpoints to identify strands and loops; colour inertia (majority vote, split-off detection) and dye balancing so colours stay continuous
- Spring physics per tile (underdamped, about 22 percent overshoot) with typed-array state and variant swap at mid-rotation
- Idle travelling wave combined with value noise and per-tile randomness to decide turns
- Separate fringe canvas with swaying quadratic-curve threads whose colour eases to the strand at each exit
- CSS textile finish: fabric grid, feTurbulence speckle, drifting sheen, stitched hems, sewn label, static drop shadow
- Pointer Events for hover ripple, drag paint and click-to-cycle; deterministic test hook for stepping the simulation

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Hover the cloth to send a ripple of turns through nearby tiles. Press and drag to paint the turn (all touched tiles take the opposite orientation of the tile you started on). Click or tap without dragging to cycle the pattern mode (a wave of morphing tiles spreads from the click point); the mode buttons and the keys 1-4 (Arcs, Braid, Diagonals, Mixed) choose directly. The Tile slider (28-92 px) rebuilds the cloth with an intro wave. Shuffle (or the S key) launches a diagonal wave that re-turns about half the tiles. With no input, a slow wave keeps turning tiles. Reduced motion: no idle wave, turns are instant, fringe sway and sheen are stopped, but all interactions still work and the cloth is fully drawn.
