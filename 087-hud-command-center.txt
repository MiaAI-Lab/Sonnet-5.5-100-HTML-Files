==================== TITLE ====================
HELIX Orbital Console

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A fictional holographic operations console on a space-black field with a scrolling perspective grid floor and drifting particles. A three-column grid of chamfered, bracket-framed panels surrounds a large central instrument: SVG concentric rings (tick ring, dashed rings, segmented arcs with one amber warning segment, an animated 270-degree power gauge, bearing numerals, chevrons) with a canvas radar inside it whose sweep leaves a fading wedge, lights up moving blips and leaves short trails. On the left a drag-rotatable wireframe globe with twinkling city lights, an inclined orbit and the HELIX-7 marker sits above a target data panel. On the right are three glowing oscilloscope traces, a segmented spectrum analyser, a fuel gauge and a thrust-vector compass. A top bar shows UTC, mission elapsed time, jittering latency and sector; a bottom command line offers scan, lock, jump, status, help and clear with chip autocomplete. Panels flicker on in sequence at load. On mobile everything stacks with the radar first.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- clip-path chamfered panels built from a 1px outer polygon plus an inset polygon, with corner-bracket pseudo-elements and hover glow
- SVG instrument with JS-generated ticks and labels, rotating groups at different speeds, glow filter, stroke-dasharray arc gauge
- Canvas radar: layered fading sweep wedge, per-blip brightness decay after the sweep passes, position history trails, selection rings and animated amber targeting brackets
- Canvas globe: precomputed lat/long polylines rotated per frame, front/back-face alpha, city sprites with twinkle, drag rotation with inertia
- Three oscilloscope canvases with two-pass glow strokes; peak-hold segmented spectrum bars; segmented fuel gauge; smoothly steering compass needle
- Command interpreter with prefix and object-id autocomplete chips, Tab completion, timed log output
- Full-screen hyperspace star-streak canvas with acceleration, flash and regenerated contacts and sector after the jump
- CSS perspective grid floor animation, particle canvas, scanline and vignette overlay
- Flicker-in boot animation (stepped keyframes) with a boot log
- Optional WebAudio beeps, default off

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Hover any panel to brighten its frame. Drag the globe (mouse or touch) to rotate it; it keeps momentum. Click or tap a radar blip to select it and see its data in the Target panel. Type commands (or tap the chips): scan sends a radial wave and lists detected objects, lock (optionally with an object id) puts amber brackets on the selected or nearest contact, jump plays the hyperspace effect and moves to a new sector with new contacts, status prints a systems table, help lists commands, clear empties the log. Tab completes the top suggestion. The Audio button toggles WebAudio beeps (off by default). Reduced motion: no flicker boot, slower ring rotation, static floor, shorter jump. Animation pauses when the tab is hidden.
