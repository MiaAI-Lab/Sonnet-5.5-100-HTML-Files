==================== TITLE ====================
Wave Lab: Model RT-04

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A working ripple-tank simulator dressed as a piece of 1970s bench equipment. A 300 x 190 cell scalar wave equation is integrated with a leapfrog scheme (three sub-steps per frame, typed arrays) and drawn as a lit height field: bright phosphor amber crests (#ffb000 / #ff7a00) over deep brown-black troughs on #0a0704, upscaled smoothly inside a CRT-style tank with scanlines, glass glare, a power-on collapse animation, rulers in centimetres and corner screws. Beside it sits an engraved matte instrument rack: a preset bank, three knurled knobs (frequency, wavelength, damping) with lit tick rings, glowing phosphor readouts in recessed windows for lambda, f, c and T, a source list with per-source type, frequency multiplier, amplitude and phase, and a bench of mode, transport, slow-motion and view keys. Under the tank an oscilloscope traces the wave height at the probe (the pointer) with an edge trigger, auto scale, graticule, glow and phosphor persistence. Four presets show the classics: a plane wave through a double slit with a live intensity plot along the right edge, two in-phase point sources with calm hyperbolic nodal lines, a moving source showing the Doppler squeeze, and a parabolic mirror that focuses a plane wave onto its focus (click again to flip it into a point source at the focus). A switchable Water palette re-colours the field and the whole panel in teal and white. Copy is terse instrument-panel mono in capital letters, with the title WAVE LAB, MODEL RT-04.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- 2-D wave equation solved with an explicit leapfrog update h_next = h + (h - h_prev)(1 - damping) + c^2 * laplacian(h) on Float32Arrays (300 x 190), about 3 steps per frame driven by delta time, stability kept by clamping c = f * wavelength to 0.70 cell/step.
- Absorbing boundaries: a 24-cell quadratic sponge of extra damping plus matching display dimming; walls are Dirichlet cells stored as 0-255 coverage so hand-drawn and preset barriers keep anti-aliased edges.
- Sources are additive forcing with a Gaussian footprint (point) or a tapered column (plane wave), with gain derived from the 2-D and 1-D Green's functions so brightness stays constant when frequency or wavelength change; moving source with bounce for the Doppler preset.
- ImageData rendering: soft power-law tone mapping into a 256-entry phosphor LUT, gradient-based relief shading for a lit height-field look, packed 32-bit pixel writes, smooth upscale with a light persistence blend, then vector overlays (grid, sources with pulsing phase rings, probe brackets, focus mark, brush ring).
- Time-averaged intensity I(y) sampled on a screen column and drawn as a filled graph on the right edge of the tank (double slit).
- Oscilloscope on canvas: per-step sample ring buffer, rising-edge trigger, smoothed auto-scale, graticule with minor ticks, double-pass stroke with shadow blur and fade-out persistence.
- Instrument UI in pure CSS: engraved text shadows, inset bevels, lit LED lamps, knurled knobs from conic gradients, tick rings drawn in SVG, custom range sliders, runtime film grain from a small noise canvas, power-on scale animation.
- Knobs are real sliders (role=slider): vertical drag, wheel, arrows, PageUp/PageDown, Home/End, double-click to reset, with aria-valuetext in physical units.
- Preset generators for slits, parabola and sources; pre-roll of a few hundred steps so a developed interference pattern is visible on the first frame.
- Two complete palettes (amber and water) driven by CSS variables and canvas LUTs; responsive layout that reorders panels for phones.

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Pointer: move over the tank to place the scope probe and read X, Y and H; in Source mode click empty water to place a point source (up to four), drag a marker to move it, shift-click to remove it; Alt-click or press D to drop a pebble. Press W for Wall mode and drag to draw barriers, E for Erase mode to rub them out, S to return to Source mode. Presets (keys 1-4) rebuild the tank; pressing Reflection twice flips between a plane wave and a point source at the mirror's focus. Knobs: drag vertically, scroll the wheel, or use the arrow keys (Shift for coarse steps); the wavelength is clipped at the c = 0.70 speed limit and a lamp warns when that happens. In the source list toggle each source between point, line and moving, cycle its frequency multiplier (0.75x to 2x), set amplitude and phase with sliders, or delete it; the Add key places a new source without a pointer. Bench keys: Pause (Space), Step (.), Reset the field (R), Clear everything (C), speed 1x / 1/2x / 1/4x / 0.1x for slow motion, Grid (G), Screen intensity plot (I) and the Water/Amber palette switch (P). Touch works with the same gestures (the tank does not scroll the page). With prefers-reduced-motion the simulation starts at half speed and the power-on animation, flicker and phosphor persistence are removed, while every control keeps working.
