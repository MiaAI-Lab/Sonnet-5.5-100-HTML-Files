==================== TITLE ====================
Kintsugi Loader

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
On warm cream paper with faint fibre grain, a matte black-glaze bowl lies broken into six to nine shards that hover apart and turn slightly. As a load that stalls and bursts runs from 0 to 100% over about nine seconds, the shards slide home one by one with a soft overshoot while liquid gold fills the cracks along randomly ordered seams, each drawn as a tapered ribbon with a dark under-edge, a body gradient and a bright highlight thread, led by drifting star-shaped sparkles. Beside the bowl a large light-weight serif percentage counts up over a gold hairline, with poetic status lines that cross-fade. Every replay fractures the bowl differently. At 100% a bloom of light expands, a bright band sweeps over the gold only, the word "Restored" appears in gilded italic serif, steam curls rise from the rim and a Replay button appears; the seams keep glinting.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Runtime fracture: random seeds inside a convex bowl silhouette, Voronoi cells by half-plane clipping, then shared jagged edges cached per edge so neighbouring shards fit exactly
- Each shard is the same bowl artwork (glaze gradient, feTurbulence speckle, rim ellipse, interior, sheen, highlights) clipped by its own clipPath, with a bare-clay edge stroke
- Seams drawn as tapered filled ribbons revealed by an SVG mask whose stroke animates with stroke-dashoffset, in a random order tied to progress
- Piece motion tied to the same progress: each shard slides home before its first seam is filled, with out-back easing and idle drift while apart
- Uneven load curve of stalls and bursts built from random keyframes with smoothstep segments, click boost that triples the clock for a moment
- Sparkle pool of eight-point stars emitted at the leading edge of each seam, plus bursts on click and at completion
- Finish sequence: expanding light pulse, gradient band sweep masked to the seam shapes, gilded text clip, blurred SVG steam paths animated with dash offset
- System Didone/old-style serif stack in light weight, small-caps microcopy, paper-fibre grain from an inline SVG turbulence filter
- Responsive two-column to stacked layout, reduced-motion shortens the load to about 3.5 seconds

==================== INTERACTION ====================
The sequence starts by itself. Click or tap the bowl (or focus it and press Enter or Space) to hurry the gold: time runs about three times faster for a moment and a burst of sparkles leaves the pointer. Press R at any time, or use the Replay button after "Restored", to fracture a new bowl and start again. With prefers-reduced-motion the load takes about three and a half seconds, idle drift of the shards is removed and the steam is very slow.
