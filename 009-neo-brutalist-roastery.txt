==================== TITLE ====================
Grind Cult Roastery

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A loud, funny, fully working neo-brutalist storefront for the invented roaster GRIND CULT (a 15 kg drum named Brenda in a former tyre shop). Flat hard-yellow, hot-pink, cobalt, mint and off-white blocks sit behind 3-4px black borders and 6-14px hard offset shadows that physically press down on hover and active. The hero sets a poster-sized headline, "BURNT ON PURPOSE.", that is measured and fitted to its column whatever heavy display font the machine has (Impact / Arial Black, or a thickened Nimbus Sans Narrow fallback), beside a dotted sticker sheet holding nine hand-drawn SVG and CSS stickers that can be picked up, tilted, thrown and slapped down. Two tilted marquee bands cross under the hero. The Roast Dial section is an interactive Light-to-Dark slider with a striped roast-colour track and first/second-crack markers: it recolours a flat SVG coffee bean (wrinkles fade, oil sheen and smoke arrive, a POP! starburst fires at each crack), swaps six sets of flavour-note tags, animates four chunky cup-profile meters, updates drop temperature, roast time, weight loss and price, and adds the chosen roast to a working bag. The Brew Ratio Calculator links coffee grams, ratio and water millilitres in both directions with big steppers and preset chips, draws a filling measuring beaker, gives a mode-aware verdict, grind, temperature and time, builds a live pour plan and runs a brew clock that tells you what the scale should read. Six product bags flip in 3D on hover (or tap, or the Flip button) to reveal tasting notes and an add-to-bag button, followed by five "house rules" cards and a black footer with a fitted GRIND CULT wordmark and a newsletter form. The copy throughout is dry and specific, with real coffee vocabulary (first crack, drop temperature, natural vs washed, bloom, drawdown).

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Neo-brutalist system built from CSS custom properties: 4px borders, stacked hard box-shadows driven by an --o offset variable so every button, chip, tag and card "presses down" (translate + shadow collapse) on hover/active; black buttons use a paper-coloured second shadow so they stay legible on any colour.
- Runtime fit-to-width typography: the headline and footer wordmark are measured in JS and scaled to their columns; a canvas font probe detects a missing Impact/Arial Black and thickens the fallback with -webkit-text-stroke.
- Draggable stickers with Pointer Events and setPointerCapture: z-index raised on pick-up, random jitter rotation, velocity-based tilt while dragging, a stepped jitter animation, a "thunk" landing, fractional positions that survive resizes, arrow-key nudging for keyboard users and a Re-peel reset.
- Inline SVG sticker art (starburst polygons, ring text on circular textPaths whose textLength is set in JS, a rotating seal, a grumpy cup, a ticket tag) using filter:drop-shadow with zero blur for hard shadows on irregular shapes.
- Pure CSS double marquee: two crossing rotated bands running in opposite directions at different speeds, duplicated content for seamless loops, pause on hover.
- Roast dial: a custom-styled range input (WebKit and Firefox thumbs and stepped-colour track) that interpolates six bean-brown stops in JS and drives CSS variables on a clip-pathed SVG bean (base, shade crescent, wrinkles, crease, sheen, oil spots, smoke).
- Live bidirectional brew calculator (coffee, ratio, water) with a scaleY-animated beaker, mode detection (espresso / cold brew / strong / filter / gentle), a generated pour plan and a performance.now()-based brew clock with per-step scale targets.
- 3D card flips using perspective, preserve-3d and backface-visibility with visibility gating so hidden faces are never focusable; hover flip on pointer devices, tap and button flip elsewhere with aria-pressed.
- Slide-in bag drawer with quantity steppers, totals, scrim, Escape to close, toasts and a burst of SVG beans on add-to-bag; IntersectionObserver reveals and an intro sweep of the roast dial.
- Easter egg: triple-click the logo to rotate the section colour palette.

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Mouse and touch: drag any sticker around the sheet (it lifts, jitters and thunks down); press Re-peel to reset them. Drag the roast dial slider (or click one of the six mini beans) to change roast; crossing 1st and 2nd crack fires a POP! sticker; Add to bag puts a 250 g bag of the chosen roast in the drawer. In the calculator, type or step coffee grams, water millilitres or the ratio (or tap a preset chip) and every other value, the beaker, the verdict and the pour plan update; Start runs the brew clock and highlights the current step and target scale weight. Hover a product bag (desktop) or tap it / press its Flip button (touch and keyboard) to turn it over, then Add to bag or flip back. Open the Bag button in the header to review, change quantities or check out (a joke). Keyboard: every control is focusable with thick 4px outlines; stickers move with the arrow keys (Shift for bigger steps) and Enter tilts them; the range slider uses its native arrow / Home / End keys; Escape closes the bag. Triple-clicking the logo shuffles the palette. Reduced motion: marquees, wobbling and spinning stickers, flips, reveals and slam-in animations are switched off or reduced to instant, while every section still renders in its complete final state.
