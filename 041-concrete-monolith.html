Studio

A position, poured in one continuous take.

We do not decorate. We pour, we wait, and we let the light sign the wall. We do not decorate. We pour, we wait, and we let the light sign the wall.

I — MassWeigh what it means

A wall should weigh what it means. We size every section for the load it carries and the shadow it will throw, then we stop adding.

II — TimeLet it cure

Concrete is a slow material. We cast in one continuous take, let the formwork print its grain, and accept the weather as a co-author.

III — LightCut, never add

Openings are cut, not added. Each slot, oculus and fissure is placed by sun-study, hour by hour, before a single bar of steel is bent.
