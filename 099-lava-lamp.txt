==================== TITLE ====================
Lava Lamp 1972

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A 1970s living-room corner: vertically grooved wood panelling with grain, a canvas-drawn mustard shag rug with concentric retro rings, a striped "PEACE LOVE GROOVE" poster, a record-player silhouette with a slowly spinning disc on a walnut credenza, and a three-legged side table holding a tall tapered lava lamp with chrome cap and trumpet base. Eight wax blobs plus a pool at the bottom are a metaball field (sum of r^2/d^2 with a finite-support window) computed at 84x270 on a canvas, shaded with a gradient-derived normal for translucent lighter rims, under-light and a specular glint, then clipped to the glass shape. Buoyancy physics heat blobs at the bottom and cool them at the top. The bulb's wax-coloured glow washes the wall, floor and tabletop; a retro avocado control panel offers a chunky power switch, colour chips, a heat knob and a poke button.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Metaball field on a low-resolution canvas, per-pixel normal from the field gradient for rim light, translucency, specular and bulb under-light; CSS-upscaled for smooth edges
- Glass silhouette from a cubic Bezier profile sampled into a per-row half-width table used for both the pixel mask and the wall collisions
- Per-blob temperature model: heating near the bulb, cooling toward the top, buoyancy and drag; touch heat warms nearby blobs
- Layered SVG lamp: liquid gradient, bulb radial glow, glass edge gradients, blurred specular streaks, chrome cap and base with multi-stop metallic gradients
- Screen-blended radial glows for wall, floor and tabletop tinted by the selected palette, with subtle bulb flicker; a dim overlay darkens the room when the lamp is off
- Palette crossfade by exponential smoothing of all colour channels
- Shag rug painted once on a canvas from thousands of seeded curved fibre strokes sampled from ring colours
- Wall panelling from repeating gradients plus vertical-grain feTurbulence; poster with pattern-filled, textLength-fitted lettering; record disc animated with a squashed rotating group
- Drifting dust motes brightened near the lamp, animated film-grain overlay
- Retro toggle switch, knurled conic-gradient heat knob with keyboard support

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Flip the toggle (or press L): the bulb cools over several seconds, wax sinks and the room dims; flip it back to warm up again. Colour chips crossfade between Sunset, Lagoon and Grape. Drag the Heat knob (or use arrow keys) to change motion speed from Mellow to Volcanic. Click, tap or drag on the glass to warm nearby wax so blobs rise, with an expanding ripple; the "Give it a poke" button (P) warms all wax. Under prefers-reduced-motion the simulation runs slower, grain flicker stops and dust drifts gently. Animation pauses when the tab is hidden.
