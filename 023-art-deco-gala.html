THE MERIDIAN BALL

New Year's Eve 1929

The Evening

A night of gilt and glass

Let the old year take its bow in gold and glass,
and the new one waltz in on a river of champagne —
one last dance before the decade turns.

Eight hundred candles. One ballroom. An orchestra that has promised not to stop until the clock does. Come in from the cold; the doorman has been told to expect you.

The Programme

From dusk to dawn

  1. 8:00P.M.

    Doors & the Champagne Tower

    Coats are surrendered on the Marble Stair and the first coupe is poured from the crown of a fourteen-tier tower.

  2. 8:45P.M.

    The Grand Promenade

    Descend the emerald staircase into the Palm Court while the Meridian Orchestra plays its slowest, most flattering foxtrot.

  3. 9:30P.M.

    Supper in the Gilded Hall

    Seven courses: consommé à l'or, oysters on ice, roast squab, and a sorbet the exact colour of moonlight.

  4. 11:00P.M.

    The Cabaret

    Mademoiselle Odalys and the Sapphire Sisters, followed by a magician who is certainly not a fraud.

  5. 11:45P.M.

    The Last Waltz of 1929

    The chandeliers dim to a whisper. Take the hand nearest you. Do not look at the clock.

  6. 12:00Midnight

    The Stroke of Midnight

    A thousand corks, a rain of gold, one held breath — and then, all at once, nineteen-thirty.

  7. 1:00A.M.

    Champagne on the Roof

    Carry your glass up to the terrace and toast the first dawn of the new year with the whole city at your feet.

The Dress

Dress code

Black tie,jewels encouraged.

Gentlemen

White tie is welcomed. Bring cufflinks, gloves, and a pocket watch that runs a little fast.

Ladies

Beaded gowns, long gloves, feathers, headbands. The more it catches the light, the better.

Everyone

Leave the daylight at the coat check. No denim, no umbrellas, no hurry.
