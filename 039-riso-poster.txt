==================== TITLE ====================
Sundown Sessions Riso Poster

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A 3:4 three-drum risograph poster for a fictional open-air jazz night in Lisbon, sitting taped to a desk-grey surface beside a cream "press room" job ticket. Yellow, pink and blue are three separate multiply-blended layers on cream stock: a striped yellow sun whose lower half turns orange as pink halftone dots grow over it, a leaning saxophonist in a hat printed in blue with a golden yellow sax and a paper-white sticker outline, blue and pink palm fronds, stars, triangles, a squiggle, musical notes, a horizon and pier, a fat condensed SUNDOWN in pink (yellow shadow, blue outline) over SESSIONS in blue (yellow shadow), a masthead, lineup, DOORS 18:00 / FREE ENTRY highlight, a decorative QR block, registration crosses, paper fibres, ink speckles, a faint roller streak and a deckled edge. The console offers a Registration slider (Alignment 92% by default), three ink toggles, a Reprint button and a draggable sun.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Three absolutely stacked ink layers with mix-blend-mode: multiply over a paper layer inside an isolated stacking context, so overprints produce orange, green, purple and near-black.
- Halftones: one SVG path of thousands of dots per gradient (pink sun ramp, pink and yellow glow rings, blue dusk gradient, sax shading) at ink-specific screen angles (75°, 15°, 45°) generated by a small JS function; plus CSS radial-gradient dot fields clipped to a circle and a triangle.
- Riso stencil grain: a procedurally generated tileable drop-out mask per drum (canvas noise with cluster blur) applied as CSS mask-image; SVG feTurbulence filters give paper tooth and mottling; paper fibres, speckles and a deckled clip-path edge.
- One saxophonist drawn once in hidden defs and reused via use elements as knock-out (masks for the sun layers and blue layer), as blue ink and as yellow ink, with a fatter "ring" twin producing the paper outline; sun stripes are mask cut-outs.
- SVG text with textLength and spacingAndGlyphs so the condensed heavy title fills the poster width in any system font.
- Registration model: per-drum direction, rotation and amplitude scaled by the Alignment value, applied as transforms; each Reprint re-rolls the drift.
- Reprint via the Web Animations API: layers hidden, then yellow, pink, blue slide in with rotation and a top-down clip-path wipe while a translucent press bar sweeps the sheet.
- Sun dragging with a transparent focusable slider handle (pointer events, touch-safe, arrow keys, Home, double-click to glide back); knock-outs stay fixed while sun, glow and sky hole move.
- Registration crosses printed in all three inks that fan apart as drums drift.

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Drag the Registration slider from Sloppy to Perfect: the live readout ("Alignment 92%") and a witty note update while the three drums slide and rotate relative to each other; registration crosses split into colours. Toggle Yellow, Pink and Blue chips to lift or lay each ink. Press Reprint (Enter/Space/tap) to blank the sheet and print the drums one by one with a new random drift, with a live status line. Drag the sun anywhere in the sky (mouse or touch); focus it and use the arrow keys (Shift for bigger steps), Home or double-click to put it back. The layout stacks on phones. prefers-reduced-motion: the reprint shows each ink in sequence without sliding, wiping or easing, and the sun glides instantly.
