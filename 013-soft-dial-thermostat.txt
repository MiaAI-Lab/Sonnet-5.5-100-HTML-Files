==================== TITLE ====================
Halo Climate: Soft Dial Thermostat

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A single warm-grey control panel (#e6e2dc) in which every part, from the huge rotary dial to the smallest key, looks extruded or pressed from the same soft material with white top-left highlights and warm grey bottom-right shadows. The hero is a 540px SVG dial: an inset halo groove holds a luminous arc that fills from cool aqua through soft gold to sunrise orange as the target rises, a bead with a breathing glow rides the arc end, a 29-tick ring with numerals wraps the knurled knob, and the big number (for example 22.5 °C) sits in a carved readout well with a live "Heating / Cooling / Holding" status and the current room temperature. The whole page material drifts subtly from cool blue-grey at 16° to warm peach at 30° and dims further in Night mode. Around the dial sit a four-key Cool / Heat / Auto / Eco tray (the chosen key is pressed in), an inset twelve-hour outlook chart with a gradient temperature line, dotted forecast, aim line and hover read-out, embossed fan-speed and humidity sliders with a spinning fan and a filling droplet, Away and Night push-keys with lit LEDs, and an "Estimated cost today" figure that counts smoothly whenever any setting changes. The interface is a quiet time-lapse simulation (1 second equals 1 minute) so the room temperature actually creeps toward the setpoint.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Neumorphic shading system: paired light and dark box-shadows held in CSS custom properties, separate raised, pressed and inset recipes, convex versus concave 145-degree gradients and 1px inner highlights, so all controls share one light source.
- SVG rotary dial: an inner-shadow filter (feFlood, feComposite, feGaussianBlur, feOffset) carves the halo groove; 90 pre-coloured arc segments (aqua, gold, sunrise) are revealed through a mask whose round-capped path is driven by stroke-dashoffset with pathLength=1000, giving a true angular gradient with rounded ends.
- Filter-free glow: five widening, faint masked copies of the arc plus a CSS-animated radial halo on the bead, chosen so dragging stays cheap to repaint.
- Detent-magnetism function (flattened notches every 9.64 degrees) feeding a sub-stepped damped spring for the knob, plus flick inertia with friction and soft end-stops, then snap to 0.5-degree steps; a knurled conic-gradient rim rotates under a fixed satin sheen so the light never spins with the knob.
- Ambient tint pipeline: surface, highlight, shadow, track and accent colours are mixed in JS from the setpoint and the Night amount and written to CSS variables (throttled), so every panel surface shifts together.
- Twelve-hour chart drawn as generated SVG: temperature-mapped vertical gradient stroke, dotted forecast, self-scaling y-axis with smooth range morphing, now marker, and pointer scrubbing tooltip.
- Time-lapse physics: outdoor sine model, hysteresis HVAC controller (Eco widens the band), 8-hour forecast recomputed twice a second and eased into the chart, and a cost model that changes with target, mode, fan, humidity, Away and Night and is displayed through an exponential count-up.
- Rendering discipline: animating parts (knurl, dimple, caret, chart, fan, LEDs) sit on their own compositor layers; the grain texture is a 128px canvas-generated tile; intro uses wall-clock timing so it finishes even on slow devices.
- Accessibility: dial exposed as role=slider with aria-valuetext, roving-tabindex radiogroup for modes, role=switch keys, focus-visible rings, live region for mode changes, 4.5:1 label contrast on the grey surface.
- Opt-in WebAudio: a faint pitched tick per 0.5-degree step and a soft low thock for keys, created only after the sound button is pressed, master gain 0.16; state saved to localStorage inside try/catch.

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Drag the knob (mouse or touch) to turn it: it moves relative to your finger, follows through soft notches every 0.5 degrees, and a flick keeps it turning with inertia until friction and the end stops settle it on a notch; tapping the outer ring jumps the arc there. With the dial focused, arrow keys nudge 0.5 degrees, PageUp and PageDown move 2 degrees, Home and End go to 16 and 30; the mouse wheel steps too. Each step tints the panel, updates the big number, arc, ticks, forecast and cost. The four mode keys behave as a radio group (arrows move between them). The Away and Night keys depress on press and stay pressed with an LED lit; Away holds the room at 17 degrees (27.5 in Cool mode) and Night eases the aim by 1.5 degrees while darkening the surface. The fan and humidity sliders drag, tap or use arrow keys; the fan icon spins faster with speed and the droplet fills with humidity. Hover or drag across the chart for a time and temperature read-out. The speaker button (default off) enables the tick sounds. Clicking the halo logo runs a calibration sweep of the dial. With prefers-reduced-motion the spring, inertia, sweep, fan spin, LED pulse and bead breathing are turned off or made instant, and the page remains fully usable.
