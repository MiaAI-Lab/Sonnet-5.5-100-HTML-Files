==================== TITLE ====================
Line & Loop: Monoline Alphabet Lab

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A bold editorial type specimen for a hand-built monoline alphabet. Forty-one glyphs (A–Z, 0–9 and . , ! ? -, with lowercase sharing the capitals) are written as SVG path data on a 100-unit em, capitals 70 units tall between a cap line at y 15 and a baseline at y 85, made only of straight strokes and circular or elliptical arcs. The page opens on a huge left-aligned hero line, "Hello, gorgeous" by default, whose letters draw themselves stroke by stroke while a coral ink dot rides each pen tip, over hairline cap and baseline guides that carry tiny margin labels and a slow scanning highlight. Under it sit a text field and a row of custom controls (stroke weight, spacing, slant, wobble, terminal shape, three colour schemes, guides, replay and Copy SVG). Below the lab come a seven-column glyph grid with construction circles that replays each character when you hover it, a strip of the word LOOP at six weights from hairline to heavy, an inverted colour band that sets the pangram "Sphinx of black quartz, judge my vow" while an alphabet of 26 chips lights up, and a colophon with live statistics. Section headings are themselves drawn in the face; all interface text is uppercase, tracked system monospace. The default palette is ink blue #1b2a6b on cream #f7f1e6 with coral #ff6b4a; the other schemes are coral on plum and mint on forest.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Hand-authored glyph data: every character is a short list of stroke paths (lines, circular and elliptical arcs) plus dots, with per-glyph skeleton width, optical side-bearings (2 to 9 units) and construction circles; a kerning table of 35 diagonal and open-letter pairs plus 0.7 times the stroke weight keeps heavy weights from colliding; letter forms use consistent radii (17 to 46) and cap height so the set reads as one family.
- Layout engine in JS: uppercase mapping, word wrapping to a unit width, 118-unit line pitch, viewBox fitting that also accounts for slant and wobble overhang, and scale-to-container with a viewport-height cap.
- Stroke draw-on with stroke-dashoffset on paths normalised by pathLength=1 (dash pattern "1 2.2" so round caps never leave a dot), driven by a small requestAnimationFrame engine: strokes of a glyph run in writing order, glyphs are staggered, dots pop in, and a per-glyph pen dot follows getPointAtLength.
- Style through CSS custom properties: stroke width, terminal (stroke-linecap), joins (round, or miter with miterlimit 2 so acute corners bevel), slant as skewX around the baseline, and three colour schemes switched with a data attribute and cross-faded; no re-render is needed for most changes.
- Wobble: each stroke is resampled every 2.2 units with getPointAtLength, cached per glyph, then displaced with two-dimensional value noise (a different seed for every letter) and re-evaluated at 8 fps so the line boils like hand-inked animation.
- Play on the specimen: pointer position hit-tests glyph boxes; hovering bounces the letter and its neighbours with the Web Animations API, dragging across letters erases them (reverse dash) and redraws them, and an idle wave ripples across the hero every few seconds.
- Editorial specimen composition: hairline rules, coral cap and baseline guides with margin labels, a glyph grid with construction rings, weight strip, inverted pangram band with alphabet chips, computed colophon, uppercase mono small-caps UI, draw-on and fade reveals triggered by IntersectionObserver.
- Copy SVG builds standalone markup (xmlns, viewBox, baked colour, weight, terminals, slant and the current wobble) and writes it with the Clipboard API, falls back to a hidden textarea and execCommand, and finally reveals a selectable textarea with instructions.
- Custom-styled native controls (range sliders with filled tracks, radio-style segmented control and colour swatches with arrow-key navigation), visible focus rings, aria labels, live status text and prefers-reduced-motion handling (no draw-on, bounce, boil, pens or scan line).

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Type in the field (up to 48 characters; unsupported characters are skipped with a note) and the hero re-sets itself, drawing only the letters you changed. Drag the sliders to change stroke weight (2 to 16 units), spacing, slant (±16°) and wobble (0 to 100%); pick round, square or butt terminals and one of three colour schemes; toggle the guides; press Replay (or Enter in the field) to redraw the whole line; press Copy SVG to copy the markup, with a visible confirmation or a selectable fallback. Move the mouse over the hero and letters bounce gently, their neighbours following; press and drag across letters to erase and redraw them (on touch, tap to bounce and drag to erase). If you leave the hero alone a soft wave runs through it every few seconds and a coral highlight scans along the guide lines. Hover (or tap) any cell of the glyph grid to replay how that glyph is drawn and to light up its construction circles. Scrolling draws in the section headings, the glyph grid, the weights strip and the pangram, whose alphabet chips light up as each letter is written. With prefers-reduced-motion everything appears at once without draw-on, bounce, boil, pen dots or scanning highlight, and all controls still work.
