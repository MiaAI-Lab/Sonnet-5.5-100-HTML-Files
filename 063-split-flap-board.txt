==================== TITLE ====================
Split-Flap Departures Board

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A matte black Solari-style departure board hung from two steel rods, with a chunky bevelled frame, rivets and a printed condensed "DEPARTURES / Terminal 0" sign. Ten fictional flights (TIME, FLIGHT, DESTINATION, GATE, STATUS) are made of individual split-flap modules with a hinge line, pins and inner shadow, warm white letters on charcoal, and amber, green or red status words. A message line above the flights, a flipping local clock, a brass "Board a message" plaque and a sliding detail plate with a cross-shaped gate map and flip-digit boarding countdown complete the piece. On power-on the modules ripple into place; afterwards a random row rewrites every few seconds, each cell cycling through the drum toward its letter.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Per-cell split-flap built from four halves: static top and bottom halves, plus a falling top flap and a rising bottom flap rotated with rotateX inside a perspective parent, with backface hiding and angle-driven shading
- A JS-driven flip scheduler on requestAnimationFrame: each cell steps through its character drum with staggered per-cell delays, gravity-style easing, a slower final flip and lazily created flap elements
- Glyphs drawn via attr(data-c) pseudo-elements clipped to each half, so no per-half text nodes
- WebAudio clack: short band-passed noise bursts plus a low thump, batched per frame by how many flaps landed, master gain capped, off by default behind a brass speaker button
- Live clock and boarding countdown that reuse the same flap modules and flip every second
- Message plaque: brass gradients and engraved inset input; messages are centred, sanitised, paginated by word and flipped in cell by cell
- Row inspection through invisible full-row buttons, an amber selection bar, a grid-rows animated drawer and a generated SVG terminal map with a marching dashed route
- Board logic that keeps times ascending, weights statuses by row, blinks FINAL CALL and GATE CHANGE, and updates an aria-live summary plus a hidden table
- Responsive grid: rows reflow to two lines and the FLIGHT column moves down on phones, with cell size derived from the viewport by clamp()/calc()

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Watch the board update on its own, or click/tap/focus any row (keyboard: Tab to a row, Enter) to highlight it and slide out the detail plate with gate map, walking time and countdown; click again or press Close to dismiss. Type a message into the brass plaque (or pick a suggestion) and press Board it: the top line flips to spell it, paging through long messages. The brass speaker button toggles the clacking sound (default off). A visually hidden aria-live region announces each update. With prefers-reduced-motion the flips get shorter and the drawer and marching route animations stop.
