==================== PROMPT ====================
Build "Ebru — Marbling Studio", an interactive paper-marbling toy on canvas. Implementation guidance: represent each ink drop as a polygon of ~180 points; when a new drop of radius r is added at centre c, every existing point p is displaced radially by p' = c + (p − c)·sqrt(1 + r²/|p − c|²) (the classic mathematical-marbling formula), then the new circle is appended; a comb/rake stroke (drag) displaces points along the drag direction with exponential falloff, p' = p + α·exp(−d/λ)·dir; a swirl tool rotates neighbours around the pointer. Render polygons in draw order with filled colours; re-subdivide long edges to keep curves smooth and cap total points (~8k). Palette: cream paper #f4ecda, indigo #23306b, vermilion #d8452b, turquoise #1f9a9a, ochre #d9a441, blush #e9b7a5, black-green #16302b, with subtle paper fibre texture and a thin gilded border. Type: a refined old-style serif for the small title "Ebru" and tracked small-caps tool labels. UI: a slim dock at the bottom with pigment swatches, a tool toggle (Drop / Comb / Swirl), "Clear tray" and "New pattern" (seeds a beautiful preset). Click to drop ink (radius grows with hold time); drag with Comb to pull lines through the paint. An automatic demo composes a preset in ~6 s with gentle comb strokes, then invites the visitor to play. Keep it at 60 fps.

No external dependencies of any kind: no CDN, no external fonts (system font stacks only), no external images, no external SVG files, no libraries, no network requests. It must be responsive from 360px phones to large desktops, validate as standard HTML5, respect prefers-reduced-motion, support mouse and touch input, and run without console errors. ==================== TITLE ====================
Ebru Marbling Studio

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A quiet bench-top studio for paper marbling. A cream sheet with a hand-painted fibre texture sits in a thin gilded frame (double gold rim, hairline inset rule, four engraved corner ornaments) on a deep black-green desk; a small italic "Ebru" title (from the Persian ebr, "cloud") and a live hint sit above it, and a slim cream dock below carries seven pigment pots, a Drop / Comb / Swirl toggle, a size slider, Undo, Clear tray, New pattern and Save. The ink is real mathematical marbling: every drop is a polygon that starts as a 180-point circle and grows under the visitor's finger, pushing all existing ink outward with the area-preserving radial formula; a multi-tine comb drags ink along its motion with exponential falloff across the tines; a swirl twists nearby ink around the pointer. Long edges are re-subdivided with the 4-point interpolatory scheme and the total is capped near 8,000 points. On load the studio composes one of three traditional-style patterns by itself in about six seconds (Gel-Git with two combing passes, Çarkıfelek with concentric rings stirred into wheels, Battal with a scatter of stones) and then invites the visitor to take over. Everything is drawn flat with a crisp feathered outline, so the palette of indigo, vermilion, turquoise, ochre, blush, black-green and cream reads like ink on cotton paper.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Mathematical marbling engine: drops as polygons; p' = c + (p - c)*sqrt(1 + r^2/|p - c|^2) applied to all existing points; live growth while held by pushing with the incremental area (r_new^2 - r_old^2), so neighbours flow away as the drop swells.
- Comb model: (2*half+1) tines with spacing S, displacement m * 0.7 * exp(-d/lambda) * Gaussian envelope along the stroke, applied in sub-steps of 9 px with a smoothed axis, so the comb overtakes the ink and leaves clean arches instead of bulldozing it; area-preserving twist map for the swirl.
- Adaptive geometry: 4-point interpolatory subdivision of edges longer than 5 px, curvature-aware decimation whenever the total exceeds 8,000 points.
- WebGL renderer using stencil-then-cover fills (arbitrary polygons, no triangulation) plus feathered triangle-strip outlines (a soft meniscus pass and a crisp thin line) for smooth edges without MSAA; automatic Canvas 2D fallback if WebGL is unavailable; context-loss handling.
- Scripted "director" that plays timed drop, comb and swirl actions with easing; three recipe generators with curated palettes and layouts that adapt to landscape or portrait trays; reduced-motion mode fast-forwards to the finished sheet.
- Paper made from CSS radial-gradient mottling plus one seamless fibre tile painted on a small canvas and repeated; gilded frame, inset rule and SVG corner ornaments; desk grain from a generated noise tile.
- Tool cursors as a light SVG overlay (tinted drop ring, tine row that rotates with the stroke, rotating spiral) that costs nothing per frame.
- Undo history of point-array snapshots, a skimming "Clear tray" wipe with a cream edge, and Save as PNG composited from paper, ink and fibres.
- Roving-tabindex radio groups, keyboard shortcuts and a keyboard-driven virtual brush (arrow keys move, Space held drops, combs or swirls).

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Press (mouse, finger or pen) on the sheet with the Drop tool: a drop of the chosen pigment blooms under the pointer and keeps growing while held (the longer the hold, the wider the drop), pushing the surrounding ink away; release to set it. Switch to Comb and drag: a rake of nine tines pulls the ink into feathers along the stroke direction. Switch to Swirl and press-and-hold (optionally move): the ink is stirred into a spiral, and the spin direction alternates with each press. The Size slider (or the mouse wheel, or [ and ]) changes drop size, tine gap or swirl width for the active tool. Pick pigments from the seven pots (or keys 1-7), tools with D, C, S; Z or the Undo button steps back; Clear tray skims the sheet clean with a moving edge; New pattern (or N) composes the next pattern (Gel-Git, Çarkıfelek, Battal) with fresh palettes and layouts; the down-arrow button saves the sheet as a PNG. Keyboard: Tab to the sheet, use the arrow keys to move a virtual brush (Shift for big steps) and hold Space or Enter to drop, comb or swirl. The first touch anywhere on the sheet ends the automatic demo and hands over control. With prefers-reduced-motion the demo pattern appears instantly, the skim is instant and interface transitions are near-zero, while all tools still work.
