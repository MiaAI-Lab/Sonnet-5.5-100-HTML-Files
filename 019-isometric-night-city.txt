==================== TITLE ====================
Lumen District

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A floating 12 x 12 isometric city block drawn entirely from SVG polygons: nine extruded buildings (an office tower with antenna, terracotta housing with a rooftop garden, a domed library, a neon-signed hotel, a striped-awning cafe, a market with a steaming vent, lofts with a water tank, a ceramics works with a smoking chimney and a glass conservatory), a park of round trees and pines, a road cross with zebra crossing, a tram line with overhead wire crossing a river on a small bridge, street lamps, and cars with headlights and tail-lights. The slab shows layered ground strata and a river notch. A pill switch flips the whole scene between a violet-and-pink night, with lit windows in shifting patterns, lamp cones, headlight beams, glowing neon, stars and a rising moon, and a cream-and-sky morning with terracotta and olive materials, soft long shadows, clouds and a low sun. A hot-air balloon drifts, birds cross the sky, and steam curls from vents.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- A 2:1 dimetric projection helper P(x,y,z) plus box / face helpers generates every polygon; all faces follow one light rule (top lightest, left mid, right shaded and cooler) computed in HSL from each building's day and night base colour, with the same rim highlights on every prism.
- Painter's-order depth sorting by pairwise footprint comparison and topological sort, re-run as cars and the tram move.
- Day/night crossfade by JS-interpolated colour tokens (sky gradient stops, ground, water, strata, building faces, foliage) written to CSS variables over 1.7 s, with lamp cones, pools, beams, windows, neon, stars and moon fading via a shared night value.
- Cast shadows built as convex hulls of a building footprint and its offset copy, visible by day only.
- Windows as dark + lit polygon pairs; random toggles, a rolling floor sweep and a few flickering panes, each fading by CSS transition.
- Cars follow polyline paths (including turns) with per-direction pre-built shapes whose head- or tail-lights sit on the correct visible face; a tram shuttles over the bridge and dwells at each end.
- Neon signs skewed onto the building face with an affine matrix and a glow filter, with an irregular CSS flicker; steam puffs, beacon lights, flapping birds, drifting clouds and balloon.
- Hover lifts a building 8 px over a soft footprint shadow using a static hit polygon (no flicker), with an HTML info tag placed from the building's screen rectangle; roof gardens and antennas grow with a spring transform.
- Keyboard and touch: buildings are focusable buttons; on touch the first tap selects and the second toggles.

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Hover (or focus with Tab) a building to lift it and read its name, height and use; click, tap again on touch, or press Enter/Space to grow or remove its rooftop garden or antenna. The sun/moon switch or the N key changes between night and day with a smooth transition. Cars, tram, balloon, birds and steam run on their own. With prefers-reduced-motion the transition is instant, vehicles rest mid-road, and steam, birds, balloon and flicker stop.
