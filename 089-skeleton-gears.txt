==================== TITLE ====================
Calibre 07 — Skeleton

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A large inline-SVG skeleton watch movement on a bronze sunburst plate inside a gold case with an engraved Roman-numeral minute ring. A JS generator builds every wheel outline from module and tooth count (involute-like teeth, rim, hub, spoke windows), and each wheel pair is phase-aligned so teeth sit in gaps. The train runs barrel (64T) to centre wheel (60T, minute hand) to third (64T) to fourth (64T, seconds subdial) to a 15-tooth escape wheel, driven by a pallet fork with ruby pallets and a balance wheel with a pulsing blued hairspring at 4 Hz. Hands show real local time; the seconds hand ticks in quarter-second steps with an impulse bounce. Polished Geneve-striped bridges, a power-reserve subdial, slotted steel screws, ruby jewels in gold chatons, ambient shadows and a sapphire-glass highlight finish it.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- JS tooth-path generator (involute-like flank profile, asymmetric ratchet teeth for escape and ratchet wheels) with skeleton spoke cut-outs via evenodd paths
- Mesh phase solver: every meshing pair gets an offset so a tooth of one wheel meets a gap of the other at all times, with exact gear-ratio angles
- Fixed-light bevels: rotating outlines clipped by fixed light/dark half-planes so highlights do not spin with the wheel
- Fixed brass gradient plus radial sheen clipped to each rotating gear shape
- Escapement animation: fork flips at each balance zero-crossing, escape wheel advances half a tooth per beat, balance swings +-250 deg at 4 Hz, hairspring breathes
- Quarter-second seconds tick with damped-cosine impulse bounce, blended into continuous spin when the crown is turned
- Crown drag time-setting with eased offset; slow-motion time lag that catches up by spinning forward
- Sunburst and guilloche engraving generated in JS; Roman numerals and minute ticks with engraved dark/light double text
- Blued-steel faceted dauphine hands with offset shadows; ruby jewels, gold chatons, slotted screws
- Train-chain hover highlight with dashed sapphire overlay and floating label

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Hover (or focus / tap) any wheel to dim the rest and trace the going train with a dashed sapphire line and a label such as "Escape wheel · 15 teeth · 28,800 vph". Drag the gold crown on the right up or down (or use arrow keys when focused, Shift for larger steps) to set the time; gears spin rapidly forward or back and the hands follow. "Slow-motion 0.1x" slows the movement so the pallet fork and escape wheel can be watched, and releasing it spins the train forward to catch up to real time. "Wind the barrel" spins the ratchet wheel and refills the power-reserve gauge; low reserve reduces balance amplitude. Under prefers-reduced-motion the bounce, sweep and balance amplitude are reduced and time changes snap.
