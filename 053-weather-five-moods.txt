==================== TITLE ====================
Five Skies · Port Marlow

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A weather almanac for one invented harbour town, built as a single frosted-glass dashboard whose whole personality changes with the sky. A tall hero card holds a living canvas illustration of the current weather with a huge ultra-light temperature numeral (18°), the condition, a witty "feels like" line and a discreet hint for that scene; a floating segmented pill straddles its lower edge and switches Clear, Rain, Snow, Storm and Fog. Clear is a warm afternoon over the water with a rotating-ray sun, pointer-steered lens flare, drifting clouds, a glittering sea, sailboats, gulls and a striped lighthouse. Rain is a bokeh-lit rooftop skyline seen through a wet window: soft out-of-focus lights, angled streaks in three depths that splash on the wet quay, and droplets on the glass that act as tiny inverted lenses of the sharp scene and can be made to run. Snow is a pale blue valley with fir forests, a cabin with a warm window and smoking chimney, a glowing lamp post, ground sparkles and three depths of swirling flakes. Storm is a churning violet night with fractal cloud decks, procedurally forked lightning that floods the hero, page and glass with light, a delayed "thunder" word with flash-to-bang distance, wind-swept rain and a lighthouse whose beam sweeps the sea. Fog is a layered woodland of silhouette firs and broadleaf trees dissolving into drifting mist bands, a diffuse sun with soft shafts, out-of-focus foreground trunks and an occasional foghorn. Every colour of the interface (accent, tint, ink, glass, page gradient, shadows) glides between the five palettes through registered CSS custom properties. Beneath the hero sit a 12-hour strip with a hand-smoothed temperature curve that draws itself, a five-day forecast with temperature-coloured hi/lo range bars, a wind compass whose needle springs to a new bearing and wobbles with the gusts, a wave-filled humidity droplet, a UV scale with a sliding thumb, and a sunrise/sunset arc along which the sun glides. All data is illustrative and each sky is a different fictional day.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Registered CSS custom properties (@property with <color> syntax) declared on :root so accent, tint, ink, glass, gradient, scrim, vignette and shadow colours transition smoothly when data-sky changes; colour-mix() for glass, blooms and pills
- Frosted-glass cards (backdrop-filter blur + saturate, hairline borders, inner highlight) over drifting colour blooms, with a frame-time governor that starts in a flat "lite" mode and only enables the blur and bloom animation once frames prove fast (falls back again on slow machines)
- Five hand-coded Canvas 2D scenes sharing sprite caches, seeded RNG and a time-based crossfade between skies (buffer canvas blended with eased alpha), running on delta-time with a clamped step and paused when hidden or off-screen
- Rain droplets as cached inverted-lens sprites sampled from the sharp scene buffer, a downscaled blurred copy for the out-of-focus world, ~14 Hz world refresh, splash pool with parabolic droplets and sliding drops with trails
- Procedural lightning by recursive midpoint displacement with branching, drawn in three additive glow passes with a double-flash envelope, radial screen illumination, a CSS page-wide flash layer and a delayed thunder caption computed from the flash-to-bang delay
- Periodic fractal value-noise (5 octaves) baked at low resolution into seamless scrolling storm cloud decks, with a lightning-lit twin sprite crossfaded during strikes
- Procedural fir and broadleaf silhouettes, layered parallax tree rows with pointer parallax, drifting fog strips generated from soft elliptical gradients
- Inline SVG widgets: Catmull-Rom smoothed temperature path with stroke-dash draw-in and gradient area, dial with generated ticks and a spring-transformed wobbling needle, clip-path droplet with two sloshing wave layers, gradient UV scale, sun arc positioned with getPointAtLength and tweened
- Symbol sprite of hand-drawn weather icons, temperature-to-colour gradient stops for range bars, tweened numerals and unit-aware formatting (°C/°F, km/h/mph, proper minus sign)
- System font stacks only, light-weight (200) display numerals, tabular figures, responsive layout with clamp(), grid and a horizontally scrollable hourly strip on phones

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Pick a sky with the segmented control (real radio inputs, so arrow keys work) or press 1 to 5; the canvas crossfades over about a second while the colours, numerals, hourly curve, forecast bars, compass needle, humidity level, UV thumb and sun position all animate to the new day. The °C/°F toggle in the header converts every temperature and switches wind between km/h and mph. A "Tour" button cycles the five skies every few seconds. Inside the hero each sky reacts to the pointer or finger: Clear steers the sun and its lens flare, Rain sets nearby droplets running down the glass, Snow is pushed sideways by a sweep or a click, Storm calls down a bolt wherever you click and then shows the delayed thunder word, Fog parallax-drifts the trees. Hovering the hourly strip highlights an hour and the strip scrolls sideways on phones. With prefers-reduced-motion the whole scene runs at about a third of the speed, lightning is dimmed and rarer, and transitions shorten, but every scene still looks complete. Animation pauses when the tab is hidden or the hero is scrolled out of view.
