01 — Why Wanderlight

Comfort, at a few hundred million kilometres.

We built the ship around the part of the trip you’ll actually remember: the window, the weight of your own body, and the way home.

  • Every seat is a window

    Curved aerogel panes, two metres across, tuned to soften the sun’s edge without dimming a single star. Nobody on Wanderlight has ever asked to close the shade.

    2.0 m panoramic glass

  • Gravity, your way

    Spin-decks let you dial anywhere from 0.2 g to a full 1 g. Sleep heavy, dine light, and float through the observation ring the moment the view demands it.

    0.2 g — 1.0 g on demand

  • A ticket home, always

    Every itinerary carries a guaranteed return leg, a fourteen-day acclimatisation stay at Kestrel Station, and a Wayfinder who knows your name and how you take your tea.
