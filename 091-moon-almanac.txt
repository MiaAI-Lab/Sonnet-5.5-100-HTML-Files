==================== TITLE ====================
Lunar Almanac

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A three-column almanac on a midnight-indigo sky of twinkling canvas stars. In the centre a giant canvas moon (procedural maria, several hundred craters, a ray crater, limb darkening, halo, gentle libration) is shaded for the chosen date with a true terminator ellipse and faint bluish earthshine, ringed by a 30-tick lunation dial with a rust marker at the current age. The left column gives the phase name and story, an info card (moon age, illumination, next full moon, approximate distance) and the traditional full-moon name of the month. The right column is a Monday-first month grid of one accurate mini SVG moon per day with today marked, plus the approximate dates and times of the month's four primary phases. A flip-book slider scrubs the whole year. At the foot of the page a wolf silhouette sits on a pine ridge and tilts its head to howl whenever the moon is full.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Astronomy from the mean synodic month (29.530588853 d) and epoch JD 2451550.1; illumination = (1 - cos(2 pi age / S)) / 2
- Primary phases from mean lunation offsets (0, 1/4, 1/2, 3/4) listed for the displayed month; next full moon and approximate distance from an anomalistic-month cosine model
- Procedural moon texture on an offscreen canvas: maria blotches, 500+ radial-gradient craters with rims, ray system, grain
- Terminator drawn as a half-disc plus an ellipse of width |cos(phase)| times the radius, blurred and composited over the texture; earthshine via screen blend
- Mini-moon SVGs using the same lit-path geometry, mirrored for waning phases
- Smooth phase morph by easing the Julian date toward the target so the terminator sweeps naturally, including year flip-book scrubbing
- Libration by slowly drifting and rotating the texture inside a fixed clip
- Twinkling canvas star field with an occasional shooting star
- Inline SVG wolf silhouette with a CSS-transitioned head tilt and a drifting Awoo caption; ridge with generated pines
- High-contrast serif small caps with mono numerals; hairline cards with backdrop blur

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Hover a day in the grid (or tab to it) to preview that date on the giant moon; click or tap to select it. Left and right arrow keys step one day, the month arrows move between months, and "Today" jumps back to the present. The "Flip through the year" slider scrubs day by day through the whole year while the phase animates. Clicking or activating the wolf makes it howl on demand, and it howls by itself (head tilt, caption, halo pulse) when the shown moon is within a day of full. Stars and libration slow down under prefers-reduced-motion and the morph becomes near-instant.
