II  ·  Calibre VC‑01

The movement

Three hundred and twelve parts, assembled twice — first to be perfected, then to be forgotten. Every bridge is bevelled by hand at forty-five degrees; every screw is heated to the exact second it turns blue.

III  ·  Particulars

Everything we will tell you

  • Case39.5mm

    Eighteen-carat champagne gold, hand-polished. Sapphire crystal front and back; water resistant to thirty metres, which is to say rain, not oceans.

  • Thickness8.4mm

    Slim enough to vanish beneath a cuff, and to be forgotten there until someone asks the time.

  • CalibreVC‑01

    Hand-wound, made and finished in-house. Three hundred and twelve parts, thirty-three rubies, no rotor to hide behind.

  • Frequency4Hz

    Twenty-eight thousand eight hundred vibrations an hour. Free-sprung balance with a Breguet overcoil.

  • Power reserve88hours

    Twin barrels. Wind it on Monday morning and it will still be running on Thursday.

  • Edition40a year

    Each numbered on the mainplate and signed by the watchmaker who set it going.
