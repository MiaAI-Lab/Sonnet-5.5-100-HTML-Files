==================== TITLE ====================
Herbarium Plates, Volume I

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A scrolling herbarium editorial on cream laid paper with fibres and foxing. A title spread sets a huge Didone "Herbarium" beside a self-inking olive sprig; five plates follow (fern, ginkgo, olive, poppy, lavender), each a sheet with a fine double border, Roman plate numeral, italic-caps Latin name, small-caps common name, a scale bar, a short description and handwritten margin notes. The drawings are generated procedurally in SVG but read as hand-inked: tapered outlines, midribs, veins, hatching, crinkled petals, hairy stems and whorled florets. When a plate scrolls into view the strokes draw themselves, then a watercolour wash (sage, olive, poppy red, lavender, ochre) blooms outward with soft, displaced edges and a slight misregistration. A sticky index of five dots tracks progress, and an illustrated colophon shows the watercolour tin with the five pigments.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Seeded procedural generators per plant: recursive fronds with lobed, tapered pinnae, fan leaves with forking veins, lance leaves in pairs, polar-curve crinkled petals, whorled floret rings
- Tapered outlines built as ribbons and leaf profiles from centrelines, with Catmull-Rom smoothing and slight jitter for a hand-inked line
- Stroke drawing with pathLength=1 and stroke-dashoffset driven by requestAnimationFrame, strokes merged by weight into a few hundred paths
- Watercolour: fills through an SVG filter (feTurbulence + feDisplacementMap + blur + grain mask), darker pigment-edge strokes, multiply blend, offset from the ink, and a radial soft-edged mask that grows via a registered --r property
- Paper drawn once on canvas: fibres, foxing freckles and tea-stained edges used as data-URL layers
- Hover or tap a part to highlight it and draw a leader line with its botanical name (pinnule, sorus, calyx, drupe, stigmatic disc and more) using invisible hit shapes
- IntersectionObserver builds plates lazily and starts drawing when they are mostly visible; sticky side index with progress line
- Old-style serif italics, small caps, a Didone display and a chancery hand for margin notes; illustrated colophon tin in inline SVG

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Scroll through the spread; each plate inks itself and then blooms with colour. Rest the pointer on (or tap) any part of a drawing to see it highlighted with a leader-line label. Use "Redraw plate" to replay the inking. The right-hand index dots (bottom pill on phones) jump between plates and show progress. Keyboard users can tab to the index and redraw buttons. With prefers-reduced-motion the plates appear fully drawn and washed without animation.
