==================== TITLE ====================
The Tea Wheel

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A cream-paper field guide to tea flavour built around a three-ring SVG sunburst: six tea families (White, Green, Oolong, Black, Pu-erh, Herbal) in celadon, jade, amber, brick, umber and plum; 24 flavour groups in a 34% tint; and 67 specific tasting notes such as Fresh-cut hay, Nori, Toasted chestnut and Orchid in a 60% tint. Arcs have 2px gaps and rounded corners and carry radial labels. A serif title block, search field, colour legend and a hand-drawn teapot outline sit on the left. The wheel's hub is a centre card showing the hovered name, a one-line tasting description, the tea's brew temperature and time and a small cup icon that fills with the liquor colour; a ring around it counts down a steeping timer. Clicking a family or flavour group zooms that branch to fill the whole wheel, with a Back button. Illustrative-data notes appear discreetly.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Sunburst generated from a nested data tree; angular widths proportional to the note count; every arc is an SVG path built from annular-sector maths with constant 2px gaps (asin offsets) and rounded corners (inset shape plus same-colour round-join stroke).
- Ring tints computed in JS by mixing each tea colour with the paper colour; label colour chosen by relative luminance.
- Radial labels rotated and flipped to stay upright, with font size fitted to arc length and ring thickness (hidden when they cannot fit), recomputed each frame while zooming.
- Zoom transitions: all arc angles animated with cubic easing toward a remapped target (root branch becomes 360°, its ancestors become full rings, other branches collapse and fade); Back returns one level.
- Lineage highlighting: hovered/focused/pinned node plus ancestors and descendants stay lit while everything else dims; search highlights matching notes and their ancestors with a glow.
- Centre card with animated cup icon (clipped liquid rect scaling in the tea's liquor colour), brewing line and a steeping ring (stroke-dashoffset) driven by requestAnimationFrame, plus a x20 demo speed and a soft WebAudio chime.
- Steam wisps: three SVG paths rising and fading while any segment is hovered.
- Hand-drawn teapot outline drawn on with stroke-dashoffset, lid that clatters on hover, click for rotating brewing tips.
- Keyboard model: arcs are focusable with roving arrow navigation (left/right siblings, up parent, down child), Enter to zoom or pin, Escape/Backspace to go back, "/" focuses search.
- Responsive grid: on phones the centre card drops below the wheel; small-screen labels only show when they fit.

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Hover (or focus) any arc to light its lineage and update the centre card; on touch, tap. Click or tap a tea family or flavour group to zoom into that branch; use the Back button, Escape or Backspace to return. Click a note to pin it. Type in the search box (or press "/") to highlight matching notes, with a match count and an x to clear. Press Steep in the centre card to run the countdown ring for the selected tea (the x20 chip speeds it up for a demo); a chime plays at the end. Click the teapot for a brewing tip. prefers-reduced-motion: zoom and card transitions are instant, steam and teapot animations are off.
