==================== TITLE ====================
FORM / MOTION Showreel

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A thirty-second looping title sequence drawn entirely on one 1920x1080 logical canvas that is letterboxed and scaled to any viewport. Six scenes share one language of flat cream, ink, vermilion, cobalt and sun colour, heavy grotesque lettering, mono tape-label HUD chips and hard-cut rhythm: a dot becomes a ring that splits into bars that resolve into the word FORM; sixteen Bauhaus tiles drop, roll, pop and spin into a poster and kick on a 100 bpm beat; a single continuous line draws a face and then morphs through a flower into a bird over a misregistered colour disc; WHERE / IDEAS / TAKE / SHAPE punch in full-bleed with four different reveals; 48 rays burst and rotate while an odometer rolls to 100; and an end card with a rotating asterisk fades to black to loop. A hidden-when-idle control bar offers play/pause, a scene-marked scrubber, speed and sound toggles. Film grain and a vignette finish the frame.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- Custom timeline engine: one master time t (seconds) on requestAnimationFrame with delta-time, speed multiplier, loop wrap and scene table; each scene receives its local time and derives sub-progress with a seg(t,a,b) helper
- Hand-written easing library: cubic in/out/in-out, back in/out, elastic out, exponential in/out/in-out, bounce out
- Scene 1: annulus fill with even-odd holes, eight arcs that shrink into dashes, dashes converted to rotated rectangles that fly and rotate into letter columns before each letter overshoots into place
- Scene 2: 16 procedural tile shapes (circle, half, quarter, triangle, ring, bars, concentric arcs, diamond) with four entrance modes (bounce drop, roll, elastic pop, spin) and quarter-turn or colour-swap hard cuts on each beat, plus beat-synced pulse squares
- Scene 3: Catmull-Rom sampled one-line drawings (face, rose-curve flower with stem, bird) resampled to 520 points by arclength and morphed point by point, with pen-tip, line-boil jitter and wing flap
- Scene 4: text fitted to width by measurement; clip-mask wipe, per-letter masked slide, elastic scale punch and six sliding slices
- Scene 5: 48 wedge fills with beat pulse widths, digit-by-digit rolling odometer with clipped columns and cream iris exit
- Film grain from a runtime-generated noise tile animated with steps(), CSS vignette, tape-label HUD chips and a 24 fps timecode drawn in the canvas
- ResizeObserver-driven canvas sizing with devicePixelRatio cap; accessible custom slider scrubber with scene tick marks and jump buttons
- Optional WebAudio: square tick plus band-pass noise whoosh at each scene cut, soft ticks on beats (master gain 0.2)

==================== INTERACTION ====================
The reel autoplays and loops. Click or tap the stage (or press Space / K) to pause and resume. The bottom control bar (auto-hides after a few idle seconds, reappears on pointer, touch or key activity) holds play/pause, a timecode, a scrubber (click or drag anywhere to seek; arrow keys move one second; tick marks and the numbered scene buttons jump to scenes), a speed button cycling 1x, 2x, 0.5x, and a sound toggle (off by default; cues start only after the click). Keys 1-6 jump to scenes, S cycles speed, M toggles sound, left/right arrows seek two seconds. Reduced motion: the reel starts paused on the FORM title frame with grain animation disabled and can be played manually.
