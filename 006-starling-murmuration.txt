==================== TITLE ====================
Murmuration at Dusk

==================== DESCRIPTION ====================
A full-screen dusk over a reed-fringed lake, drawn on two canvases: a static one with the peach-to-rose-to-indigo sky, a low sun with a wide glow, three layers of hazy hills, a lake that echoes the sky with a glittering sun path, and a bank of short reeds; and a live one carrying drifting rose clouds, the flock, its mirrored and blurred reflection, swaying tall reeds with plumes, a startle ring and a peregrine falcon. The flock is a 3D boid simulation projected with perspective, drawn as short dark ticks whose alpha accumulates into smoke, with a smaller sub-flock that chases a second roost so bridges and streamers form between clouds. The title "Murmuration" is set in a light old-style serif with very wide tracking on the indigo sky, above a rotating field note in tracked capitals and italics, and a pill control chooses the flock size.

==================== TECHNIQUES ====================
- 3D boids (position and velocity in typed arrays) with separation, alignment and cohesion evaluated over at most 16 neighbours per bird, taken from a uniform spatial hash grid (counting-sort build each frame, random start cell to avoid bias).
- Roost attractor on a Lissajous path; each bird chases where the roost was up to ~3 s ago, which draws the flock into ribbons; a ~24% sub-flock follows a second, differently phased roost.
- Perspective projection with depth-dependent size and darkness; ticks oriented by velocity, length modulated by speed with a flap shimmer, stroked in 12 batched paths (two depth classes x six groups) so alpha builds density.
- Falcon: pointer-driven silhouette with wings that sweep back with speed, a fading trail and a live km/h caption; birds within its radius are repelled and pushed sideways so the flock splits and flows around it; the F key flies an automatic dive.
- Startle: click/tap/Space impulse with quadratic falloff, temporarily weakened cohesion/alignment (re-forming over ~1 s), expanding ring.
- Lake reflection: the flock is redrawn mirrored into a quarter-size canvas and drawn back in 26 rippling, fading strips, so no canvas read-back is needed.
- Static scene cached on its own canvas; reeds sway with layered sines; sun-path glitter; drifting cloud streaks.
- Field notes rotate with cross-fades and react to events; flock-size control (500/1500/3000, scaled down on small screens), counts capped by screen area, and a gentle frame-time governor (disabled under automation).
- DPR capped at 1.25, delta-time simulation with clamped steps, loop paused when the tab is hidden.

==================== INTERACTION ====================
Move the mouse (or drag a finger) and a peregrine falcon flies with the pointer; the flock parts around it and closes behind, and a caption reads its speed. Click, tap or press Space/Enter to startle the flock: it bursts outward and re-forms. Press F to send the falcon on a scripted dive, 1/2/3 (or the pill buttons, arrow keys work) to change the flock size. Field notes change every ~7 seconds and respond to the falcon and the startle. Reduced motion slows the simulation, stops flicker, cloud drift and the falcon trail, and starts the flock at 500 and caps it at 1,000.
